### REQ-0119: Directed Causal Precedence DAG Construction & Cycle Detection
- **Normative Statement**: The compiler shall construct a directed causal precedence graph $G_{\text{prec}} = (V, E)$ for all action executions and state transitions, asserting strict acyclicity to guarantee deadlock-free execution sequences.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  In $G_{\text{prec}}$, vertices represent action executions and directed edges $(u, v) \in E$ represent causal precedence ($u \prec v$). The solver verifies:
  $$
  \text{Acyclic}(G_{\text{prec}}) \iff \forall v \in V, \quad v \notin \text{Reach}(v, G_{\text{prec}})
  $$
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  A cycle in the causal precedence graph shall emit compiler error `E0252` (Causal Precedence Deadlock Cycle).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Assembles causal precedence DAG from action control flows and message exchanges.
  - **AC-2**: Employs Tarjan's strongly connected components algorithm to detect cyclic causal dependencies.
  - **AC-3**: Generates valid topological execution sequences for scheduling.
