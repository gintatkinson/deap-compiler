### REQ-0097: SysML v2 Quantities Library: `ISQ` & `ISQBase` Dimension Systems
- **Normative Statement**: The SysML v2 lowering engine shall provide built-in bindings for the normative SysML v2 `ISQ` and `ISQBase` libraries (`sysml.library/Domain Libraries/Quantities and Units/ISQBase.sysml`), defining the 7 base dimensions of the International System of Quantities: Length ($L$), Mass ($M$), Time ($T$), Electric Current ($I$), Thermodynamic Temperature ($\Theta$), Amount of Substance ($N$), and Luminous Intensity ($J$).
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Every quantity kind in the ISQ corresponds to a unique dimensional vector in $\mathbb{Z}^7$:
  $$
  \text{dim}(Q) = [M]^\alpha [L]^\beta [T]^\gamma [I]^\delta [\Theta]^\epsilon [N]^\zeta [J]^\eta, \quad \alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta,\epsilon,\zeta,\eta \in \mathbb{Z}
  $$
  Derived ISQ dimensions (Force, Energy, Power, Pressure) are defined via integer combinations of base dimensions.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  An unrecognized or undeclared physical dimension shall emit compiler error `E0262` (Unknown Physical Dimension).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Pre-loads and binds `ISQ.sysml` and `ISQBase.sysml` models.
  - **AC-2**: Associates base ISQ dimensions with canonical quantity kinds.
  - **AC-3**: Correctly computes derived quantity dimensions for mechanics, thermodynamics, and electromagnetism.
