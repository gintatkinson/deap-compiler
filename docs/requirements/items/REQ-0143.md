### REQ-0143: Run-Time Assurance (RTA) Simplex/Duplex Architecture Pattern Synthesis
- **Normative Statement**: The safety compiler shall synthesize Run-Time Assurance (RTA) simplex and duplex architectural patterns for safety-critical controllers, generating an advanced complex controller, a formally verified baseline recovery controller, a safety invariant monitor, and a switching decision logic block.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  The RTA architecture is formalized as:
  $$
  u(t) = \begin{cases}
  u_{\text{complex}}(t), & \text{if } \mathcal{M}(\mathbf{x}(t)) \equiv \text{Safe} \\
  u_{\text{recovery}}(t), & \text{if } \mathcal{M}(\mathbf{x}(t)) \equiv \text{Unsafe}
  \end{cases}
  $$
  Where $\mathcal{M}$ is the online safety monitor checking control barrier functions and state envelope constraints.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  An RTA architecture lacking a verified recovery controller shall emit compiler error `E0419` (Missing RTA Recovery Controller).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Generates structural SysML components for complex, recovery, and switching units.
  - **AC-2**: Binds safety invariant predicates to the switching decision monitor.
  - **AC-3**: Synthesizes verified statechart switching logic for runtime execution.
