### REQ-0090: SysML v2 Expose Filtering & Viewpoint Conformance Grammar
- **Normative Statement**: The SysML v2 lowering engine shall implement `expose` statements within views, generating filtered subgraphs of the model arena containing only explicitly exposed packages, classifiers, and relationships.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $\mathcal{A}$ be the complete model graph and $E_{\text{view}}$ be the expose filter expressions. The rendered view model $\mathcal{A}_{\text{view}}$ satisfies:
  $$
  \mathcal{A}_{\text{view}} = \{ n \in \mathcal{A} \mid \exists e \in E_{\text{view}} \text{ such that } \text{MatchesFilter}(n, e) \}
  $$
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Exposing an element that does not exist in the model shall emit warning `E0255` (Exposed Element Not Found).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Parses `expose Package_A::Classifier_B;` inside view definitions.
  - **AC-2**: Evaluates wildcard and recursive expose filters (`expose Package_A::*`).
  - **AC-3**: Emits filtered model subgraphs containing only exposed AST nodes.
