### REQ-0060: KerML Interaction & Step Abstract Syntax Semantics
- **Normative Statement**: The KerML lowering engine shall implement KerML `Interaction` and `Step` elements. Interactions shall model behavioral exchanges between classifiers over temporal occurrences, specifying causal sequences of message and item transfers.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  An `Interaction` is a behavioral classifier specializing `Behavior` and `Association`. Each constituent `Step` represents an action execution or feature transfer within the interaction lifecycle:
  $$
  \text{Interaction}(I) = \langle s_1, s_2, \dots, s_m \rangle, \quad s_i \in \text{Step}
  $$
  Steps are causally ordered via precedence relations: $s_i \prec s_j \implies \text{end}(s_i) \le \text{start}(s_j)$.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Cyclic causal dependencies between steps in an interaction shall emit `E0219` (Cyclic Interaction Step Precedence).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Ingests interaction definitions and associated interaction steps.
  - **AC-2**: Validates temporal precedence orderings between execution steps.
  - **AC-3**: Maps message transfers between participant lifelines to typed interactions.
