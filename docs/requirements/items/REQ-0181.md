### REQ-0181: Linear State-Space Matrix Evaluation & Higher-Order Numerical Solvers (RK4)
- **Normative Statement**: The simulation engine shall evaluate continuous and discrete linear state-space representations derived from structural and behavioral AST parameters, and synthesize 4th-Order Runge-Kutta (RK4) integration solvers for high-precision simulation of non-linear continuous-time dynamics.
- **Formal Invariant**: Continuous linear state-space evaluation satisfies:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dot{\mathbf{x}}(t) &= \mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}(t) + \mathbf{B}\mathbf{u}(t) \\
\mathbf{y}(t) &= \mathbf{C}\mathbf{x}(t) + \mathbf{D}\mathbf{u}(t)
\end{aligned}
$$
where $\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$, $\mathbf{B} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times m}$, $\mathbf{C} \in \mathbb{R}^{p \times n}$, and $\mathbf{D} \in \mathbb{R}^{p \times m}$.
For non-linear dynamics $\dot{\mathbf{x}} = f(t, \mathbf{x})$, the synthesized RK4 integration engine evaluates step updates with integration step $h > 0$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{k}_1 &= f(t_n, \mathbf{x}_n) \\
\mathbf{k}_2 &= f\left(t_n + \frac{h}{2}, \mathbf{x}_n + \frac{h}{2}\mathbf{k}_1\right) \\
\mathbf{k}_3 &= f\left(t_n + \frac{h}{2}, \mathbf{x}_n + \frac{h}{2}\mathbf{k}_2\right) \\
\mathbf{k}_4 &= f(t_n + h, \mathbf{x}_n + h\mathbf{k}_3) \\
\mathbf{x}_{n+1} &= \mathbf{x}_n + \frac{h}{6}(\mathbf{k}_1 + 2\mathbf{k}_2 + 2\mathbf{k}_3 + \mathbf{k}_4)
\end{aligned}
$$
exhibiting local truncation error $\mathcal{O}(h^5)$ and global accumulated truncation error $\mathcal{O}(h^4)$.
Complexity bound: State evaluation and RK4 step computation executes in deterministic $O(n^2)$ time per step.
