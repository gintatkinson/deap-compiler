### REQ-0162: Bottom-Up Feature-First Dependency Ordering & DAG Scheduling
- **Normative Statement**: The projection engine shall synthesize specifications using a bottom-up, feature-first dependency ordering. Leaf features and data types shall be generated and registered first, followed by composite assemblies, and concluding with top-level Epics.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $G = (V, E)$ be the dependency DAG of specification elements. The generation schedule $\mathcal{S}$ is the reverse topological order:
  $$
  (u, v) \in E (u \text{ depends on } v) \implies \text{Index}(v, \mathcal{S}) < \text{Index}(u, \mathcal{S})
  $$
  This ensures that when an Epic is generated, all child Feature identifiers and URLs are already finalized and resolved.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Cyclic dependencies preventing bottom-up ordering shall emit compiler error `E0210`.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Emits leaf data types and child features before parent assemblies.
  - **AC-2**: Resolves feature links in Epic tasklists without placeholder gaps.
  - **AC-3**: Bottom-up ordering is strictly deterministic across runs.
