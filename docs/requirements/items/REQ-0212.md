### REQ-0212: Positive AST Provenance Gate & Elimination of Negative Regex Exemption Tags
- **Normative Statement**: The compiler validation gate shall enforce 100% positive AST provenance across all generated specifications, rejecting any use of negative regex exemption tags or dummy assumption markers to bypass linter checks.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $\mathcal{T}_{\text{exempt}}$ be the set of deprecated exemption tag strings. The validation gate asserts:
  $$
  \forall D \in \text{Documents}, \quad \forall t \in \mathcal{T}_{\text{exempt}}, \quad t \notin \text{Content}(D)
  $$
  All claims must be supported by positive AST provenance; negative exemptions fail closed at the linter gate.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Encountering a deprecated exemption tag shall emit compiler error `E0120`.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Automated scanner confirms zero occurrences of deprecated exemption tags.
  - **AC-2**: Specifications pass linter gates purely through positive AST provenance.
  - **AC-3**: Attempts to insert dummy exemption tags fail closed at the validation gate.
