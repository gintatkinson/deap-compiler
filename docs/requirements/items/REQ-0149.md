### REQ-0149: High-Density Subsystem Interconnect Clustering & Dependency Partitioning
- **Normative Statement**: The ICD engine shall perform automated clustering and dependency partitioning (e.g. using Cuthill-McKee bandwidth reduction) over the $N^2$ matrix to minimize cross-subsystem interconnect complexity and group tightly coupled components into cohesive architectural modules.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $\pi$ be a permutation of subsystem indices $\{1 \dots N\}$. The optimization objective minimizes matrix bandwidth:
  $$
  \min_\pi \max_{(i, j): M_{\pi(i)\pi(j)} \ne \emptyset} |i - j|
  $$
  Clustering identifies cohesive subsystems with high internal coupling and minimal external interfaces.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  A fully dense interconnect graph indicating lack of architectural modularization shall emit informational warning `E0442` (High Interconnect Coupling Density).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Computes optimal subsystem ordering to group tightly coupled interfaces near the diagonal.
  - **AC-2**: Reports architectural coupling metrics and cluster cohesion scores.
  - **AC-3**: Identifies architectural decoupling opportunities in complex systems.
