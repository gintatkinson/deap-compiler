### REQ-0043: Circular Dependency & Metamodel Inheritance Cycle Detection
- **Normative Statement**: The AST engine shall implement a cycle detection algorithm (Tarjan's strongly connected components) that detects circular specialization hierarchies (`specializes`, `:>`) and direct containment loops. Any cycle in inheritance or structural composition shall be rejected at compile time.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $G_{\text{inherit}} = (V, E)$ be the directed inheritance graph where $(u, v) \in E \iff u \text{ specializes } v$. The compiler verifies:
  $$
  \text{IsAcyclic}(G_{\text{inherit}}) \iff \forall v \in V, \quad v \notin \text{Descendants}(v)
  $$
  Composition containment graph $G_{\text{comp}}$ is similarly checked for strict acyclicity.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Discovery of an inheritance cycle shall emit compiler error `E0210` (Cyclic Specialization Hierarchy); a composition loop shall emit `E0211` (Recursive Composition Loop).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Rejects circular inheritance chains (e.g. `A specializes B; B specializes A`) with clear cycle paths in diagnostics.
  - **AC-2**: Rejects self-specializing elements (`A specializes A`).
  - **AC-3**: Traversal completes in $O(|V| + |E|)$ time without infinite recursion or stack overflow.
