### REQ-0213: Port Direction Modifier & Forward Symbol Resolution Preservation in Complex Topologies
- **Normative Statement**: The compiler lowering engine shall preserve port direction modifiers (`in`, `out`, `inout`) and resolve forward symbol references across deeply nested and mutually recursive subsystem topologies without semantic degradation or ordering dependency.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  For an arbitrary multi-file subsystem topology with forward references:
  $$
  \forall p \in \text{Ports}, \quad \text{Direction}(\text{Lower}(p)) \equiv \text{Direction}(\text{Source}(p))
  $$
  Two-pass symbol hoisting guarantees that all forward references resolve to valid `NodeId` handles regardless of file declaration order.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Port direction loss or unresolved forward reference in valid topologies shall emit compiler error `E0208`.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Port directionality is preserved across multi-level structural nesting.
  - **AC-2**: Forward references between mutually dependent packages resolve cleanly.
  - **AC-3**: Complex multi-file models compile with zero ordering artifacts.
