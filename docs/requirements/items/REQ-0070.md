### REQ-0070: KerML Behaviors: Performance, Execution & ActionUsage Semantics
- **Normative Statement**: The KerML lowering engine shall implement KerML behavioral execution semantics, modeling `Performance` occurrences and `ActionUsage` steps. Behaviors shall execute over time, transforming system states and performing discrete action sequences.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  A `Performance` is an occurrence representing the execution of a `Behavior`. The execution lifecycle satisfies:
  $$
  \text{State}(P) \in \{ \text{Unstarted}, \text{Executing}, \text{Suspended}, \text{Completed}, \text{Terminated} \}
  $$
  Preconditions must hold at $t_{\text{start}}$; postconditions must hold at $t_{\text{end}}$.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Action execution with unsatisfied static preconditions shall emit compile-time warning `E0228` (Statically Unsatisfied Precondition).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Parses behavioral performance elements with explicit start and end lifecycles.
  - **AC-2**: Binds action steps to parent behavioral performance executions.
  - **AC-3**: Preserves action preconditions and postconditions for downstream formal verification.
