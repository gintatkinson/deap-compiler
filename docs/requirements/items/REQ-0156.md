### REQ-0156: Logical Channel Bandwidth Utilization & Capacity Budgeting
- **Normative Statement**: The ICD engine shall compute the aggregate bandwidth utilization for all shared communication buses and logical channels, verifying that aggregate traffic does not exceed the maximum allocated channel throughput capacity.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  For communication bus $B$ carrying signals $\sigma_1, \dots, \sigma_k$:
  $$
  \text{Bandwidth}(B) = \sum_{i=1}^k \left( \text{PayloadBits}(\sigma_i) + \text{HeaderOverhead}(\sigma_i) \right) \cdot f_{\text{rate}}(\sigma_i)
  $$
  Capacity bound: $\text{Bandwidth}(B) \le C_{\text{max}}(B) \cdot \rho_{\text{util\_limit}}$, where $\rho_{\text{util\_limit}} \le 0.70$ (70% maximum bus load).
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Exceeding the maximum channel bandwidth budget shall emit compiler error `E0447` (Communication Channel Bandwidth Overflow).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Computes data transmission rates for all signals across shared buses.
  - **AC-2**: Validates total bus load against configured maximum utilization limits (e.g. 70%).
  - **AC-3**: Emits bandwidth utilization breakdown charts in ICD documentation.
