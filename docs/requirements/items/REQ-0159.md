### REQ-0159: Protocol Framing Overhead Calculation & Header Serialization Penalty
- **Normative Statement**: The ICD engine shall calculate protocol framing overheads (headers, trailers, start/stop delimiters, parity, bit-stuffing penalties) for specific protocol profiles (e.g. Ethernet, Serial, CAN, SpaceWire) to compute true physical wire occupancy.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $L_{\text{payload}}$ be payload bytes and $P$ be the protocol profile:
  $$
  L_{\text{frame}} = L_{\text{header}}(P) + L_{\text{payload}} + L_{\text{checksum}}(P) + L_{\text{stuffing}}(P, L_{\text{payload}})
  $$
  Framing efficiency metric: $\eta = \frac{L_{\text{payload}}}{L_{\text{frame}}} \le 1.0$.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  A payload exceeding maximum transmission unit (MTU) without fragmentation support shall emit `E0450` (Payload Exceeds Protocol MTU).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Computes framing overheads for declared interface protocol profiles.
  - **AC-2**: Enforces MTU boundaries on single-frame transmissions.
  - **AC-3**: Accurately projects true physical bus utilization accounting for framing bytes.
