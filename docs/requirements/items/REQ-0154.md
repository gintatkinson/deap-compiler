### REQ-0154: SI Unit Verification in $\mathbb{Q}^7$ Rational Metric Space across Signal Flows
- **Normative Statement**: The ICD engine shall verify that the engineering units assigned to signals in the Master Signal Flow Dictionary match the dimensional vectors of connected source and destination ports in the $\mathbb{Q}^7$ rational metric space.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $\sigma$ be a signal carrying declared unit $u_\sigma$. The engine verifies:
  $$
  \text{dim}(u_\sigma) \equiv \text{dim}(P_{\text{src}}.\text{unit}) \equiv \text{dim}(P_{\text{tgt}}.\text{unit}) \in \mathbb{Q}^7
  $$
  Signal conversion shims are mandated whenever unit scaling differs (e.g. `psi` to `Pascal`).
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Unit dimensional discrepancy across connected signal interfaces shall emit `E0220` (Dimensional Incoherence Fault).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Validates signal units against port definitions using 7D rational metrology.
  - **AC-2**: Detects unit mismatches across subsystem boundaries at compile time.
  - **AC-3**: Generates conversion scaling factors in emitted interface adapter code.
