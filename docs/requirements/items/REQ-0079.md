### REQ-0079: SysML v2 ActionDefinition (`action def`) & ActionUsage (`action`) Lowering
- **Normative Statement**: The SysML v2 lowering engine shall lower `action def` and `action` usages into `ActionDefNode` and `ActionUsageNode` entities. Action definitions shall represent reusable behavioral processes, while action usages shall represent discrete steps within behavioral control flows.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  In SysML v2, `action def` specializes KerML `Behavior` and `OccurrenceDefinition`:
  $$
  \text{ActionDef} \le \text{Behavior} \le \text{OccurrenceDefinition}
  $$
  An action usage may contain an internal control flow sequence, fork/join parallel branches, decision points, and sub-actions.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Action declarations missing typed parameter signatures shall emit `E0112` (Untyped Action Parameter).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Parses `action def ExecuteControlStep { ... }` into an `ActionDefNode`.
  - **AC-2**: Lowers action usages with typed input and output parameters.
  - **AC-3**: Constructs internal action control flow graphs with predecessor and successor edges.
