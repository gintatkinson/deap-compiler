### REQ-0069: KerML PortionKind, TimeSlice, Snapshot & EventOccurrence Semantics
- **Normative Statement**: The KerML lowering engine shall implement KerML occurrence portioning semantics, including `PortionKind` (`TimeSlice`, `Snapshot`), and discrete `EventOccurrence`s.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  A `Snapshot` of occurrence $O$ at time $t_0$ is an instantaneous temporal portion:
  $$
  \text{Snapshot}(O, t_0) = O \cap \{ (x,y,z,t) \mid t = t_0 \}
  $$
  A `TimeSlice` of occurrence $O$ over $[t_1, t_2]$ is a continuous sub-interval:
  $$
  \text{TimeSlice}(O, t_1, t_2) = O \cap \{ (x,y,z,t) \mid t_1 \le t \le t_2 \}
  $$
  An `EventOccurrence` models an instantaneous occurrence signaling a state change or message arrival.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  A `Snapshot` declared with a non-zero time duration shall emit compiler error `E0227` (Non-Zero Duration in Snapshot).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Ingests and lowers `snapshot` and `timeslice` occurrence portions.
  - **AC-2**: Validates that snapshot elements have identical start and end time coordinates.
  - **AC-3**: Event occurrences correctly bind to transition triggers and message receptions.
