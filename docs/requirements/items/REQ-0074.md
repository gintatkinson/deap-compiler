### REQ-0074: SysML v2 ItemDefinition (`item def`) & ItemUsage (`item`) Lowering
- **Normative Statement**: The SysML v2 lowering engine shall lower `item def` and `item` usages into `ItemDefNode` and `ItemUsageNode` entities. Items shall represent physical matter, energy, or data payloads that flow through ports and connections.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  In SysML v2, `item def` specializes KerML `Structure`. Item instances have distinct identity or value semantics:
  $$
  \text{ItemDef} \le \text{Structure}
  $$
  Items model the payload data types transferred across item flows and connection bindings.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Attempting to instantiate an item usage without a valid item definition type shall emit `E0204`.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Parses `item def Payload_Data { ... }` into an `ItemDefNode`.
  - **AC-2**: Ingests item attributes representing physical payload properties (e.g. mass, volume, bit length).
  - **AC-3**: Binds item definitions cleanly to item flow declarations.
