### REQ-0142: Quantitative Risk Priority Number (RPN) Integer Scoring Engine ($RPN = S \times O \times D$)
- **Normative Statement**: The safety compiler shall compute quantitative Risk Priority Numbers (RPN) for all synthesized failure modes, calculating integer scores based on Severity ($S \in [1..10]$), Occurrence ($O \in [1..10]$), and Detection ($D \in [1..10]$).
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  For each failure mode $f$:
  $$
  RPN(f) = S(f) \times O(f) \times D(f), \quad RPN(f) \in [1 \dots 1000]
  $$
  High-criticality threshold: Any failure mode with $S(f) \ge 9$ or $RPN(f) \ge 200$ is flagged as safety-critical, mandating an associated safety invariant or Run-Time Assurance monitor.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Failure mode scores with components outside the $[1..10]$ integer range shall emit `E0418` (FMECA Score Out of Range).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Computes integer RPN scores for all declared and synthesized failure modes.
  - **AC-2**: Automatically flags safety-critical failure modes exceeding threshold limits.
  - **AC-3**: Verifies that every high-RPN failure mode possesses an allocated safety invariant.
