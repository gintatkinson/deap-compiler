### REQ-0148: $N^2$ Interface Symmetry & Directed Flow Antisymmetry Verification
- **Normative Statement**: The ICD engine shall verify topological consistency across the $N^2$ matrix, ensuring that physical connections exhibit topological symmetry while directed item flows exhibit appropriate anti-symmetry between transceivers.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  For physical bidirectional interconnects $C$:
  $$
  c \in M_{ij} \iff \text{Conjugate}(c) \in M_{ji}
  $$
  For directed information flows:
  $$
  f \in M_{ij} (\text{flow from } i \to j) \implies f \notin M_{ji}
  $$
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Unpaired physical connection endpoints or asymmetrical interface declarations shall emit `E0441` (Asymmetric Interface Interconnect Fault).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Verifies physical connector symmetry across matrix diagonal reflections.
  - **AC-2**: Verifies directed flow anti-symmetry for unilateral signal transmissions.
  - **AC-3**: Flags one-sided or orphaned interface connections.
