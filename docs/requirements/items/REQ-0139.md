### REQ-0139: Automated Boolean Safety Invariant Synthesis from Hazard Scenarios
- **Normative Statement**: The safety compiler shall synthesize formal Boolean safety invariants from identified unsafe control action scenarios. For each confirmed hazardous condition, the compiler shall synthesize the logical negation of the hazardous state as a formal invariant predicate.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $H(\mathbf{x}) \equiv \text{true}$ define a hazardous system condition over state variables $\mathbf{x}$. The synthesized safety invariant $\mathcal{I}_{\text{safe}}$ is:
  $$
  \mathcal{I}_{\text{safe}}(\mathbf{x}) \equiv \neg H(\mathbf{x})
  $$
  The compiler synthesizes this invariant into an `assert constraint` block attached to the responsible controller.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Synthesizing a trivial or self-contradictory invariant (e.g. $\mathcal{I} \equiv \text{false}$) shall emit `E0415` (Trivially False Safety Invariant).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Synthesizes Boolean invariant expressions from hazard state descriptions.
  - **AC-2**: Automatically injects synthesized invariants into the canonical AST model.
  - **AC-3**: Invariants evaluate to `true` across all verified safe operational envelopes.
