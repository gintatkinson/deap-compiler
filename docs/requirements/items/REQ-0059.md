### REQ-0059: KerML Association, AssociationStructure & Link Semantics
- **Normative Statement**: The KerML lowering engine shall implement KerML `Association` and `AssociationStructure` classifiers. Associations shall declare two or more typed participant ends, modeling multi-ary relational links between classifiers with multiplicity and navigability constraints.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  An `Association` $A$ between classifiers $C_1, \dots, C_k$ defines end features $e_1: C_1, \dots, e_k: C_k$. An instantiated `Link` $L$ of association $A$ is a tuple:
  $$
  L = (o_1, \dots, o_k) \in \text{Instances}(C_1) \times \dots \times \text{Instances}(C_k)
  $$
  Multiplicities on each end constrain the number of links associated with any single participant instance.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  An association with fewer than two participant ends shall emit compiler error `E0218` (Degenerate Association End Count).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Ingests and lowers binary and n-ary association definitions.
  - **AC-2**: Validates end feature multiplicity constraints against link instances.
  - **AC-3**: Emits valid KerML syntax for association structures and connector links.
