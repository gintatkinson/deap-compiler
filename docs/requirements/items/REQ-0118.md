### REQ-0118: Temporal Contradiction & Causal Inversion Diagnostic Detection
- **Normative Statement**: The temporal solver shall detect temporal contradictions and causal inversions, specifically identifying situations where an effect action is constrained to occur before its triggering cause, emitting precise diagnostics with source-mapped spans.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $A_{\text{cause}}$ trigger $A_{\text{effect}}$ across a causal link. The causal ordering invariant enforces:
  $$
  t_{\text{start}}(A_{\text{effect}}) \ge t_{\text{trigger}}(A_{\text{cause}})
  $$
  A causal inversion occurs whenever asserted timing constraints imply $t_{\text{start}}(A_{\text{effect}}) < t_{\text{trigger}}(A_{\text{cause}})$.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Detection of a causal inversion shall emit compiler error `E0251` (Causal Inversion Violation).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Detects backward-in-time causation constraints in action workflows.
  - **AC-2**: Pinpoints the exact causal chain and timing annotations causing the fault.
  - **AC-3**: Rejects models containing temporal deadlocks or causal loops.
