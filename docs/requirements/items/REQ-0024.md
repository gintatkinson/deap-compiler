### REQ-0024: Tabular Schema Ingestion for Formal Constraints & Operational Envelopes
- **Normative Statement**: The ingestion engine shall parse tabular schemas defining formal constraints, safety conditions, and operational envelopes into `IngestedConstraint` entities, capturing constraint expressions, formal invariant types (`assert`, `assume`), and severity classifications.
- **Formal Invariant**: Each ingested constraint $C$ is represented as a formal verification tuple:
$$
C = \langle \text{id}, \text{owner}, \text{expr}, \text{kind}, \text{severity} \rangle
$$
where constraint classifications satisfy:
$$
\text{kind} \in \{ \text{Assert}, \text{Assume} \}, \quad \text{severity} \in \{ \text{Critical}, \text{Degraded}, \text{Advisory} \}
$$
Verbatim Expression Preservation Invariant:
$$
\text{RawString}(\text{expr}) \equiv \text{SourceCellText}(\text{Column}_{\text{expr}})
$$
Constraint expressions are preserved verbatim for downstream Boolean AST parsing, SMT encoding, and invariant contract proving.
- **Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds**: Parsing constraint rows executes in $O(R)$ linear time with zero expression mutation.
- **Verification & Conformance Criteria**: Verification suite asserts 100% preservation of constraint syntax across tabular ingestion passes.
