### REQ-0068: KerML Occurrences: OccurrenceDefinition & OccurrenceUsage Semantics
- **Normative Statement**: The KerML lowering engine shall implement KerML `OccurrenceDefinition` and `OccurrenceUsage` elements. Occurrences shall model entities that have dynamic lifecycles and exist over four-dimensional spatio-temporal extents.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  An `Occurrence` $O$ possesses a temporal extent $[t_{\text{start}}, t_{\text{end}}] \subset \mathbb{R}$ and a spatial boundary envelope in space-time:
  $$
  \text{Extent}(O) = \{ (x, y, z, t) \in \mathbb{R}^4 \mid t_{\text{start}} \le t \le t_{\text{end}} \wedge (x,y,z) \in \text{Volume}(t) \}
  $$
  All physical systems, components, actions, and states derive from `OccurrenceDefinition` or `OccurrenceUsage`.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Temporal extent bounds where start time exceeds end time ($t_{\text{start}} > t_{\text{end}}$) shall emit `E0226` (Inverted Occurrence Temporal Extent).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Parses `occurrence def` and `occurrence` usage constructs into typed occurrence nodes.
  - **AC-2**: Associates temporal start and end bounds with occurrence instances.
  - **AC-3**: Establishes structural composition between parent and child occurrence lifecycles.
