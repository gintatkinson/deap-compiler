### REQ-0066: KerML Expressions: Expression, Literal, OperatorExpression & Invocations
- **Normative Statement**: The KerML lowering engine shall implement the normative KerML expression hierarchy, including `Literal` values (Integer, Real, Boolean, String), `OperatorExpression` (arithmetic, comparison, logical), and function/behavior `InvocationExpression`s.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  An `Expression` AST node is evaluated within a scope binding $\sigma$:
  $$
  \text{eval}(E, \sigma) \in \text{Value}
  $$
  Binary operator expressions $E_1 \odot E_2$ enforce type checking on operands:
  $$
  \text{type}(E_1) = T_1 \wedge \text{type}(E_2) = T_2 \implies \exists \text{op} \in \text{Operators}(\odot, T_1, T_2)
  $$
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Type errors in expression operands (e.g. adding a `Boolean` to a `Real`) shall emit `E0224` (Operator Type Mismatch).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Parses literal integers, reals, booleans, and strings cleanly into AST expression nodes.
  - **AC-2**: Evaluates operator precedence according to standard KerML grammar rules.
  - **AC-3**: Function invocation expressions type-check arguments against declared parameter signatures.
