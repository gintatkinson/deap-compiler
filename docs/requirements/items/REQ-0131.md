### REQ-0131: Downward Realization Completeness Gate ($\forall \text{Element}, \exists \text{Req}$)
- **Normative Statement**: The safety compiler shall enforce a downward realization completeness gate. Every structural part, port, state machine, and behavioral action must trace upward to at least one justifying requirement, eliminating gold-plating and unverified features.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $\mathcal{E}_{\text{concrete}}$ be all non-infrastructure model elements. The realization gate verifies:
  $$
  \forall e \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{concrete}}, \quad |\{ r \in \mathcal{R} \mid e \xrightarrow{\text{satisfies}} r \}| \ge 1
  $$
  Every element in the design must possess a direct, verifiable justification.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  A design element lacking an upward justifying requirement shall emit compiler error `E0408` (Unjustified Design Element Gate).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Inspects every structural part and action for upward requirement linkages.
  - **AC-2**: Flags ungrounded design elements as compliance failures.
  - **AC-3**: Generates justification matrices for systems engineering compliance audits.
