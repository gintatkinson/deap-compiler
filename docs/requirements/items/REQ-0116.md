### REQ-0116: Allen's 13 Qualitative Interval Relations Axiomatic Algebraic Solver
- **Normative Statement**: The dynamics solver shall implement an axiomatic constraint solver for Allen's 13 qualitative temporal interval relations (`before`, `after`, `meets`, `met-by`, `overlaps`, `overlapped-by`, `starts`, `started-by`, `during`, `contains`, `finishes`, `finished-by`, `equals`).
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $X = [x_s, x_e]$ and $Y = [y_s, y_e]$ be two temporal intervals. The 13 mutually exclusive relations are defined by endpoint ordering constraints:
  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  X \text{ before } Y &\iff x_e < y_s \\
  X \text{ meets } Y &\iff x_e = y_s \\
  X \text{ overlaps } Y &\iff x_s < y_s < x_e < y_e \\
  X \text{ starts } Y &\iff x_s = y_s \wedge x_e < y_e \\
  X \text{ during } Y &\iff y_s < x_s \wedge x_e < y_e \\
  X \text{ finishes } Y &\iff y_s < x_s \wedge x_e = y_e \\
  X \text{ equals } Y &\iff x_s = y_s \wedge x_e = y_e
  \end{aligned}
  $$
  Along with the 6 inverse relations (`after`, `met-by`, `overlapped-by`, `started-by`, `contains`, `finished-by`).
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  An unresolvable contradiction between asserted temporal relations shall emit `E0250` (Temporal Interval Relation Contradiction).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Ingests qualitative temporal assertions between behavioral actions.
  - **AC-2**: Validates mutual exclusivity of asserted Allen relations between interval pairs.
  - **AC-3**: Maps high-level timing constraints to endpoint inequality systems cleanly.
