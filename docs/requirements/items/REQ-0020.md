### REQ-0020: Table Row-to-Entity Structural Mapping & Identifier Sanitization
- **Normative Statement**: The ingestion engine shall map each valid table row to a corresponding intermediate representation entity (`IngestedPart`, `IngestedPort`, `IngestedAttr`, `IngestedConstraint`, or `IngestedAction`). The engine shall sanitize raw entity names into valid SysML v2 / KerML basic identifiers using deterministic replacement of invalid characters.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $S_{\text{raw}}$ be the raw string identifier from the table row. The sanitization function $\sigma_{\text{id}}$ satisfies:
  $$
  \sigma_{\text{id}}(S_{\text{raw}}) = \begin{cases}
  S_{\text{raw}}, & \text{if } S_{\text{raw}} \in \text{BasicName} \\
  \text{SanitizeIdentifier}(S_{\text{raw}}), & \text{otherwise}
  \end{cases}
  $$
  Where `SanitizeIdentifier` replaces non-alphanumeric characters with underscores (`_`) and prefixes leading numeric characters with `id_`:
  $$
  \sigma_{\text{id}}(\text{"123-Alpha (Beta)"}) = \text{"id_123_Alpha_Beta_"}
  $$
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Identifier collision resulting from sanitization (two distinct raw strings producing identical sanitized identifiers) shall emit `E0105` (Identifier Sanitization Collision).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Raw names with spaces, hyphens, and parentheses convert deterministically into valid SysML identifiers.
  - **AC-2**: Identifier collisions are detected and rejected with explicit diagnostic pointers.
  - **AC-3**: Original raw identifiers are preserved in metadata annotations (`@raw_name`) on the AST node.
