### REQ-0095: SysML v2 Quantities Library: `Quantities` & Measurement Scale Lowering
- **Normative Statement**: The metrology subsystem shall bootstrap the normative SysML v2 `Quantities` standard library package, formalizing physical quantities as numerical magnitude-unit pairs and distinguishing measurement scales into ratio scales and interval scales.
- **Formal Invariant**: A quantity $Q$ is formalized as a product of numerical value $v \in \mathbb{R}$ and measurement unit $u \in \text{Unit}$:
$$
Q = v \cdot u
$$
Measurement scale classification:
- `RatioScale`: Pertains to quantities with an absolute zero ($0 \cdot u = \text{zero}$), supporting scalar multiplication and division: $k \cdot (v \cdot u) = (k v) \cdot u$.
- `IntervalScale`: Pertains to quantities with an arbitrary reference origin, requiring affine transformations for unit conversion: $v' = m \cdot v + c$.
Dimensional homogeneity: Adding quantities $Q_1 + Q_2$ requires identical physical dimension: $\text{dim}(Q_1) \equiv \text{dim}(Q_2)$.
Complexity bound: Quantity construction and scale classification execute in $O(1)$ scalar operations.
