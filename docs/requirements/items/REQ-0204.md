### REQ-0204: Synthetic-Only Test Fixture Isolation & Clean Ground-Zero Baseline
- **Normative Statement**: All automated test suites, benchmarks, and regression suites across the compiler repository shall operate strictly on synthetic-only abstract test fixtures (`tests/fixtures/synthetic/`). All test suites shall execute exclusively against generic synthetic fixtures.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $\mathcal{T}_{\text{fixtures}}$ be the test fixture corpus. The synthetic purity predicate enforces:
  $$
  \forall f \in \mathcal{T}_{\text{fixtures}}, \quad \text{Tokens}(f) \subset \text{AbstractPlaceholders} \cup \text{SysMLKeywords}
  $$
  Synthetic fixtures employ generic tokens (`Package_0`, `Classifier_Alpha`, `Port_1`, `Flow_A`).
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Detection of proprietary or non-abstract tokens in test fixtures shall fail git pre-commit verification and emit compiler error `E0121` (Property-based parser fuzzing or generator crash reproduction).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: 100% of test fixtures employ pure abstract MBSE models.
  - **AC-2**: Automated audit confirms zero domain-specific token contamination in test directories.
  - **AC-3**: Synthetic fixtures thoroughly exercise all grammar edges and complex topologies.
