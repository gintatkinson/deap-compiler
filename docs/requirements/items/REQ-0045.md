### REQ-0045: Lossless AST Serialization & Deserialization (JSON & CBOR Formats)
- **Normative Statement**: The AST engine shall provide high-performance, lossless serialization and deserialization of `CompiledModelArena` to and from minified JSON and binary CBOR formats. Serialized representations shall preserve all node IDs, spans, attributes, constraints, and provenance metadata.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  For an arena $\mathcal{A}$, the serialization $\mathcal{S}$ and deserialization $\mathcal{D}$ round-trip satisfies:
  $$
  \mathcal{D}(\mathcal{S}(\mathcal{A})) \cong \mathcal{A}
  $$
  Deser performance: Deserializing a 100,000-node model from CBOR binary format shall execute in under 15 milliseconds.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Corrupted or version-incompatible serialized AST data shall emit `E0213` (AST Deserialization Failure).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Round-trip serialization to JSON preserves 100% of node properties and provenance spans.
  - **AC-2**: CBOR binary serialization achieves compact size (less than 25% of equivalent JSON).
  - **AC-3**: Deserialized AST graph produces identical compiler behavior to freshly parsed memory graph.
