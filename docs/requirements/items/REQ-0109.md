### REQ-0109: Generalized Kirchhoff Flow Law ($\sum \text{Through} = 0$) at Conservative Junctions
- **Normative Statement**: The network solver shall synthesize generalized Kirchhoff Flow Law equations at all conservative junction nodes. The algebraic sum of all through variables (flow rates) entering and exiting a conservative topological junction must sum to zero at every instant.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $J$ be a conservative junction connected to flow branches $k = 1, \dots, m$ with through variables $f_k$ and incidence orientation $\sigma_k \in \{ +1, -1 \}$:
  $$
  \sum_{k=1}^m \sigma_k \cdot f_k(t) = 0, \quad \forall t \ge 0
  $$
  This formulation enforces mass conservation, charge conservation, and volume continuity at junctions.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  An isolated conservative junction with only incoming or only outgoing flows shall emit `E0305` (Unbalanced Flow Junction Divergence).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Automatically generates flow conservation balance equations for all conservative network junctions.
  - **AC-2**: Validates flow balance during continuous-time simulation model generation.
  - **AC-3**: Rejects topological configurations that violate conservation laws at compile time.
