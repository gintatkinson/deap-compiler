### REQ-0167: Feature Layer 1: Domain State, Data Models & Primitive Structural Typing
- **Normative Statement**: Feature specifications shall document Layer 1 by exhaustively cataloging all owned structural attributes, primitive data types, value ranges, default values, physical engineering units, and structural containment relationships derived from the AST `PartDefNode`.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Layer 1 emission extracts attribute records:
  $$
  \text{AttrTable}(P) = \{ (a.\text{name}, a.\text{type}, a.\text{unit}, a.\text{bounds}, a.\text{default}) \mid a \in \text{attributes}(P) \}
  $$
  All types must use standard capitalized UML primitives (`String`, `Integer`, `Real`, `Boolean`) or referenced classifier names.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Untyped attributes or unresolvable type references in Layer 1 shall emit `E0204`.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Generates comprehensive attribute specification tables in Layer 1.
  - **AC-2**: Captures physical units, numerical bounds, and defaults for every attribute.
  - **AC-3**: Documents structural containment and sub-component composition.
