### REQ-0077: SysML v2 ItemFlow (`flow`) & Streaming Direction Lowering
- **Normative Statement**: The SysML v2 lowering engine shall lower `flow` usages into `ItemFlowUsageNode` entities. Item flows shall specify the directional transfer of item payloads (matter, energy, or data) between ports or actions across connectors.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  A `flow` usage defines source, target, and payload item type:
  $$
  \text{flow of } I \text{ from } p_1 \text{ to } p_2;
  $$
  Item flow consistency requires that $p_1$ possesses an output or inout feature compatible with $I$, and $p_2$ possesses an input or inout feature compatible with $I$.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Transferring an item flow into an incompatible port payload type shall emit `E0206` (Item Flow Payload Type Mismatch).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Parses `flow of Item_Alpha from port_1 to port_2;` into an AST item flow node.
  - **AC-2**: Validates payload type compatibility against connected port interfaces.
  - **AC-3**: Supports streaming flow rates and continuous item transfers.
