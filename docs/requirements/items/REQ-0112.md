### REQ-0112: Abstract Power Product Balance ($\sum P_{\text{in}} = \sum P_{\text{out}} + \frac{dE}{dt}$)
- **Normative Statement**: The flow network solver shall verify the overarching power product balance across closed physical component assemblies, ensuring that total input power equals total output power plus the rate of internal energy storage and dissipation.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  For component boundary $\Omega$ with external ports $k = 1, \dots, m$ and internal energy states $E_{\text{internal}}$:
  $$
  \sum_{k=1}^m P_k(t) = \frac{d}{dt} E_{\text{internal}}(t) + P_{\text{dissipated}}(t), \quad \text{where } P_{\text{dissipated}}(t) \ge 0
  $$
  Passive systems enforce the passivity condition: $\int_0^T \sum P_k(t) \, dt \ge -E_{\text{initial}}$.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  A closed passive component that generates spontaneous unbounded power shall emit warning `E0225` (Passivity Invariant Violation).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Synthesizes global energy and power balance equations across component boundaries.
  - **AC-2**: Verifies that passive components have non-negative dissipation rates.
  - **AC-3**: Incorporates internal energy storage dynamics into generated continuous-time state-space models.
