### REQ-0023: Tabular Schema Ingestion for Attributes, Data Types & Numerical Envelopes
- **Normative Statement**: The ingestion engine shall parse tabular schemas defining attributes, data types, default values, engineering units, and numerical bounds into `IngestedAttr` intermediate entities where numerical envelopes are extracted as rational or real intervals with explicit dimensional unit tags.
- **Formal Invariant**: Each ingested attribute $A$ is represented as a typed structural property tuple:
$$
A = \langle \text{id}, \text{owner}, \tau, v_{\text{default}}, [L, U], \text{unit} \rangle
$$
Envelope Consistency Invariant:
$$
L \le U \quad \wedge \quad (v_{\text{default}} \neq \bot \implies L \le v_{\text{default}} \le U)
$$
Unbounded boundary conditions:
$$
L \in \mathbb{R} \cup \{ -\infty \}, \quad U \in \mathbb{R} \cup \{ +\infty \}
$$
Unit dimensional representation: Engineering units map to rational exponent vectors over SI base dimensions $\mathbf{u} \in \mathbb{Q}^7$.
Malformed intervals where $L > U$ trigger an immediate diagnostic error $\text{DiagnosticCode::E0103}$.
- **Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds**: Attribute parsing executes in $O(R)$ time with respect to table rows, with $O(1)$ interval validation and unit tag hashing.
- **Verification & Conformance Criteria**: Verification suite asserts that numerical envelopes are extracted with full mathematical precision and boundary violations ($L > U$) fail closed.
