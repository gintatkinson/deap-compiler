### REQ-0152: Connection Binding Rosters & Physical End-to-End Pin Mapping
- **Normative Statement**: The ICD engine shall synthesize Connection Binding Rosters mapping every logical signal connection to concrete physical wiring pins, communication channels, bus addresses, or message identifiers.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  A physical binding entry maps logical connection $C$ to physical pin assignment:
  $$
  \text{Binding}(C) = \langle C, \text{ChannelID}, \text{PinSource}, \text{PinTarget}, \text{VoltageLevel}, \text{Impedance} \rangle
  $$
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Conflicting pin assignments (two distinct signals mapped to the same physical hardware pin) shall emit `E0445` (Physical Pin Assignment Collision).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Generates pin mapping tables for physical component interconnects.
  - **AC-2**: Validates that pin assignments are collision-free across all connectors.
  - **AC-3**: Emits physical wiring schedule documentation directly from AST bindings.
