### REQ-0064: KerML Conjugation Relationship (`~`) Lowering & Port Inversion
- **Normative Statement**: The KerML lowering engine shall implement KerML conjugation (`~`). Applying conjugation to a port or interface definition shall invert all directional features (`in` becomes `out`, `out` becomes `in`, `inout` remains `inout`), enabling symmetric, plug-and-play component interconnects.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $P$ be a port definition and $\widetilde{P} = {\sim}P$ be its conjugated form. The direction inversion operator $\tau_{\text{conj}}$ satisfies:
  $$
  \tau_{\text{conj}}(\text{dir}) = \begin{cases}
  \text{Out}, & \text{if } \text{dir} = \text{In} \\
  \text{In}, & \text{if } \text{dir} = \text{Out} \\
  \text{InOut}, & \text{if } \text{dir} = \text{InOut}
  \end{cases}
  $$
  Conjugation involution: ${\sim}({\sim}P) \equiv P$.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Conjugating non-port and non-interface classifiers shall emit compiler error `E0222` (Illegal Conjugation Target).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Applying `~` to a port definition generates inverted port feature directions in the type lattice.
  - **AC-2**: A port typed by $P$ connects cleanly to a port typed by ${\sim}P$ without explicit adapter shims.
  - **AC-3**: Double conjugation (${\sim}{\sim}P$) evaluates to an identical type signature to $P$.
