### REQ-0135: Hierarchical Control Structure (HCS) AST Extraction & Controller-Process Topology
- **Normative Statement**: The safety compiler shall extract a formal Hierarchical Control Structure (HCS) from the AST model graph. The HCS shall identify controllers, actuators, controlled processes, sensors, control actions, and feedback signals, formalizing control boundaries.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  An HCS is formalized as a directed control topology:
  $$
  \text{HCS} = (C, A, P, S, \mathcal{U}_{\text{control}}, \mathcal{Y}_{\text{feedback}})
  $$
  Where $C$ are controllers, $A$ are actuators, $P$ is the controlled plant process, $S$ are sensors, $\mathcal{U}_{\text{control}} \subseteq C \times A \times P$ are control actions, and $\mathcal{Y}_{\text{feedback}} \subseteq P \times S \times C$ are feedback signals.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  An open control loop lacking feedback paths from controlled process back to the controller shall emit `E0412` (Open Control Loop Lacking Feedback).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Automatically discovers control loops by analyzing port connections and item flows.
  - **AC-2**: Classifies components into controller, actuator, process, and sensor roles based on AST metadata.
  - **AC-3**: Emits structured HCS diagram models and topological rosters.
