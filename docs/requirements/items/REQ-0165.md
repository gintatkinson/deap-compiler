### REQ-0165: Epics Projection: High-Level Macro Statechart Diagrams & Operational Scenarios
- **Normative Statement**: Every generated Epic shall include a macro-level system state machine diagram (rendered in Mermaid `stateDiagram-v2`) illustrating subsystem operational lifecycles, macro operational modes, initialization, nominal execution, degraded operation, and shutdown sequences.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  The macro statechart is extracted from the subsystem's top-level `StateDefNode`:
  - Initial pseudo-state `[*] --> Initializing`
  - Nominal states with transitions annotated with triggers and actions in double quotes
  - Safe shutdown and terminal states `SafeShutdown --> [*]`
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Malformed statechart transitions or unclosed fences shall emit `E0504`.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Emits valid `stateDiagram-v2` diagrams in every Epic.
  - **AC-2**: Captures complete macro operational lifecycle from start to termination.
  - **AC-3**: All transition labels containing special characters are properly enclosed in double quotes.
