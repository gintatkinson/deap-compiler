### REQ-0157: Rate Monotonic Scheduling (RMS) Schedulability Bound Verification ($U \le n(2^{1/n}-1)$)
- **Normative Statement**: The ICD engine shall perform Rate Monotonic Scheduling (RMS) schedulability analysis for periodic signal transmission tasks, verifying that total CPU or bus utilization does not exceed the Liu & Layland schedulability bound.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  For $n$ periodic transmission tasks with computation times $C_i$ and periods $T_i$:
  $$
  U = \sum_{i=1}^n \frac{C_i}{T_i} \le n \left( 2^{1/n} - 1 \right)
  $$
  For large $n$, the asymptotic bound is $U \le \ln(2) \approx 0.693$.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Utilization exceeding the RMS schedulability bound shall emit compiler warning `E0448` (RMS Task Schedulability Bound Exceeded).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Evaluates RMS schedulability bound across periodic signal communication tasks.
  - **AC-2**: Automatically assigns rate-monotonic priorities (shorter period = higher priority).
  - **AC-3**: Emits schedulability margins into interface timing documentation.
