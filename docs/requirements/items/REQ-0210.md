### REQ-0210: Non-Destructive Reverse-Synchronization Entity Renaming & Anchor Preservation
- **Normative Statement**: The reverse synchronization engine shall perform entity renaming using immutable UUIDv5 identity anchors. Modifying an entity's prose title or Markdown header shall update the AST node name in-place without deleting the entity or severing its topological connection edges.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $F$ be an entity with identity anchor $U = \text{UUIDv5}$. If the markdown title changes from $N_{\text{old}}$ to $N_{\text{new}}$:
  $$
  \text{NodeId}(F_{\text{new}}) == \text{NodeId}(F_{\text{old}}) \quad \wedge \quad \text{Connections}(F_{\text{new}}) == \text{Connections}(F_{\text{old}})
  $$
  The model graph structure and relationship edges are completely preserved.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Accidental deletion of a renamed entity during reverse synchronization shall trigger immediate compiler failure and emit compiler error `E0122` (Order-independent column extraction or key binding failure).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Automated test renames feature titles in Markdown and executes reverse synchronization.
  - **AC-2**: Verifies that the existing AST node is updated in-place rather than deleted and recreated.
  - **AC-3**: Verifies that all connection edges and requirement links remain intact after rename.
