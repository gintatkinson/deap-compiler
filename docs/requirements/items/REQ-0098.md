### REQ-0098: SysML v2 Quantities Library: `SI` Base & Derived Unit Definitions
- **Normative Statement**: The SysML v2 lowering engine shall provide built-in bindings for the normative SysML v2 `SI` and `SIPrefixes` libraries (`sysml.library/Domain Libraries/Quantities and Units/SI.sysml`), defining the 7 base SI units (`m`, `kg`, `s`, `A`, `K`, `mol`, `cd`), decimal prefixes (`kilo`, `milli`, `micro`, etc.), and coherent derived units (`N`, `J`, `W`, `Pa`, `V`, `ohm`).
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Every SI unit $U$ is defined relative to the coherent base units:
  $$
  1 \text{ Unit} = c \cdot \text{kg}^\alpha \cdot \text{m}^\beta \cdot \text{s}^\gamma \cdot \text{A}^\delta \cdot \text{K}^\epsilon \cdot \text{mol}^\zeta \cdot \text{cd}^\eta, \quad c \in \mathbb{R}^+
  $$
  Where $c$ is the scaling factor (e.g. $c = 10^3$ for `kilometer`, $c = 10^{-3}$ for `millimeter`).
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Undeclared unit symbols or mismatched unit-to-dimension bindings shall emit `E0263` (Invalid SI Unit Declaration).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Pre-loads and binds the complete `SI.sysml` and `SIPrefixes.sysml` libraries.
  - **AC-2**: Resolves SI base and derived units with exact conversion factors $c$.
  - **AC-3**: Supports prefix composition (e.g. `kilo` + `meter` = `km`) with correct scale multiplier.
