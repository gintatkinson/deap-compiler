### REQ-0137: STPA Combinatorial Unsafe Control Action (UCA) Tensor Expansion ($U = A \times G$)
- **Normative Statement**: The safety compiler shall execute a combinatorial tensor expansion ($U = A \times G$) generating candidate Unsafe Control Actions (UCAs). The expansion shall pair every control action $a \in A$ with the 4 universal STPA guide words ($g \in G$): Not Providing, Providing, Providing Too Early / Too Late / Out of Sequence, and Stopped Too Soon / Applied Too Long.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $A = \{ a_1, \dots, a_m \}$ be the set of control actions and $G = \{ g_1, g_2, g_3, g_4 \}$ be the guide words. The candidate UCA tensor $U$ is:
  $$
  U = A \times G \times S_{\text{context}} = \{ (a, g, s) \mid a \in A, \, g \in G, \, s \in S_{\text{context}} \}
  $$
  Tensor cardinality: $|U| = 4 \cdot |A| \cdot |S_{\text{context}}|$.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Combinatorial explosion exceeding configured evaluation limits (default 100,000 candidates) shall emit `E0414` (UCA Combinatorial Expansion Bound Exceeded).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Automatically enumerates the complete Cartesian product of control actions and guide words.
  - **AC-2**: Evaluates guide words across all active system operational contexts.
  - **AC-3**: Populates the master STPA hazard analysis matrix with structured candidate rows.
