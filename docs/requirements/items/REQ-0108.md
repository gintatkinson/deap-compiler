### REQ-0108: Generalized Conjugate Variable Pair Modeling (Effort $\times$ Flow = Power)
- **Normative Statement**: The metrology engine shall model physical domain interfaces using generalized conjugate power variable pairs consisting of an Across variable ($e$, Effort/Potential) and a Through variable ($f$, Flow/Rate). The product of effort and flow must dimensionally evaluate to Power ($[M] [L]^2 [T]^{-3}$).
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  For any conservative physical domain $D$, the conjugate variables satisfy:
  $$
  \mathbf{d}_{e} + \mathbf{d}_{f} \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{Power}} = (1, 2, -3, 0, 0, 0, 0) \in \mathbb{Q}^7
  $$
  Canonical conjugate domain instances:
  - Electrical: Voltage ($e: M L^2 T^{-3} I^{-1}$) $\times$ Current ($f: I$) = Power ($M L^2 T^{-3}$)
  - Mechanical Translational: Force ($e: M L T^{-2}$) $\times$ Velocity ($f: L T^{-1}$) = Power ($M L^2 T^{-3}$)
  - Hydraulic/Pneumatic: Pressure ($e: M L^{-1} T^{-2}$) $\times$ Volume Flow ($f: L^3 T^{-1}$) = Power ($M L^2 T^{-3}$)
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  A conjugate pair declaration whose dimensional product does not equal Power shall emit `E0224` (Non-Power Conjugate Variable Product).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Validates that all physical port definitions declare compliant conjugate variable pairs.
  - **AC-2**: Computes instantaneous power throughput across physical interfaces as $P(t) = e(t) \cdot f(t)$.
  - **AC-3**: Enforces conjugate variable dimensional consistency across domain libraries.
