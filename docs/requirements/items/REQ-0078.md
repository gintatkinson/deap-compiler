### REQ-0078: SysML v2 AllocationDefinition (`allocation def`) & AllocationUsage Lowering
- **Normative Statement**: The SysML v2 lowering engine shall lower `allocation def` and `allocate` usages into `AllocationDefNode` and `AllocationUsageNode` entities. Allocations shall model cross-cutting mappings, such as allocating behavioral actions to structural parts or logical components to physical hardware.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  In SysML v2, `allocation def` specializes KerML `ConnectionDefinition`. An allocation usage establishes:
  $$
  \text{allocate } e_{\text{logical}} \text{ to } e_{\text{physical}};
  $$
  Allocation mappings are preserved in a bipartite allocation matrix within `AstModelGraph`.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Allocating between incompatible semantic categories (e.g. allocating a port to an expression) shall emit `E0303` (Invalid Allocation Mapping).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Parses `allocate action_step to part_processor;` into an allocation node.
  - **AC-2**: Ingests allocation definitions modeling custom allocation relationship kinds.
  - **AC-3**: Tracks bidirectional allocation mappings (allocated-from and allocated-to) on AST elements.
