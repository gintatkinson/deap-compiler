### REQ-0072: SysML v2 PartDefinition (`part def`) & PartUsage (`part`) Lowering
- **Normative Statement**: The SysML v2 lowering engine shall lower `part def` and `part` usages to `PartDefNode` and `PartUsageNode` entities. Part definitions shall represent structural component types, while part usages shall represent component instances within structural assemblies.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  In SysML v2, `part def` specializes KerML `Class` and `Structure`:
  $$
  \text{PartDef} \le \text{Structure} \le \text{Class}
  $$
  A `part` usage $u$ declared inside part definition $D$ represents composite containment: $D \text{ contains } u$. Multiplicity defaults to `[1..1]` when omitted.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Declaring a part usage typed by a non-existent part definition shall emit `E0204` (Untyped Feature Usage).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Parses `part def Classifier_Alpha { ... }` into a canonical `PartDefNode`.
  - **AC-2**: Parses `part sub_1 : Subsystem_Beta[1..*];` into a canonical `PartUsageNode`.
  - **AC-3**: Correctly builds structural composition trees across nested parts.
