### REQ-0129: 7-Tier Bipartite Traceability DAG Architecture
- **Normative Statement**: The safety compiler shall construct a 7-tier bipartite traceability DAG linking requirements, design elements, interfaces, constraints, hazards, safety invariants, and verification witnesses across the entire system specification.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  The 7 tiers are formally ordered:
  $$
  T_1 (\text{Mission Requirements}) \longrightarrow T_2 (\text{System Requirements}) \longrightarrow T_3 (\text{Architectural Classifiers}) \longrightarrow T_4 (\text{Ports \& Interfaces}) \longrightarrow T_5 (\text{Safety Invariants}) \longrightarrow T_6 (\text{Realization Code}) \longrightarrow T_7 (\text{Verification Witnesses})
  $$
  Traceability edges between tiers represent directed realization and verification allocations:
  $$
  E_{\text{trace}} \subseteq \bigcup_{i=1}^6 (T_i \times T_{i+1})
  $$
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  A severed link or illegal backwards edge violating the 7-tier ordering shall emit compiler error `E0406` (Traceability Tier Inversion Violation).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Builds 7-tier traceability DAG from SysML `satisfy`, `verify`, and allocation relations.
  - **AC-2**: Validates monotonic progression across tiers without backward cycles.
  - **AC-3**: Supports end-to-end forward and backward impact queries across all tiers.
