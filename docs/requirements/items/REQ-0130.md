### REQ-0130: Upward Allocation Completeness Gate ($\forall \text{Req}, \exists \text{Element}$)
- **Normative Statement**: The safety compiler shall enforce an upward allocation completeness gate. Every functional, performance, and safety requirement declared in the specification must trace upward to at least one parent system objective and downward to at least one architectural realization element.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $\mathcal{R}$ be the set of all requirements and $\mathcal{E}$ be the set of structural realization elements. The completeness gate verifies:
  $$
  \forall r \in \mathcal{R}, \quad |\{ e \in \mathcal{E} \mid e \xrightarrow{\text{satisfies}} r \}| \ge 1
  $$
  Unallocated requirements represent unfulfilled specifications and fail the compilation gate.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  An unallocated or unsatisfied requirement shall emit compiler error `E0407` (Unsatisfied Requirement Allocation Gate).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Traverses requirements tree and asserts 100% allocation coverage against design elements.
  - **AC-2**: Rejects builds containing unallocated orphan requirements at compile time.
  - **AC-3**: Emits structured allocation matrix tables documenting requirement-to-element bindings.
