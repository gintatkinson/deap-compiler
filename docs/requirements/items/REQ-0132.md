### REQ-0132: Verification Witness Completeness Gate ($\forall \text{Req}, \exists \text{Witness}$)
- **Normative Statement**: The safety compiler shall enforce a verification witness completeness gate. Every requirement must possess at least one associated verification witness (formal proof, test case, simulation scenario, or inspection procedure) verifying its fulfillment.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $\mathcal{W}$ be the set of verification witnesses. The witness completeness gate verifies:
  $$
  \forall r \in \mathcal{R}, \quad |\{ w \in \mathcal{W} \mid w \xrightarrow{\text{verifies}} r \}| \ge 1
  $$
  Requirements transition to verified status exclusively upon association with an active verification witness.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  A requirement lacking a verification witness shall emit compiler error `E0409` (Missing Verification Witness Gate).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Validates that all requirement nodes link to verification cases via `verify` relationships.
  - **AC-2**: Computes verification coverage percentage across the requirements catalog.
  - **AC-3**: Reports unverified requirements with explicit diagnostic warnings.
