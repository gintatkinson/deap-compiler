### REQ-0107: Conservative vs Non-Conservative Physical Flow Classification
- **Normative Statement**: The flow solver shall classify all physical port connections and item flows into either Conservative flows (governed by Kirchhoff conservation laws, e.g. mass, charge, fluid, heat) or Non-Conservative flows (signal, data, informational streams).
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  A flow connection $F$ is classified by predicate $\chi_{\text{cons}}$:
  $$
  \chi_{\text{cons}}(F) \in \{ \text{Conservative}, \text{NonConservative} \}
  $$
  Conservative flows participate in topological junction and loop constraint synthesis; non-conservative flows evaluate as unilateral directed functional dependencies.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Connecting a conservative port directly to a non-conservative signal port shall emit `E0304` (Conservative / Non-Conservative Port Type Mismatch).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Accurately distinguishes between physical conservative flows and informational signal flows.
  - **AC-2**: Automatically selects appropriate conservation equations for conservative junctions.
  - **AC-3**: Rejects illegal mixed connections between energy flows and raw information ports.
