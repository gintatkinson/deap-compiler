### REQ-0086: SysML v2 RequirementDefinition (`requirement def`) & RequirementUsage Lowering
- **Normative Statement**: The SysML v2 lowering engine shall lower `requirement def` and `requirement` usages into `RequirementDefNode` and `RequirementUsageNode` entities. Requirements shall specify formal text specifications, requirement IDs, and formal subject elements.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  In SysML v2, `requirement def` specializes `ConstraintDefinition`. A requirement $R$ contains:
  $$
  R = \langle \text{id}: \text{Spur}, \text{text}: \text{String}, \text{subject}: \text{Option}<\text{NodeId}>, \text{assumes}: \text{Vec}<\text{Expr}>, \text{requires}: \text{Vec}<\text{Expr}> \rangle
  $$
  Requirements model the contract that system realization elements must satisfy.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  A requirement lacking an identifier or textual statement shall emit `E0402` (Malformed Requirement Declaration).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Parses `requirement def Req_Alpha { doc /* text */ }` into a `RequirementDefNode`.
  - **AC-2**: Captures requirement IDs, docstrings, and formal requires/assumes clauses.
  - **AC-3**: Binds requirements into the 7-tier traceability DAG.
