### REQ-0099: Formal $\mathbb{Q}^7$ Rational Vector Space over SI Base Dimensions
- **Normative Statement**: The metrology engine shall model all physical dimensions as a formal 7-dimensional vector space over the field of rational numbers $\mathbb{Q}^7$. The basis vectors shall correspond strictly to the 7 SI base quantities: Mass ($M$), Length ($L$), Time ($T$), Electric Current ($I$), Thermodynamic Temperature ($\Theta$), Amount of Substance ($N$), and Luminous Intensity ($J$).
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  A physical dimension $\mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{Q}^7$ is represented as a rational 7-tuple:
  $$
  \mathbf{d} = (\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \epsilon, \zeta, \eta) \in \mathbb{Q}^7
  $$
  The zero vector $\mathbf{0} = (0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0)$ represents pure dimensionless quantities. Addition in $\mathbb{Q}^7$ models quantity multiplication; scalar multiplication by $k \in \mathbb{Q}$ models exponentiation to power $k$.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Attempting to declare a dimension outside the 7-dimensional rational basis shall emit `E0262` (Unknown Physical Dimension).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Vector addition in $\mathbb{Q}^7$ correctly computes composite dimensions.
  - **AC-2**: Vector negation correctly computes the dimension of reciprocal quantities ($1/x$).
  - **AC-3**: Rational vector coordinates support fractional exponents (e.g. square roots).
