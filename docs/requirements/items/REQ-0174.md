### REQ-0174: Downstream Projections: Bidirectional Realization Matrices, STPA Matrices & ICD Tables Synthesis
- **Normative Statement**: The downstream projection engine shall synthesize complete bidirectional realization matrices, structured STPA matrices (Losses, Hazards, UCAs, Safety Constraints, FMECA), and Level 1C ICD tables linking Epics to Features, Features to User Stories, and User Stories to Use Cases, ensuring 100% bidirectional traceability between specification artifacts and AST source models.
- **Formal Invariant**: The realization mapping is formalized as an incidence matrix:
$$
\mathbf{R} \in \{0, 1\}^{|\text{Specs}| \times |\text{ASTNodes}|}, \quad R_{ij} = 1 \iff \text{Spec}_i \text{ realizes } \text{Node}_j
$$
satisfying the full coverage invariant (zero orphan AST nodes):
$$
\forall j \in \{1, \dots, |\text{ASTNodes}|\}, \quad \sum_{i=1}^{|\text{Specs}|} R_{ij} \ge 1
$$
and STPA safety traceability mapping every safety constraint to at least one Hazard and one Loss:
$$
\forall \text{SC}_k, \quad \exists H \in \text{Hazards}, \, L \in \text{Losses} \quad \text{such that } \text{Mitigates}(\text{SC}_k, H) \wedge \text{LeadsTo}(H, L)
$$
Complexity bound: Matrix synthesis and coverage verification executes in deterministic polynomial time $O(|\text{Specs}| \cdot |\text{ASTNodes}|)$.
