### REQ-0073: SysML v2 PortDefinition (`port def`) & DirectedPort Usage Lowering
- **Normative Statement**: The SysML v2 lowering engine shall lower `port def` and `port` usages into `PortDefNode` and `PortUsageNode` entities. The engine shall support directed ports (`in`, `out`, `inout`) and conjugated ports (`~`).
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  In SysML v2, `port def` specializes KerML `Structure` with interaction capabilities. A `port` usage declares:
  $$
  \text{port } p : P [m_{\min} \dots m_{\max}];
  $$
  Directed ports declare feature directions that govern message and item flow exchanges.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Invalid port direction tokens or missing types shall emit `E0108` (Invalid Port Direction).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Parses `port def Port_Alpha { ... }` into a `PortDefNode`.
  - **AC-2**: Parses directed port usages: `in port p_in : Type_A;` and `out port p_out : Type_B;`.
  - **AC-3**: Correctly flags conjugated port usages (`port p : ~Port_Alpha;`).
