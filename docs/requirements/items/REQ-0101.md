### REQ-0101: Exact Rational Exponent Arithmetic without Floating-Point Loss
- **Normative Statement**: The metrology engine shall compute all dimensional algebra operations using exact rational arithmetic. The engine shall eliminate floating-point representations in dimensional calculations to prevent rounding drift, precision loss, and false-positive dimensional mismatches.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  For two rational exponents $\frac{a}{b}$ and $\frac{c}{d}$:
  $$
  \frac{a}{b} + \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a \cdot d + c \cdot b}{b \cdot d}, \quad \frac{a}{b} \cdot \frac{c}{d} = \frac{a \cdot c}{b \cdot d}
  $$
  Arithmetic overflow checking: Integer operations verify `checked_add` and `checked_mul` to prevent integer wrap on extreme exponents.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Arithmetic overflow during rational exponent evaluation shall emit `E0265` (Dimensional Exponent Arithmetic Overflow).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Fractional powers such as $\sqrt{L^2 T^{-2}} = L T^{-1}$ evaluate with exact rational integers $(1, 1)$ and $(-1, 1)$.
  - **AC-2**: Repeated multiplication and division cycles return precisely to initial dimensions with zero drift.
  - **AC-3**: Demonstrates verified zero floating-point rounding errors across 10,000 randomized algebraic cycles.
