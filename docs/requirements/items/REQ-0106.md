### REQ-0106: Composite Unit Graph Reduction & Base SI Normalization
- **Normative Statement**: The metrology engine shall provide an automated composite unit reduction engine (`units.rs`) that decomposes arbitrary user-defined derived units into their irreducible SI base unit products and rational scaling coefficients.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  The unit decomposition is represented as a directed acyclic reduction graph where derived units expand recursively:
  $$
  \text{Reduce}(\text{Watt}) = \text{Reduce}(\text{Joule} / \text{Second}) = \text{Reduce}((\text{Newton} \cdot \text{Meter}) / \text{Second}) = \text{kg} \cdot \text{m}^2 \cdot \text{s}^{-3}
  $$
  Scale coefficient $c$ is accumulated: $c_{\text{final}} = \prod c_i$.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  A cycle in unit derivation definitions shall emit compiler error `E0223` (Recursive Unit Derivation Cycle).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Reduces complex composite units (e.g. `Pascal`, `Tesla`, `Coulomb`) to canonical SI base units.
  - **AC-2**: Normalizes scaled units (e.g. `mW`, `kV`, `km/h`) to base SI with exact numeric scaling factors.
  - **AC-3**: Prevents unit dropping during schema transformation passes.
