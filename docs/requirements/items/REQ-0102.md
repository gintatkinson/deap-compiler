### REQ-0102: Static Dimensional Homogeneity Validation for Additive Expressions ($A \pm B$)
- **Normative Statement**: The type checker shall enforce static dimensional homogeneity on all additive and comparative expressions ($A + B$, $A - B$, $A == B$, $A < B$, etc.). The operands must possess identically equal dimensional vectors in $\mathbb{Q}^7$.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $A$ and $B$ be expression AST nodes with inferred dimensions $\mathbf{d}_A, \mathbf{d}_B \in \mathbb{Q}^7$. The homogeneity rule enforces:
  $$
  \text{expr} = A \pm B \implies \mathbf{d}_A \equiv \mathbf{d}_B
  $$
  The result dimension is identical to the operand dimension: $\mathbf{d}_{A \pm B} = \mathbf{d}_A$.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Attempting to add, subtract, or compare expressions with mismatched dimensions shall emit compiler error `E0220` (Dimensional Incoherence Fault).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Adding quantities of compatible units (e.g. `meter` + `kilometer`) validates cleanly after unit scaling.
  - **AC-2**: Rejects addition of incompatible quantities (e.g. `meter` + `second`) at compile time.
  - **AC-3**: Diagnostic error cites the conflicting dimension vectors and source token spans.
