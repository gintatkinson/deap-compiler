### REQ-0208: Composite Unit Rational Exponent Invariance in $\mathbb{Q}^7$ & Base SI Preservation
- **Normative Statement**: The physical metrology engine shall guarantee exact dimensional conservation during composite unit parsing and AST lowering. The engine shall preserve rational exponents in the 7D rational space $\mathbb{Q}^7$ without unit dropping, precision loss, or dimensional degradation.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  For an attribute $a$ carrying composite unit $u$, the dimensional projection $\mathbf{d}(u) \in \mathbb{Q}^7$ satisfies:
  $$
  \mathbf{d}(\text{Ingest}(u)) \equiv \mathbf{d}(\text{ASTLower}(u)) \equiv \mathbf{d}(\text{Emit}(u))
  $$
  Bijective unit mapping invariant: The set of dimensioned attributes in emitted models must be bijective with dimensioned attributes in the parsed source schemas.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Loss or truncation of unit exponents during AST transformations shall emit compiler error `E0220`.
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Automated test asserts zero dropped units across complex composite units (`kW`, `m/s^2`, `N*m`).
  - **AC-2**: Rational exponents retain exact irreducible fractions in $[M]^\alpha [L]^\beta [T]^\gamma$.
  - **AC-3**: Unit conversion scaling factors accumulate without floating-point drift.
