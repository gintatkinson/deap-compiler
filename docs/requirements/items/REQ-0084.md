### REQ-0084: SysML v2 ConstraintDefinition (`constraint def`) & ConstraintUsage Lowering
- **Normative Statement**: The SysML v2 lowering engine shall lower `constraint def` and `constraint` usages into `ConstraintDefNode` and `ConstraintUsageNode` entities. Constraints shall specify formal Boolean invariant expressions over system parameters.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  In SysML v2, `constraint def` specializes KerML `Function` with a Boolean return type:
  $$
  \text{ConstraintDef} \le \text{Function} \longrightarrow \text{Boolean}
  $$
  A constraint usage asserts that the predicate expression evaluates to `true` across all valid system states:
  $$
  \forall s \in \text{SystemStates}, \quad \text{Predicate}(s) \equiv \text{true}
  $$
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  A constraint expression evaluating to a non-Boolean type shall emit `E0224` (Non-Boolean Constraint Expression).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Parses `constraint def LimitCheck { in val : Real; val <= 100.0; }`.
  - **AC-2**: Binds constraints to owning part definitions and usages.
  - **AC-3**: Preserves constraint expressions for formal verification and SMT solver lowering.
