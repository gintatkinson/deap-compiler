### REQ-0029: OMG IDL AST Ingestion for Structs, Interfaces & Directional Parameters
- **Normative Statement**: The ingestion engine shall provide an OMG IDL parser (`readers/idl.rs`) that parses `.idl` interface specifications into intermediate schema IR. The parser shall lower IDL structs into data types, interfaces into component parts, and operations with directional parameters (`in`, `out`, `inout`) into typed action operations.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $\mathcal{I}_{\text{idl}}$ be an IDL abstract syntax tree. The mapping $\mathcal{T}_{\text{idl}}$ satisfies:
  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  \text{interface } I &\longmapsto \text{IngestedPart}(I, \text{is\_interface}: \text{true}) \\
  \text{op}(in\, T_1\, p_1, out\, T_2\, p_2) &\longmapsto \text{IngestedAction}(\text{op}, \text{inputs}: [(p_1, T_1)], \text{outputs}: [(p_2, T_2)])
  \end{aligned}
  $$
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Unsupported or malformed IDL constructs shall emit `E0114` (OMG IDL Ingestion Error).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Ingests IDL modules, interfaces, structs, and typedefs cleanly into intermediate IR.
  - **AC-2**: Correctly maps parameter directions (`in`, `out`, `inout`) to action parameter signatures.
  - **AC-3**: Resolves nested module scoping into fully qualified namespace paths.
