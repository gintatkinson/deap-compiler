### REQ-0144: Cubic Polynomial Spline Bumpless Transfer Function ($\lambda(\tau) = 3\tau^2 - 2\tau^3$)
- **Normative Statement**: The safety compiler shall synthesize cubic polynomial spline bumpless transfer functions for RTA switching logic. Transitioning between complex and recovery controllers shall smoothly interpolate actuator commands over switching duration $T_{\text{switch}}$, eliminating control transients and mechanical shocks.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $\tau = \frac{t - t_{\text{switch}}}{T_{\text{switch}}} \in [0, 1]$. The blended control output $u_{\text{blend}}(t)$ satisfies:
  $$
  u_{\text{blend}}(t) = (1 - \lambda(\tau)) \cdot u_{\text{complex}}(t) + \lambda(\tau) \cdot u_{\text{recovery}}(t)
  $$
  Where the Hermite blending spline $\lambda(\tau) = 3\tau^2 - 2\tau^3$ satisfies boundary conditions:
  $$
  \lambda(0) = 0, \quad \lambda(1) = 1, \quad \frac{d\lambda}{d\tau}(0) = 0, \quad \frac{d\lambda}{d\tau}(1) = 0
  $$
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Configuring a non-positive switching duration ($T_{\text{switch}} \le 0$) shall emit `E0420` (Invalid RTA Switching Duration).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Emits mathematical equations for $C^1$-continuous cubic bumpless transfer.
  - **AC-2**: Zero first-derivative velocity discontinuities at transition boundaries ($t = 0, t = T$).
  - **AC-3**: Generates continuous simulation solver and embedded C code for the transfer block.
