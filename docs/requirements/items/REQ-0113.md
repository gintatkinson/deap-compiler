### REQ-0113: Parametric Operational Envelopes & Closed Interval $[v_{\min}, v_{\max}]$ Validation
- **Normative Statement**: The metrology engine shall validate system parameters and operating signals against declared operational envelopes. Operational envelopes shall be specified as closed real intervals $[v_{\min}, v_{\max}] \subset \mathbb{R}$ accompanied by engineering units.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  Let $x$ be an attribute or signal with operational envelope $\mathcal{E}_x = [v_{\min}, v_{\max}]$ and operating point $v_{\text{nom}}$. The compiler verifies:
  $$
  v_{\min} \le v_{\text{nom}} \le v_{\max}
  $$
  At compile time, static constant values and initial states are checked for membership: $v_{\text{init}} \in \mathcal{E}_x$.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  An attribute default value or initial state outside its declared operational envelope shall emit `E0226` (Parametric Value Outside Operational Envelope).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Ingests operational envelopes specified in tabular schema attributes.
  - **AC-2**: Validates nominal and default attribute values against envelope bounds at compile time.
  - **AC-3**: Generates runtime bounds monitors that trigger alerts when signals escape operational envelopes.
