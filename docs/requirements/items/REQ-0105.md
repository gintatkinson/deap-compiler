### REQ-0105: Rational Power Evaluation for Fractional Exponents ($A^{p/q}$)
- **Normative Statement**: The metrology engine shall compute dimensions for power expressions ($A^k$) where $k = \frac{p}{q} \in \mathbb{Q}$ is a rational constant, multiplying the base dimensional vector by scalar $k$.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  For expression $A$ with dimension $\mathbf{d}_A$ raised to rational power $k = \frac{p}{q}$:
  $$
  \mathbf{d}_{A^k} = k \cdot \mathbf{d}_A = \left( k \alpha, k \beta, k \gamma, k \delta, k \epsilon, k \zeta, k \eta \right)
  $$
  Rational division requirement: If $k$ is non-integer, each coordinate $k \cdot x$ must evaluate to a valid reduced fraction.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Raising an expression to a non-constant dimensioned exponent shall emit `E0222` (Variable Dimensioned Exponent Violation).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Computes $\sqrt{A} = A^{1/2}$ for an area ($L^2$), resulting in length ($L$).
  - **AC-2**: Rejects variable expressions in the exponent position when exponent has non-zero physical dimensions.
  - **AC-3**: Preserves exact rational coordinates in result dimensions.
