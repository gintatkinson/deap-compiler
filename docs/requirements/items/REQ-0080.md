### REQ-0080: SysML v2 StateDefinition (`state def`), StateUsage (`state`) & ParallelState
- **Normative Statement**: The SysML v2 lowering engine shall lower `state def`, `state`, and `parallel state` usages into `StateDefNode` and `StateUsageNode` entities. States shall model discrete operational modes within hierarchical state machines, supporting composite states and orthogonal parallel regions.
- **Formal Semantics & Invariants**:
  In SysML v2, `state def` specializes KerML `Behavior`. A state $S$ has lifecycle duration:
  $$
  \text{Duration}(S) = [t_{\text{entry}}, t_{\text{exit}}]
  $$
  Parallel states contain two or more orthogonal regions $R_1, \dots, R_m$ that execute concurrently without mutual exclusion.
- **Error Diagnostics & Quality Boundaries**:
  Declaring a parallel state with fewer than two concurrent regions shall emit `E0251` (Degenerate Parallel State Region Count).
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - **AC-1**: Parses `state def OperationalModes { ... }` into a `StateDefNode`.
  - **AC-2**: Parses hierarchical nested states and composite statecharts cleanly.
  - **AC-3**: Correctly identifies and lowers `parallel state` blocks containing orthogonal regions.
