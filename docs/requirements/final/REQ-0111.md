### REQ-0111: Abstract Power Product Balance ($\sum P_{\text{in}} = \sum P_{\text{out}} + \frac{dE}{dt}$)

- **Normative Statement**: The physical metrology, abstract flow conservation engine, and network constraint synthesis pipeline of Subsystem 5 shall synthesize and verify the overarching power product balance across closed conservative network assemblies, subsystem boundaries, and lumped physical control volumes. The constraint synthesis engine shall mandate that the algebraic sum of all net input power entering an assembly boundary via external conservative interaction ports equals the total output power exiting the boundary plus the instantaneous time rate of change of internal stored energy and the non-negative rate of internal power dissipation.

  Specifically, the metrology engine, network solver, and constraint synthesis pipeline shall strictly enforce the following structural, algebraic, energetic, and diagnostic invariants:
  1. **Assembly Boundary, Ports & Conjugate Power Product Formulation**:
     - An abstract physical assembly or component control volume shall be formalized by a closed topological boundary $\Omega$ interacting with external networks exclusively through a finite set of $m$ external conservative ports $\mathcal{P}_{\text{ext}} = \{ P_1, \dots, P_m \}$.
     - Each conservative external port $P_k \in \mathcal{P}_{\text{ext}}$ shall declare or bind a generalized conjugate variable pair $(e_k(t), f_k(t))$ defined over the 7-dimensional International System of Quantities (ISQ) rational metric space $\mathbb{Q}^7$ in accordance with REQ-0107, comprising an Across variable $e_k(t)$ (effort / potential) and a Through variable $f_k(t)$ (flow / rate).
     - The instantaneous scalar power $P_k(t)$ transmitted across port $P_k$ shall be defined as the algebraic product of its conjugate Across and Through variables:
       $$P_k(t) = e_k(t) \cdot f_k(t)$$
     - Each external port shall possess a reference orientation coefficient $\sigma_k \in \{-1, +1\}$, where $\sigma_k = +1$ designates incoming power flow into boundary $\Omega$ (input power), and $\sigma_k = -1$ designates outgoing power flow exiting boundary $\Omega$ (output power).
     - The assembly external ports shall partition into incoming ports $\mathcal{P}_{\text{in}} = \{ P_k \in \mathcal{P}_{\text{ext}} \mid \sigma_k = +1 \}$ and outgoing ports $\mathcal{P}_{\text{out}} = \{ P_k \in \mathcal{P}_{\text{ext}} \mid \sigma_k = -1 \}$.
     - Total instantaneous input power entering boundary $\Omega$ shall be defined as $P_{\text{in}}(t) = \sum_{P_k \in \mathcal{P}_{\text{in}}} c_k \cdot P_k(t)$, and total instantaneous output power exiting boundary $\Omega$ shall be defined as $P_{\text{out}}(t) = \sum_{P_k \in \mathcal{P}_{\text{out}}} c_k \cdot (-P_k(t))$, where $c_k \in \mathbb{Q}^+$ is the exact rational unit conversion scale factor to coherent SI Watts.
     - Net instantaneous external power entering boundary $\Omega$ shall be defined as:
       $$P_{\text{ext}}(t) = \sum_{k=1}^m \sigma_k \cdot c_k \cdot P_k(t) = P_{\text{in}}(t) - P_{\text{out}}(t)$$
  2. **Internal Energy Storage & Accumulation Invariant**:
     - The total internal energy stored within boundary $\Omega$ at time $t$ shall be represented by state variable $E_{\text{internal}}(t) = \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{storage}}} E_s(t)$, where $\mathcal{S}_{\text{storage}}$ denotes the set of internal energy storage elements.
     - Storage elements shall encompass generalized capacitive / potential storage states (such that stored energy satisfies $E_{C, s} = \int e_s \, dq_s$, where $q_s$ is the accumulated through state with $\frac{dq_s}{dt} = f_s$) and generalized inductive / kinetic storage states (such that stored energy satisfies $E_{L, s} = \int f_s \, d\lambda_s$, where $\lambda_s$ is the accumulated across state with $\frac{d\lambda_s}{dt} = e_s$).
     - The instantaneous time rate of internal energy storage shall equal the exact time derivative $\frac{d}{dt} E_{\text{internal}}(t) = \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{storage}}} \frac{d E_s}{dt}(t)$.
     - Dimensional Homogeneity of Stored Energy: The internal stored energy $E_{\text{internal}}$ must possess the exact physical dimension vector of Energy in $\mathbb{Q}^7$:
       $$\mathbf{d}(E_{\text{internal}}) \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{Energy}} = (1, 2, -2, 0, 0, 0, 0) \in \mathbb{Q}^7$$
       corresponding to $[M]^1 [L]^2 [T]^{-2}$ (Joules), ensuring that its time derivative identically evaluates to the canonical Power dimension:
       $$\mathbf{d}\left( \frac{d E_{\text{internal}}}{dt} \right) \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{Energy}} - \mathbf{e}_T = (1, 2, -3, 0, 0, 0, 0) \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{Power}} \in \mathbb{Q}^7$$
     - Any storage state declaration violating dimensional consistency shall be deterministically rejected at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`DimensionalMismatch`).
  3. **Power Dissipation & Second Law Non-Negativity Invariant**:
     - Internal dissipative elements $\mathcal{R}_{\text{dissip}}$ (generalized resistors, viscous dampers, fluid friction channels, conductances, thermal dissipation paths) shall dissipate power at the instantaneous rate $P_{\text{dissipated}}(t) = \sum_{r \in \mathcal{R}_{\text{dissip}}} c_r \cdot e_r(t) \cdot f_r(t)$.
     - In strict compliance with the Second Law of Thermodynamics and physical realizability, the instantaneous power dissipation across all dissipative elements within boundary $\Omega$ shall be non-negative at all instants of time:
       $$P_{\text{dissipated}}(t) \ge 0, \quad \forall t \ge 0$$
     - For ideal lossless conservative assemblies ($\mathcal{R}_{\text{dissip}} = \emptyset$), the dissipation term vanishes identically: $P_{\text{dissipated}}(t) \equiv 0$.
     - Any conservative assembly, passive component, or constitutive parameter definition that exhibits strictly negative instantaneous dissipation ($P_{\text{dissipated}}(t) \lt 0$) without an explicitly declared internal active energy source shall be deterministically rejected at compile time or static analysis with diagnostic error code `E0301` (`KirchhoffConservationViolation`).
  4. **Overarching Abstract Power Product Balance Equation**:
     - The constraint synthesis engine shall synthesize the overarching First Law power conservation equation balancing net external port power against the rate of internal energy storage and dissipation:
       $$P_{\text{in}}(t) = P_{\text{out}}(t) + \frac{d E_{\text{internal}}}{dt}(t) + P_{\text{dissipated}}(t), \quad \forall t \ge 0$$
       or equivalently in signed external port summation notation:
       $$\sum_{k=1}^m \sigma_k \cdot c_k \cdot P_k(t) = \frac{d}{dt} E_{\text{internal}}(t) + P_{\text{dissipated}}(t), \quad \forall t \ge 0$$
     - In steady-state conditions where internal stored energy is constant ($\frac{d E_{\text{internal}}}{dt} \equiv 0$), the power product balance reduces to:
       $$P_{\text{in}} = P_{\text{out}} + P_{\text{dissipated}}$$
     - For ideal conservative, lossless steady-state assemblies ($\frac{d E_{\text{internal}}}{dt} \equiv 0$ and $P_{\text{dissipated}} \equiv 0$), the power product balance enforces strict input-output equality:
       $$P_{\text{in}} = P_{\text{out}} \iff \sum_{k=1}^m \sigma_k \cdot c_k \cdot P_k = 0$$
  5. **Passivity Contract & Integral Energy Invariant**:
     - For any physical assembly or component model annotated with the `passive` contract modifier, the compiler shall enforce the passivity condition across all time horizons $T \ge 0$:
       $$\int_0^T P_{\text{ext}}(t) \, dt = \int_0^T \sum_{k=1}^m \sigma_k \cdot c_k \cdot P_k(t) \, dt \ge -E_{\text{initial}}$$
       where $E_{\text{initial}} = E_{\text{internal}}(0) \ge 0$ represents the initial stored energy at $t = 0$.
     - The passivity contract establishes that a passive system cannot deliver more net energy to its external environment than was originally stored within it:
       $$E_{\text{extracted}}(T) = -\int_0^T P_{\text{ext}}(t) \, dt \le E_{\text{initial}}, \quad \forall T \ge 0$$
     - Any assembly declared with the `passive` contract modifier whose constitutive parameters or dynamic equations permit unbounded net energy extraction ($E_{\text{extracted}}(T) \gt E_{\text{initial}}$) or negative storage states ($E_{\text{internal}}(t) \lt 0$) shall be deterministically rejected with diagnostic error code `E0301` (`KirchhoffConservationViolation`).
  6. **Tellegen's Theorem Invariance across Internal Network Interconnects**:
     - For any internal topological network multigraph $\mathcal{G}_{\text{internal}} = (\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}, \mathcal{E}_{\text{internal}})$ within boundary $\Omega$ whose branches satisfy Kirchhoff Flow Law (REQ-0108) and Kirchhoff Potential Law (REQ-0109), the sum of powers over all internal branches identically equals the net power crossing external boundary ports:
       $$\sum_{b \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{internal}}} e_b(t) \cdot f_b(t) = \sum_{k=1}^m \sigma_k \cdot c_k \cdot P_k(t) = P_{\text{ext}}(t)$$
     - Ideal internal conservative junctions $\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc, ideal}}$ shall satisfy zero net power absorption:
       $$\sum_{J \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc, ideal}}} \left( e_J(t) \cdot \sum_{b \in \text{Incident}(J)} A_{J, b} \cdot f_b(t) \right) = 0$$
     - The compiler shall verify that topological interconnects and ideal junctions introduce zero spurious power generation or leakage into the global network energy ledger.
  7. **Dimensional Homogeneity & Exact Rational Unit Scaling**:
     - All terms appearing in the power product balance equation ($P_{\text{in}}$, $P_{\text{out}}$, $P_k$, $\frac{d E_{\text{internal}}}{dt}$, $P_{\text{dissipated}}$) must evaluate to the identical canonical Power dimension vector $\mathbf{d}_{\text{Power}} = (1, 2, -3, 0, 0, 0, 0) \in \mathbb{Q}^7$.
     - When port quantities or power parameters are declared using non-coherent units (e.g., kilowatts, milliwatts, metric horsepower, BTU per hour), the compiler shall scale each term by its exact rational multiplier $c_k \in \mathbb{Q}^+$ relative to the coherent SI base unit (Watt).
     - Any dimensional mismatch detected among power terms shall be deterministically rejected at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`DimensionalMismatch`).
  8. **Static Verification & Dynamic Constraint AST Synthesis**:
     - For static network assemblies with constant declared boundary flows and potentials, the compiler shall evaluate numerical power balance at compile time. If static net input power does not equal static output power plus static dissipation within machine epsilon ($|P_{\text{in}} - P_{\text{out}} - P_{\text{dissipated}}| \gt 0$), the compiler shall emit compile-time diagnostic error code `E0301` (`KirchhoffConservationViolation`).
     - For dynamic and parametric models, the compiler shall synthesize formal equality constraint AST nodes representing the power product balance and passivity conditions.
     - Synthesized constraint expressions shall be lowered into intermediate representations: LUMI IR SSA basic blocks, SMT-LIB2 verification assertions per REQ-0188 / REQ-0197, and continuous simulation equation blocks per REQ-0179.
  9. **Architectural Directives Compliance**:
     - *Directive 1 (Deterministic UUIDv5 Anchors)*: Every lowered assembly boundary descriptor, external port power handle, internal storage state node, dissipation term handle, and synthesized power balance constraint AST node shall be assigned an immutable deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 identifier computed via $\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(n))$ in accordance with REQ-0010.
     - *Directive 2 (Bounded Work-Stealing Parallelism)*: Parallel power balance verification, dimensional validation, and constraint synthesis across independent packages, assemblies, and subgraphs shall execute on a bounded work-stealing thread pool with worker bound $N_{\text{max\_workers}}$ in accordance with REQ-0012.
     - *Directive 3 (String Interner Memory Allocation Exception)*: Assembly names, port identifiers, variable names, and unit symbol strings shall be interned into the deduplicated global string interner pool during lexical ingestion per REQ-0036 as the sole permitted exception to zero-copy memory management.
     - *Directive 4 (Synchronization Token Error Recovery)*: The metrology engine shall implement synchronization token error recovery on statement delimiters (`;`) and block boundaries (`}`). When semantic violations occur (`E0300`, `E0301`), diagnostics shall accumulate in the error sink buffer $\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}$, substitute typed error sentinels ($\bot_{\text{power}}$, $\bot_{\text{dim}}$), and continue auditing subsequent assemblies. If accumulated diagnostics reach $C_{\text{max\_errors}}$, terminal diagnostic `E0199` shall be emitted and compilation halted gracefully in accordance with REQ-0013 and REQ-0016.
     - *Directive 5 (Panic-Free Release Isolation)*: The power product balance verification pipeline shall enforce a deterministic, non-panicking execution contract with total-function error handling. Performance latency ($\lt 25\text{ ms}$) and peak memory RSS ($\lt 100\text{ MB}$ for $10^5$ network elements) shall be evaluated exclusively in compiled Release mode (`--release`) under dedicated benchmark harnesses isolated from CI logic tests in accordance with REQ-0011 and REQ-0196.

  All diagnostic messages, test models, and illustrative examples shall strictly employ synthetic abstract MBSE placeholders (`Package_0`, `Package_1`, `Classifier_Alpha`, `Assembly_Alpha`, `Assembly_Beta`, `Port_1`, `Port_2`, `Port_3`, `FlowBranch_1`, `FlowBranch_2`, `param_x : Real`, `Quantity_1`, `Network_Alpha`), strictly prohibiting hardcoded domain concepts, mock data, and non-ASCII dashes.
