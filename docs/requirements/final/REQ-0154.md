## 1. Normative Statement

The ICD engine of Subsystem 8 SHALL verify channel bandwidth budgets, asserting aggregate traffic remains within headroom limits.

The verification pipeline MUST enforce:
1. **Channel Capacities**: Channel $c \in \mathcal{C}$ (`LogicalChannel_Alpha`) declares throughput $C_{\max}(c) \gt 0$ and safety factor $\rho_{\text{limit}}(c) \in (0, 1]$ (default $0.70$). Invalid parameters emit `E0209`. Unresolved endpoints (`Port_1`, `Port_2`) emit `E0200`.
2. **Traffic Flows**: Multiplexed flows $F(c)$ (`Flow_1`) partition into periodic ($f_\sigma \gt 0$) and aperiodic ($T_{\min}(\sigma) \gt 0$, rate $r(\sigma) = 1 / T_{\min}(\sigma)$). Wire footprint is $L_\sigma + H_\sigma$ (payload $L_\sigma \gt 0$, framing $H_\sigma \ge 0$). Non-positive values emit `E0209`.
3. **Bandwidth Sum**: Evaluates $\text{Bandwidth}(c) = \sum_{\sigma \in F(c)} (L_\sigma + H_\sigma) \cdot r(\sigma)$. Idle channels ($F(c) = \emptyset$) evaluate to $0$.
4. **Budget Gate**: Asserts $U(c) = \text{Bandwidth}(c) / C_{\max}(c) \le \rho_{\text{limit}}(c)$ against `BandwidthBudget_0`. Overflow emits `E0209` and binds $\bot_{\text{bw}}$.
5. **Directives**: UUIDv5 anchors (Directive 1); work-stealing ceiling $N_{\text{max\_workers}}$ (Directive 2); symbol interning (Directive 3); delimiter recovery halts at $C_{\text{max\_errors}}$ with `E0199` (Directive 4); Release budget ($< 25\text{ ms}$, $< 100\text{ MB}$) (Directive 5).

## 2. Formal Invariant

Let $\mathcal{C}$ denote logical channels, $\mathcal{P}$ boundary ports, and $F(c)$ multiplexed flows. The gate satisfies:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\forall c\in\mathcal{C},&\quad C_{\max}(c)\in\mathbb{R}^+\land\rho_{\text{limit}}(c)\in(0,1] \\
r(\sigma)&=\begin{cases}f_\sigma,&\sigma\in F_{\text{periodic}}(c) \\ 1/T_{\min}(\sigma),&\sigma\in F_{\text{aperiodic}}(c)\end{cases} \\
\text{Bandwidth}(c)&=\sum_{\sigma\in F(c)}(L_\sigma+H_\sigma)\cdot r(\sigma),\quad U(c)=\text{Bandwidth}(c)/C_{\max}(c) \\
\forall c\in\mathcal{C},&\quad\text{Bandwidth}(c)\le C_{\max}(c)\cdot\rho_{\text{limit}}(c)\iff U(c)\le\rho_{\text{limit}}(c) \\
(\text{Bandwidth}(c)\gt C_{\max}(c)\cdot\rho_{\text{limit}}(c)\lor C_{\max}(c)\le 0\lor\rho_{\text{limit}}(c)\notin(0,1])&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(c,\text{E0209})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land c\gets\bot_{\text{bw}} \\
\exists\sigma\in F(c):(L_\sigma\le 0\lor r(\sigma)\le 0)&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\sigma,\text{E0209})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
(\text{Src}(c)\notin\mathcal{P}\lor\text{Dst}(c)\notin\mathcal{P})&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(c,\text{E0200})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}},&\quad\Delta_c=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(c))
\end{aligned}
```

| Symbol / Term | Domain / Formal Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| $\mathcal{C}, F(c)$ | Channels and multiplexed flows |
| $C_{\max}(c), \rho_{\text{limit}}(c)$ | Channel throughput capacity and safety factor |
| $L_\sigma, H_\sigma, r(\sigma)$ | Payload bits, framing bits, and rate |
| $\text{Bandwidth}(c), U(c)$ | Cumulative bandwidth and utilization ratio |
| $\bot_{\text{bw}}, \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}, C_{\text{max\_errors}}$ | Sentinel, diagnostic sink, and error limit |
| $\Delta_c, \text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}$ | UUIDv5 anchor and namespace OID |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(|\mathcal{C}| + |F|)$ capacity accumulation ($|\mathcal{C}|$ channels, $|F| = \sum |F(c)|$ flows). Work-stealing: $O((|\mathcal{C}| + |F|)/N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})$. Space: $O(|\mathcal{C}| + |F|)$.
- **Tractable Subclass & Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Evaluates allocated channels without NP-2 bin packing (REQ-0147) or NP-8 solving (REQ-0197).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Nominal Aggregate Bandwidth Within Budget
- **Given:** Channel `LogicalChannel_Alpha` ($C_{\max} = 10^7\text{ bps}$, $\rho_{\text{limit}} = 0.70$) with flows totaling $4.5 \times 10^6\text{ bps}$.
- **When:** Lowering model and validating budget `BandwidthBudget_0`.
- **Then:** Evaluates $U = 0.45 \le 0.70$ and passes.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Boundary Saturation at Headroom Limit
- **Given:** Channel `LogicalChannel_Alpha` ($C_{\max} = 10^6\text{ bps}$, $\rho_{\text{limit}} = 0.70$) with traffic $7 \times 10^5\text{ bps}$.
- **When:** Computing utilization at headroom boundary.
- **Then:** Confirms $U = 0.70 \le 0.70$ and passes.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Bandwidth Capacity Overflow Rejection
- **Given:** Channel `LogicalChannel_Alpha` ($C_{\max} = 10^6\text{ bps}$, $\rho_{\text{limit}} = 0.70$) with flows totaling $700{,}001\text{ bps}$.
- **When:** Lowering model through budgeting verifier.
- **Then:** Emits `E0209` and binds $\bot_{\text{bw}}$.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0209` (ChannelCapacityOverflow).

### AC-04: Unresolved Endpoint & Error Recovery
- **Given:** Channels with unmapped `Port_1` and $C_{\max} \le 0$, separated by `;`.
- **When:** Executing token recovery on invalid channels.
- **Then:** Emits `E0200` and `E0209`, recovers, and halts at $C_{\text{max\_errors}}$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0200`, `E0209`, `E0199`.

### AC-05: Bitwise Determinism & Performance Envelope
- **Given:** Benchmark with $|\mathcal{C}| = 10^3$ channels and $|F| = 10^4$ flows across $N_{\text{max\_workers}}$ threads.
- **When:** Verification in Release mode (`--release`).
- **Then:** Matches UUIDv5 anchors bitwise; latency $< 25\text{ ms}$, RSS $< 100\text{ MB}$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.
