### REQ-0106: Conservative vs Non-Conservative Flow Network Classification

- **Normative Statement**: The flow solver, network classifier, and semantic metrology engine of Subsystem 5 shall deterministically classify every port connection, interface binding, and item flow in the abstract syntax tree (AST) into either a Conservative flow (governed by generalized Kirchhoff conservation laws over Across and Through conjugate variable pairs in the 7-dimensional rational physical metric space $`\mathbb{Q}^7`$) or a Non-Conservative flow (unidirectional causal signal, discrete event message, continuous informational data stream, or software telemetry channel).

  Specifically, the metrology engine, flow classifier, and constraint routing pipeline shall strictly enforce the following structural, algebraic, and topological invariants:
  1. **Seven-Dimensional Physical Metric Space ($`\mathbb{Q}^7`$) & Conjugate Power Pairs**:
     - In accordance with REQ-0098 and REQ-0099, physical dimensions shall be formalized as exact rational coordinate vectors $`\mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$ over the seven International System of Quantities (ISQ) base dimensions: Mass ($`[M]`$), Length ($`[L]`$), Time ($`[T]`$), Electric Current / Base Flow ($`[I]`$), Thermodynamic Temperature ($`[\Theta]`$), Amount of Substance ($`[N]`$), and Luminous Intensity ($`[J]`$).
     - Every conservative physical domain shall be defined by a canonical conjugate variable pair $`\langle e, f \rangle`$, where $`e`$ is an Across variable (potential/effort) and $`f`$ is a Through variable (flow rate/flux).
     - Conjugate Power Product Invariant: The dimensional sum of the conjugate Across and Through variables must identically equal the universal abstract power dimension vector $`\mathbf{d}_{\text{power}} \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$:

$$
\mathbf{d}_e + \mathbf{d}_f \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{power}} = (1, 2, -3, 0, 0, 0, 0) \in \mathbb{Q}^7
$$

       representing synthetic Power with dimensional signature $`[M]^1 [L]^2 [T]^{-3}`$. Floating-point approximations in dimensional coordinate validation are strictly prohibited.
  2. **Conservative Flow Classification Criteria ($`\mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}}`$)**:
     - An item flow or port connection $`f`$ shall be classified as Conservative ($`f \in \mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}}`$) if and only if all of the following conditions are simultaneously satisfied:
       * Both terminal endpoints/ports linked by the flow are typed as conservative physical ports possessing acausal bidirectional semantics.
       * The flow is associated with an Across and Through conjugate variable pair $`\langle e, f \rangle`$ satisfying the power product invariant $`\mathbf{d}_e + \mathbf{d}_f \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{power}}`$.
       * The flow participates in multi-terminal network topologies where branching points act as physical junctions governed by the generalized Kirchhoff Flow Law ($`\sum \text{Through} = 0`$) and closed topological loops are governed by the generalized Kirchhoff Potential Law ($`\oint \text{Across} = 0`$).
     - Conservative flows shall be routed to the acausal network constraint synthesis engine for incidence matrix assembly ($`\mathbf{A}_{\text{cons}}`$), fundamental cycle basis extraction ($`\mathbf{B}_{\text{cons}}`$), and linear/complementarity solver generation in accordance with REQ-0100 and REQ-0105.
  3. **Non-Conservative Flow Classification Criteria ($`\mathcal{F}_{\text{noncons}}`$)**:
     - An item flow or port connection $`f`$ shall be classified as Non-Conservative ($`f \in \mathcal{F}_{\text{noncons}}`$) if and only if any of the following conditions hold:
       * The flow links directed ports with explicit directional indicators (`in`, `out` per REQ-0038) carrying unilateral signals, discrete event messages, software telemetry records, or continuous informational streams.
       * The flow payload consists of a single scalar data type, record structure, or physical quantity lacking a conjugate across/through pair, or whose dimensions do not satisfy the power product condition.
       * The flow evaluates as a unilateral directed functional dependency or causal signal propagation without physical conservation constraints.
     - Non-conservative branching points shall implement signal broadcast and fan-out semantics ($`y_1 = x, y_2 = x`$), replicating the signal value across all connected downstream receiver ports without attenuation, potential drop, or Kirchhoff current division.
     - Non-conservative flows shall be routed to the causal dependency graph $`\mathcal{G}_{\text{causal}}`$ and evaluated via topological sort dependency scheduling in accordance with REQ-0048, REQ-0118, and REQ-0175.
  4. **Strict Disjoint Partitioning Invariant**:
     - The classification pipeline shall partition the universe of all declared flows $`\mathcal{F}_{\text{flow}}`$ into two strictly disjoint subsets such that:

$$
\mathcal{F}_{\text{flow}} = \mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}} \cup \mathcal{F}_{\text{noncons}}, \quad \mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}} \cap \mathcal{F}_{\text{noncons}} = \emptyset
$$

     - Every flow entity must belong to exactly one classification category; ambiguous, untyped, or partially conservative flows are strictly prohibited.
  5. **Incompatible Hybrid Connection & Domain Violation Rejection**:
     - Direct connection between a conservative port and a non-conservative port without an explicit intervening transducer or boundary converter block shall be deterministically rejected at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`IncompatibleFlowClassificationMismatch`) in accordance with REQ-0191.
     - Declaring a flow as conservative when its conjugate variable dimensions fail the power product condition ($`\mathbf{d}_e + \mathbf{d}_f \not\equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{power}}`$) shall be deterministically rejected at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`InvalidConjugatePowerPair`).
     - Multiple non-conservative driving outputs wired directly to a single non-conservative input port without an explicit arbitration, bus, or reduction operator shall be deterministically rejected at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0201` (`MultipleDriverConflict`).
  6. **Transducer & Boundary Converter Elements**:
     - Energy exchange and signal transduction between conservative physical domains and non-conservative computational/signal domains shall require explicit transducer blocks (e.g., synthetic `Transducer_Alpha`).
     - An idealized sensor block maps an Across or Through physical state variable from a conservative junction to a non-conservative signal output with zero power extraction (infinite input impedance and zero insertion loss).
     - An idealized actuator block maps a non-conservative control signal input to a controlled Across potential source or Through flux source in a conservative network.
  7. **Architectural Directives Compliance**:
     - *Directive 1 (Deterministic UUIDv5 Anchors)*: Every classified flow entity, junction node, conjugate pair descriptor, and classification record shall be assigned an immutable deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 identifier derived from the compiler namespace OID and canonical fully qualified path ($`\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(n))`$) in accordance with REQ-0010.
     - *Directive 2 (Bounded Work-Stealing Parallelism)*: Parallel classification of flow subgraphs and port connections across independent compilation units, subsystems, and package modules shall execute strictly on a bounded work-stealing thread pool with worker bound $`N_{\text{max\_workers}}`$ in accordance with REQ-0012.
     - *Directive 3 (String Interner Allocation Exception)*: Flow identifiers, port names, conjugate variable labels, and classification tags shall be interned into the deduplicated global string interner pool during lexical analysis per REQ-0036 as the sole permitted exception to zero-copy memory management.
     - *Directive 4 (Synchronization Token Error Recovery)*: The flow classification engine shall implement synchronization token error recovery on statement delimiters (`;`) and block boundaries (`}`). When semantic classification violations occur (`E0300`, `E0201`), diagnostics shall accumulate into error sink buffer $`\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}`$, substitute typed error sentinels ($`\bot_{\text{flow}}`$, $`\bot_{\text{class}}`$), and continue auditing subsequent connections. If accumulated diagnostics reach $`C_{\text{max\_errors}}`$, terminal diagnostic `E0199` shall be emitted and compilation halted gracefully in accordance with REQ-0013 and REQ-0016.
     - *Directive 5 (Panic-Free Release Isolation)*: The flow network classification engine shall strictly enforce a deterministic, non-panicking execution contract with total-function error handling. Performance latency ($`\lt 25\text{ ms}`$) and peak RSS memory ($`\lt 100\text{ MB}`$ for $`10^4`$ flows) shall be evaluated exclusively in compiled Release mode (`--release`) under dedicated benchmark harnesses isolated from CI logic tests in accordance with REQ-0011 and REQ-0196.

  All diagnostic messages, test models, and illustrative examples shall strictly employ synthetic abstract MBSE placeholders (`Package_0`, `Package_1`, `Classifier_Alpha`, `Classifier_Beta`, `Port_1`, `Port_2`, `Flow_A`, `Flow_B`, `Junction_0`, `Junction_1`, `Transducer_Alpha`, `Quantity_Alpha`, `Quantity_Beta`, `param_x : Real`), strictly prohibiting hardcoded domain concepts, mock data, and non-ASCII dashes.

- **Formal Invariant**: Let $`\mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}`$ denote the universe of allocated AST nodes in contiguous bump arena storage.

  Let $`\mathcal{P}_{\text{port}} \subset \mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}`$ denote the set of port interface nodes, and let $`\mathcal{F}_{\text{flow}} \subset \mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}`$ denote the set of item flow and connection nodes.

  The 7-dimensional physical quantity dimension space is formalized as a rational vector space over the field $`\mathbb{Q}`$:

$$
\mathbb{Q}^7 = \{ \mathbf{d} = (\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \epsilon, \zeta, \eta) \mid \alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \epsilon, \zeta, \eta \in \mathbb{Q} \}
$$

  where the ordered coordinate positions correspond to the canonical basis $`\mathcal{B} = \{ [M], [L], [T], [I], [\Theta], [N], [J] \}`$. The universal abstract power dimension vector $`\mathbf{d}_{\text{power}}`$ is defined by:

$$
\mathbf{d}_{\text{power}} = (1, 2, -3, 0, 0, 0, 0) \in \mathbb{Q}^7
$$

  Each flow node $`f \in \mathcal{F}_{\text{flow}}`$ is formalized as an 8-tuple:

$$
f = \langle \text{id}_f, \text{name}_f, p_{\text{src}}, p_{\text{tgt}}, \kappa_f, \mathbf{c}_f, \Delta_f, \text{span}_f \rangle
$$

  where:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{id}_f &\in \mathbb{N}_{32} \quad (\text{contiguous arena node handle}) \\
\text{name}_f &\in \mathcal{S}_{\text{intern}} \quad (\text{interned symbol token identifier}) \\
p_{\text{src}}, p_{\text{tgt}} &\in \mathcal{P}_{\text{port}} \quad (\text{source and target port handles}) \\
\kappa_f &\in \{ \text{FlowClass}_{\text{cons}}, \text{FlowClass}_{\text{noncons}}, \bot_{\text{class}} \} \quad (\text{flow classification}) \\
\mathbf{c}_f &\in (\mathbb{Q}^7 \times \mathbb{Q}^7) \cup \mathbb{Q}^7 \cup \{ \bot \} \quad (\text{conjugate pair or signal dimension}) \\
\Delta_f &\in \{0, 1\}^{128} \quad (\text{deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 topological anchor}) \\
\text{span}_f &= \langle \text{file\_id}, p_{\text{start}}, p_{\text{end}}, \text{line}, \text{col} \rangle \quad (\text{lexical source provenance})
\end{aligned}
$$

  Each port interface node $`p \in \mathcal{P}_{\text{port}}`$ is formalized as a 6-tuple:

$$
p = \langle \text{id}_p, \text{name}_p, \text{kind}_p, \mathbf{d}_p, \Delta_p, \text{span}_p \rangle
$$

  where:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{id}_p &\in \mathbb{N}_{32} \quad (\text{contiguous arena node handle}) \\
\text{name}_p &\in \mathcal{S}_{\text{intern}} \quad (\text{interned port name identifier}) \\
\text{kind}_p &\in \{ \text{PortKind}_{\text{cons}}, \text{PortKind}_{\text{signal\_in}}, \text{PortKind}_{\text{signal\_out}}, \text{PortKind}_{\text{signal\_inout}} \} \\
\mathbf{d}_p &\in (\mathbb{Q}^7 \times \mathbb{Q}^7) \cup \mathbb{Q}^7 \cup \{ \bot \} \quad (\text{port dimensional signature}) \\
\Delta_p &\in \{0, 1\}^{128} \quad (\text{deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 topological anchor}) \\
\text{span}_p &= \langle \text{file\_id}, p_{\text{start}}, p_{\text{end}}, \text{line}, \text{col} \rangle \quad (\text{source lexical provenance})
\end{aligned}
$$

  Strict Disjoint Flow Partition Invariant:

$$
\mathcal{F}_{\text{flow}} = \mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}} \cup \mathcal{F}_{\text{noncons}}, \quad \mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}} \cap \mathcal{F}_{\text{noncons}} = \emptyset
$$

  Conjugate Power Product Predicate: For a conjugate variable pair $`\mathbf{c}_f = \langle \mathbf{d}_e, \mathbf{d}_f \rangle \in \mathbb{Q}^7 \times \mathbb{Q}^7`$:

$$
\Phi_{\text{power}}(\langle \mathbf{d}_e, \mathbf{d}_f \rangle) \iff \mathbf{d}_e + \mathbf{d}_f \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{power}} = (1, 2, -3, 0, 0, 0, 0)
$$

  Flow Classification Predicates:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\kappa_f = \text{FlowClass}_{\text{cons}} &\iff \left( \text{kind}(p_{\text{src}}) = \text{PortKind}_{\text{cons}} \wedge \text{kind}(p_{\text{tgt}}) = \text{PortKind}_{\text{cons}} \wedge \Phi_{\text{power}}(\mathbf{c}_f) \right) \\
\kappa_f = \text{FlowClass}_{\text{noncons}} &\iff \left( \text{kind}(p_{\text{src}}) \in \{ \text{PortKind}_{\text{signal\_out}}, \text{PortKind}_{\text{signal\_inout}} \} \wedge \text{kind}(p_{\text{tgt}}) \in \{ \text{PortKind}_{\text{signal\_in}}, \text{PortKind}_{\text{signal\_inout}} \} \right)
\end{aligned}
$$

  Incompatible Hybrid Port Connection Rejection:

$$
\left( \text{kind}(p_{\text{src}}) = \text{PortKind}_{\text{cons}} \oplus \text{kind}(p_{\text{tgt}}) = \text{PortKind}_{\text{cons}} \right) \implies \kappa_f = \bot_{\text{class}}
$$

  Acausal Conservative Network Constraint Routing: Let $`\mathcal{G}_{\text{cons}} = (\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}, \mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}})`$ be the conservative network multigraph, with incidence matrix $`\mathbf{A}_{\text{cons}} \in \{-1, 0, +1\}^{|\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}| \times |\mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}}|}`$ and fundamental cycle matrix $`\mathbf{B}_{\text{cons}} \in \{-1, 0, +1\}^{N_C \times |\mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}}|}`$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\forall J_i \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}, &\quad \sum_{f \in \text{Incident}(J_i) \cap \mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}}} A_{\text{cons}, i, f} \cdot f_{\text{through}} = 0 \iff \mathbf{A}_{\text{cons}} \mathbf{f}_{\text{through}} = \mathbf{0} \\
\forall \mathcal{L}_k \in \mathcal{C}_{\text{cons}}, &\quad \sum_{f \in \text{Edges}(\mathcal{L}_k) \cap \mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}}} B_{\text{cons}, k, f} \cdot \Delta e_f = 0 \iff \mathbf{B}_{\text{cons}} \mathbf{\Delta e} = \mathbf{0}
\end{aligned}
$$

  Tellegen Orthogonality and Abstract Power Balance:

$$
\mathbf{A}_{\text{cons}} \mathbf{B}_{\text{cons}}^T = \mathbf{0}, \quad \mathbf{f}_{\text{through}}^T \mathbf{\Delta e} = \sum_{f \in \mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}}} f_{\text{through}} \cdot \Delta e_f = 0
$$

  Causal Signal Dependency Routing & Broadcast Preservation: Let $`\mathcal{G}_{\text{causal}} = (\mathcal{V}_{\text{block}}, \mathcal{F}_{\text{noncons}})`$ be the causal signal dependency multigraph:

$$
\forall p_{\text{src}} \in \mathcal{P}_{\text{port}}, \; \forall f_1, f_2 \in \text{OutFlows}(p_{\text{src}}) \cap \mathcal{F}_{\text{noncons}} \implies \text{val}(p_{\text{tgt}}(f_1)) = \text{val}(p_{\text{tgt}}(f_2)) = \text{val}(p_{\text{src}})
$$

  Deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 Anchor Invariant (Architectural Directive 1):

$$
\begin{aligned}
\forall f \in \mathcal{F}_{\text{flow}}, &\quad \Delta_f = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(f)) \\
\forall p \in \mathcal{P}_{\text{port}}, &\quad \Delta_p = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(p))
\end{aligned}
$$

  Diagnostic Trigger Rules: In accordance with REQ-0191, classification violations and invalid connection configurations deterministically emit structured diagnostics into error sink buffer $`\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}`$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\exists f \in \mathcal{F}_{\text{flow}} : \kappa_f = \bot_{\text{class}} &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(f, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0300}, \text{IncompatibleFlowClassificationMismatch}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
\exists f \in \mathcal{F}_{\text{flow}} : \text{is\_cons\_declared}(f) \wedge \neg\Phi_{\text{power}}(\mathbf{c}_f) &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(f, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0300}, \text{InvalidConjugatePowerPair}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
\exists p \in \mathcal{P}_{\text{port}} : |\text{InFlows}(p) \cap \mathcal{F}_{\text{noncons}}| \gt 1 \wedge \neg\text{has\_arbitration}(p) &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(p, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0201}, \text{MultipleDriverConflict}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}| \ge C_{\text{max\_errors}} &\implies \exists D_{\text{term}} \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} : \text{code}(D_{\text{term}}) = \text{DiagnosticCode::E0199}
\end{aligned}
$$

- **Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds**: Flow network classification, conjugate power product evaluation, and constraint routing partition execute strictly within Complexity Class $`\mathbf{P}`$.

  For an MBSE system model comprising $`N_F = |\mathcal{F}_{\text{flow}}|`$ flow connections, $`N_P = |\mathcal{P}_{\text{port}}|`$ port interfaces, and $`N_V = |\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}|`$ junction nodes:
  1. *Per-Flow Classification Complexity*:
     Inspecting source and target port interface types and validating the conjugate power product $`\mathbf{d}_e + \mathbf{d}_f \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{power}}`$ performs exactly 7 rational coordinate additions and 7 equality comparisons in $`\mathbb{Q}^7`$. Each rational arithmetic reduction terminates in bounded Euclidean algorithm division steps, executing in deterministic $`O(1)`$ time and requiring zero heap allocations:

$$
T_{\text{flow\_classify}} \in O(1)
$$

  2. *Complete Flow Graph Classification Traversal*:
     Classifying all $`N_F`$ flow connections and partitioning them into $`\mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}}`$ and $`\mathcal{F}_{\text{noncons}}`$ visits each flow descriptor exactly once in a single linear pass:

$$
T_{\text{classification}}(N_F) \in O(N_F)
$$

  3. *Conservative Flow Subgraph Assembly*:
     Allocating and populating the sparse incidence matrix $`\mathbf{A}_{\text{cons}} \in \{-1, 0, +1\}^{N_V \times |\mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}}|}`$ inspects each conservative branch once, requiring $`O(N_V + |\mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}}|)`$ time. Extracting the fundamental cycle basis via spanning tree depth-first search (DFS) requires $`O(N_V \cdot |\mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}}|)`$ time:

$$
T_{\text{cons\_subgraph}}(N_V, |\mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}}|) \in O(N_V \cdot |\mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}}|)
$$

  4. *Non-Conservative Causal DAG Assembly & Validation*:
     Constructing the causal signal dependency multigraph $`\mathcal{G}_{\text{causal}} = (\mathcal{V}_{\text{block}}, \mathcal{F}_{\text{noncons}})`$, validating single-driver invariants, and performing topological sort scheduling via Kahn's algorithm or Tarjan's strongly connected components algorithm executes in deterministic linear time:

$$
T_{\text{noncons\_dag}}(|\mathcal{V}_{\text{block}}|, |\mathcal{F}_{\text{noncons}}|) \in O(|\mathcal{V}_{\text{block}}| + |\mathcal{F}_{\text{noncons}}|)
$$

  5. *Aggregate Algorithmic Complexity*:
     The combined execution time for classification and routing partition is bounded by:

$$
T_{\text{total}}(N_F, N_P, N_V, |\mathcal{V}_{\text{block}}|) \in O(N_F + N_P + N_V \cdot |\mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}}| + |\mathcal{V}_{\text{block}}|) \subseteq O(N_F + N_V \cdot N_F)
$$

     For sparse physical topologies where junction node degrees are bounded by constant $`d_{\text{max}} = O(1)`$, the total time simplifies to strictly linear time:

$$
T_{\text{sparse}} \in O(N_F + N_P + N_V + |\mathcal{V}_{\text{block}}|)
$$

  6. *Parallel Work-Stealing Scaling (Architectural Directive 2)*:
     When flow classification across independent packages or subsystems is scheduled onto a bounded work-stealing thread pool with worker bound $`N_{\text{max\_workers}}`$:

$$
T_{\text{parallel}}(N_F, N_V, N_{\text{max\_workers}}) \in O\left( \frac{N_F + N_V \cdot |\mathcal{F}_{\text{cons}}|}{N_{\text{max\_workers}}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}} \right)
$$

  7. *Space Complexity & Memory Bounds*:
     Contiguous arena allocations for flow descriptors, port handles, classification records, and partition subgraphs are strictly bounded by $`O(N_F + N_P + N_V)`$ bytes. Deduplicated string interner memory is bounded by the number of unique port and flow symbol strings with $`O(1)`$ amortized cost. The diagnostic error sink buffer is strictly bounded by $`O(C_{\text{max\_errors}}) = O(1)`$.
