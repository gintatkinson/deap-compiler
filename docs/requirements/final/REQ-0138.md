### REQ-0138: Structured `ConstraintExpr` AST Invariant Generation with De Morgan Normalization

- **Normative Statement**: The safety compiler, formal verification framework, and invariant synthesis pipeline of Subsystem 7 shall construct, validate, and normalize safety invariants as strongly-typed `ConstraintExpr` abstract syntax tree (AST) structures in contiguous bump arena storage, applying logical equivalence transformations, recursive De Morgan laws, relational complementation, distributive expansion or bounded Tseitin definitional conversion, and algebraic constant folding to emit canonical, minimal Conjunctive Normal Form (CNF) representations.

  Specifically, the safety invariant compiler, constraint expression builder, and normalization pipeline shall strictly enforce the following structural, algebraic, and operational invariants across all compilation units:
  1. **Strongly-Typed `ConstraintExpr` Abstract Syntax Tree Architecture**:
     - The compiler shall allocate all invariant expressions within contiguous bump arena memory as strongly-typed `ConstraintExpr` AST node records.
     - Each `ConstraintExpr` node shall belong to an explicit syntactic variant:
       - Logical Connectives: Conjunction (`And`), Disjunction (`Or`), Negation (`Not`), Implication (`Implies`), Equivalence (`Iff`), and Exclusive-OR (`Xor`).
       - Relational Operators: Binary comparison predicates over scalar or dimensional terms: Equal (`Equal`), NotEqual (`NotEqual`), LessThan (`LessThan`), LessThanOrEqual (`LessThanOrEqual`), GreaterThan (`GreaterThan`), and GreaterThanOrEqual (`GreaterThanOrEqual`).
       - Atomic Propositions: Grounded Boolean predicate symbols, statechart state active predicates (`InState(State_0)`), or Boolean port flow signals.
       - Arithmetic & Dimensional Terms: Pure expressions over numerical attributes, constants, or quantities within the 7-dimensional $`\mathbb{Q}^7`$ rational metric space (REQ-0098).
       - Strongly-Typed Literals: Boolean literals (`true`, `false`), exact rational numbers, integers, strings, and enumeration constants.
     - Static Semantic Typing: The compiler shall verify that logical operators operate strictly on expressions evaluating to standard primitive `Boolean`. Relational operators must compare operands whose evaluated types unify in the subtyping semilattice and share identical dimensional exponents in $`\mathbb{Q}^7`$ (REQ-0101). Any expression violating typing or dimensional homogeneity shall be deterministically rejected at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0400` (`InvalidConstraintExpressionType`).
  2. **Implication, Equivalence & Exclusive-OR Elimination (Phase 1)**:
     - The compiler shall rewrite all high-level logical connectives into primitive Boolean conjunctions, disjunctions, and negations via total functional rewrites:
       - Implication Elimination: $`A \implies B \rightsquigarrow \neg A \lor B`$.
       - Equivalence Elimination: $`A \iff B \rightsquigarrow (\neg A \lor B) \land (\neg B \lor A)`$.
       - Exclusive-OR Elimination: $`A \oplus B \rightsquigarrow (A \lor B) \land (\neg A \lor \neg B)`$.
     - Elimination passes shall execute functional, side-effect-free recursive descents preserving exact truth-functional equivalence across all state valuations.
  3. **Negation Normal Form (NNF) via Recursive De Morgan Transformations (Phase 2)**:
     - The compiler shall push all negation operators inward to the leaf literals by recursively applying De Morgan's laws:
       - De Morgan over Conjunction: $`\neg (A \land B) \rightsquigarrow \neg A \lor \neg B`$.
       - De Morgan over Disjunction: $`\neg (A \lor B) \rightsquigarrow \neg A \land \neg B`$.
       - Generalized n-ary De Morgan: $`\neg \left( \bigwedge_{i=1}^k e_i \right) \rightsquigarrow \bigvee_{i=1}^k \neg e_i`$, and $`\neg \left( \bigvee_{i=1}^k e_i \right) \rightsquigarrow \bigwedge_{i=1}^k \neg e_i`$.
     - Double Negation Elimination: The compiler shall eliminate double negations: $`\neg(\neg A) \rightsquigarrow A`$.
     - Relational Complementation & Duality Inversion: When negating relational comparison predicates, the compiler shall invert the relational operator to its dual complement:
       - $`\neg (t_1 \lt t_2) \rightsquigarrow t_1 \ge t_2`$
       - $`\neg (t_1 \le t_2) \rightsquigarrow t_1 \gt t_2`$
       - $`\neg (t_1 \gt t_2) \rightsquigarrow t_1 \le t_2`$
       - $`\neg (t_1 \ge t_2) \rightsquigarrow t_1 \lt t_2`$
       - $`\neg (t_1 = t_2) \rightsquigarrow t_1 \ne t_2`$
       - $`\neg (t_1 \ne t_2) \rightsquigarrow t_1 = t_2`$
     - Boolean Constant Negation: $`\neg \text{true} \rightsquigarrow \text{false}`$, and $`\neg \text{false} \rightsquigarrow \text{true}`$.
     - Negation Normal Form Invariant: Upon completion of Phase 2, negation operators must appear strictly and exclusively on atomic propositions or relational leaf literals. No negation operator may dominate a conjunction or disjunction node.
  4. **Conjunctive Normal Form (CNF) Lowering & Bounded Tseitin Transformation (Phase 3)**:
     - The compiler shall convert NNF expressions into Conjunctive Normal Form (a conjunction of disjunctive clauses) using algebraic distribution:
       - Right Distribution: $`A \lor (B \land C) \rightsquigarrow (A \lor B) \land (A \lor C)`$.
       - Left Distribution: $`(A \land B) \lor C \rightsquigarrow (A \lor C) \land (B \lor C)`$.
     - Clause Explosion Mitigation & Bounded Expansion Threshold ($`K_{\text{dist\_max}}`$):
       - To prevent exponential clause growth on deeply nested disjunctions of conjunctions, the compiler shall enforce a configurable clause expansion threshold $`K_{\text{dist\_max}}`$ (defaulting to 256 clauses per invariant).
       - Prior to applying distributive laws, the compiler shall compute the projected clause cardinality $`K_{\text{proj}}`$.
       - If $`K_{\text{proj}} \le K_{\text{dist\_max}}`$, the compiler shall apply direct distributive expansion to yield a logically equivalent CNF formula.
       - If $`K_{\text{proj}} \gt K_{\text{dist\_max}}`$, the compiler shall automatically apply the Tseitin definitional transformation, introducing auxiliary Boolean proposition variables $`p_{\text{aux}} \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{aux}}`$ for complex subformulas and emitting equi-satisfiable definitional clauses, guaranteeing strictly linear $`O(N)`$ clause generation without clause explosion.
  5. **Algebraic Simplification, Subsumption & Constant Folding (Phase 4)**:
     - Identity and Domination Laws:
       - $`A \land \text{true} \rightsquigarrow A`$, and $`A \land \text{false} \rightsquigarrow \text{false}`$.
       - $`A \lor \text{false} \rightsquigarrow A`$, and $`A \lor \text{true} \rightsquigarrow \text{true}`$.
     - Idempotence: Duplicate literals within any disjunctive clause shall be merged ($`l \lor l \rightsquigarrow l`$). Duplicate clauses within the top-level conjunction shall be merged ($`C \land C \rightsquigarrow C`$).
     - Tautology Elimination: Any clause containing complementary literals ($`l \lor \neg l`$) or containing literal $`\text{true}`$ evaluates unconditionally to $`\text{true}`$ and shall be removed from the CNF conjunction. If all clauses in an invariant evaluate to $`\text{true}`$, the invariant is vacuously tautological and provides zero constraint, prompting compile-time emission of diagnostic error code `E0400` (`TautologicalSafetyInvariant`).
     - Contradiction Detection: If a clause contains literal $`\text{false}`$, $`\text{false}`$ shall be removed ($`l \lor \text{false} \rightsquigarrow l`$). If a clause becomes empty ($`\bigvee \emptyset \equiv \text{false}`$) or contains an explicit contradiction ($`l \land \neg l`$ at root), the invariant reduces to $`\text{false}`$, indicating an unsatisfiable safety invariant that can never be met in any system state, triggering compile-time rejection with diagnostic error code `E0400` (`UnsatisfiableSafetyInvariant`).
     - Clause Subsumption: If clause $`C_1 \subseteq C_2`$ (every literal of $`C_1`$ is present in $`C_2`$), then $`C_1 \land C_2 \equiv C_1`$, and clause $`C_2`$ shall be subsumed and eliminated from the CNF formula.
  6. **Canonical Deterministic Sorting & Bitwise Parity Invariant**:
     - The compiler shall sort literals within every clause according to a canonical strict total order $`\prec_{\text{lit}}`$ based on interned symbol identifiers, variable names, and polarity.
     - The compiler shall sort clauses within every CNF conjunction according to a canonical strict total order $`\prec_{\text{clause}}`$ based on clause cardinality and the canonical order of constituent literals.
     - Emitted CNF structures and serialized models shall produce 100% bitwise identical byte streams with zero diff churn across compilations, regardless of thread pool size or scheduling variations. Any reduction divergence shall trigger diagnostic error code `E0400` (`NonDeterministicNormalization`).
     - Recursion Depth Protection: Normalization passes shall enforce a maximum AST recursion depth $`D_{\text{max\_depth}}`$ (defaulting to 512 frames). If recursion depth exceeds $`D_{\text{max\_depth}}`$, the compiler shall abort traversal and emit diagnostic error code `E0400` (`ConstraintRecursionDepthExceeded`).
  7. **Downstream Safety & Verification Pipeline Integration**:
     - Safety invariants automatically synthesized from hazard scenarios (REQ-0137) and minimal hazard cut-sets (REQ-0135) shall be constructed as `ConstraintExpr` trees and normalized into canonical CNF.
     - Canonical CNF invariants shall serve as standardized inputs for formal proof obligations and decidable SMT solvers (REQ-0188, REQ-0197 NP-8).
     - Normalized invariant clauses shall be compiled into Run-Time Assurance (RTA) simplex monitors (REQ-0141) and forward invariance contracts for Control Barrier Functions (REQ-0143).
  8. **The 5 Executive Architectural Directives Compliance**:
     - *Directive 1 (Deterministic UUIDv5 Anchors)*: Every `ConstraintExpr` AST node, normalized clause, and synthetic auxiliary variable shall bind an immutable deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 identifier computed from the canonical DEAP namespace OID and node fully qualified topological path or structural formula digest ($`\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(e) \mathbin{\Vert} \text{"::"} \mathbin{\Vert} \text{StructuralHash}(e))`$) in accordance with REQ-0010. Time-based UUIDs are strictly banned.
     - *Directive 2 (Bounded Work-Stealing Parallelism)*: Parallel normalization of invariant expression sets across independent packages, components, and hazard scenarios shall execute strictly on a centralized, bounded work-stealing thread pool with worker ceiling $`N_{\text{max\_workers}}`$ in accordance with REQ-0012. Unbounded manual thread spawning is strictly banned.
     - *Directive 3 (String Interner Memory Allocation Exception)*: Predicate identifiers, variable names, invariant labels, and fully qualified path tokens shall be interned into the deduplicated global symbol interner pool during lexical analysis per REQ-0036 as the sole permitted exception to zero-copy memory management.
     - *Directive 4 (Synchronization Token Error Recovery)*: The safety compiler shall implement synchronization token error recovery on statement delimiters (`;`) and block boundaries (`}`). When semantic typing violations (`E0400`), contradictory invariants (`E0400`), or malformed expressions occur, diagnostics shall accumulate in the error sink buffer $`\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}`$, substitute typed error sentinels ($`\bot_{\text{expr}}`$), and continue validating subsequent declarations. If accumulated diagnostics reach capacity $`C_{\text{max\_errors}}`$, terminal diagnostic `E0199` shall be emitted and compilation halted gracefully in accordance with REQ-0013 and REQ-0016.
     - *Directive 5 (Panic-Free Release Paths & Benchmarking Isolation)*: The invariant normalization engine shall strictly enforce a deterministic, non-panicking execution contract with total-function error handling. Invariant generation and normalization latency ($`\lt 25\text{ ms}`$ for $`10^4`$ AST constraint nodes) and peak RSS memory ($`\lt 100\text{ MB}`$) performance gates shall be evaluated exclusively in compiled Release mode (`--release`) under dedicated benchmark harnesses isolated from CI logic tests in accordance with REQ-0011 and REQ-0196.

  All diagnostic messages, test models, and illustrative examples shall strictly employ synthetic abstract MBSE placeholders (`Package_0`, `Package_1`, `Classifier_Alpha`, `Classifier_Beta`, `Constraint_0`, `Constraint_1`, `Invariant_0`, `Invariant_1`, `State_0`, `State_1`, `Port_1`, `Flow_A`, `param_x : Real`, `param_y : Real`), strictly prohibiting hardcoded domain concepts, mock data, and non-ASCII dashes.

- **Formal Invariant**: Let $`\mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}`$ denote the universe of allocated AST nodes in contiguous bump arena storage, and let $`\mathcal{S}_{\text{intern}}`$ denote the deduplicated global symbol interner pool.

  The constraint expression universe $`\mathcal{E}_{\text{expr}} \subset \mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}`$ is inductive over terms and logical operators:

$$
  e ::= \text{LitBool}(b) \mid \text{Atom}(p) \mid \text{RelOp}(\circ, t_1, t_2) \mid \text{Not}(e) \mid \text{And}(e_1, \dots, e_k) \mid \text{Or}(e_1, \dots, e_k) \mid \text{Implies}(e_1, e_2) \mid \text{Iff}(e_1, e_2) \mid \text{Xor}(e_1, e_2)
$$

  where $`\circ \in \{ =, \ne, \lt, \le, \gt, \ge \}`$, $`b \in \{ \text{true}, \text{false} \}`$, $`p \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{intern}}`$, and $`t_1, t_2`$ are typed arithmetic or feature terms.

  Each allocated `ConstraintExpr` node $`e \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{expr}}`$ is formalized as a 6-tuple:

$$
  e = \langle \text{id}_e, \kappa_e, \mathbf{children}_e, \tau(e), \Delta_e, \text{span}_e \rangle
$$

  where:

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \text{id}_e &\in \mathbb{N}_{32} \quad (\text{contiguous arena node handle}) \\
  \kappa_e &\in \mathcal{K}_{\text{expr}} \quad (\text{syntactic operator or term kind}) \\
  \mathbf{children}_e &= (c_1, \dots, c_r) \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}^r \quad (\text{ordered sequence of child node handles}) \\
  \tau(e) &\in \mathcal{T} \quad (\text{evaluated semantic type}, \, \tau(e) \equiv \mathbb{B}) \\
  \Delta_e &\in \Sigma^{36} \quad (\text{deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 topological anchor}) \\
  \text{span}_e &= \langle \text{file\_id}, p_{\text{start}}, p_{\text{end}}, \text{line}, \text{col} \rangle \quad (\text{source lexical provenance span})
  \end{aligned}
$$

  1. Phase 1: Connective Elimination Transformations ($`\mathcal{T}_{\text{elim}}`$):

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \mathcal{T}_{\text{elim}}(\text{Implies}(A, B)) &= \text{Or}(\text{Not}(\mathcal{T}_{\text{elim}}(A)), \mathcal{T}_{\text{elim}}(B)) \\
  \mathcal{T}_{\text{elim}}(\text{Iff}(A, B)) &= \text{And}(\text{Or}(\text{Not}(\mathcal{T}_{\text{elim}}(A)), \mathcal{T}_{\text{elim}}(B)), \text{Or}(\text{Not}(\mathcal{T}_{\text{elim}}(B)), \mathcal{T}_{\text{elim}}(A))) \\
  \mathcal{T}_{\text{elim}}(\text{Xor}(A, B)) &= \text{And}(\text{Or}(\mathcal{T}_{\text{elim}}(A), \mathcal{T}_{\text{elim}}(B)), \text{Or}(\text{Not}(\mathcal{T}_{\text{elim}}(A)), \text{Not}(\mathcal{T}_{\text{elim}}(B))))
  \end{aligned}
$$

  2. Phase 2: Negation Normal Form (NNF) & De Morgan Transformations ($`\mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}`$):

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(\text{And}(e_1, \dots, e_k))) &= \bigvee_{i=1}^k \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(e_i)) \\
  \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(\text{Or}(e_1, \dots, e_k))) &= \bigwedge_{i=1}^k \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(e_i)) \\
  \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(\text{Not}(e))) &= \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(e) \\
  \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(\text{LitBool}(\text{true}))) &= \text{LitBool}(\text{false}) \\
  \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(\text{LitBool}(\text{false}))) &= \text{LitBool}(\text{true})
  \end{aligned}
$$

  Relational complementation duality preserves semantic equivalence without negation wrappers:

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(\text{RelOp}(=, t_1, t_2))) &= \text{RelOp}(\ne, t_1, t_2) \\
  \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(\text{RelOp}(\ne, t_1, t_2))) &= \text{RelOp}(=, t_1, t_2) \\
  \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(\text{RelOp}(\lt, t_1, t_2))) &= \text{RelOp}(\ge, t_1, t_2) \\
  \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(\text{RelOp}(\le, t_1, t_2))) &= \text{RelOp}(\gt, t_1, t_2) \\
  \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(\text{RelOp}(\gt, t_1, t_2))) &= \text{RelOp}(\le, t_1, t_2) \\
  \mathcal{T}_{\text{NNF}}(\text{Not}(\text{RelOp}(\ge, t_1, t_2))) &= \text{RelOp}(\lt, t_1, t_2)
  \end{aligned}
$$

  The resulting NNF expression satisfies the predicate:

$$
  \text{IsNNF}(e) \iff \forall n \in \text{Subnodes}(e), \; \kappa_n = \text{Not} \implies \kappa_{\text{child}(n)} \in \{ \text{Atom} \}
$$

  3. Phase 3: Conjunctive Normal Form (CNF) Lowering ($`\mathcal{T}_{\text{CNF}}`$):
  Every normalized invariant $`\phi`$ is represented in canonical Conjunctive Normal Form:

$$
  \phi = \bigwedge_{i=1}^m C_i = \bigwedge_{i=1}^m \left( \bigvee_{j=1}^{k_i} l_{ij} \right)
$$

  where each literal $`l_{ij}`$ is an atomic proposition $`p`$, a negated atomic proposition $`\neg p`$, a relational expression $`\text{RelOp}(\circ, t_1, t_2)`$, or a Boolean constant $`b \in \{ \text{true}, \text{false} \}`$.

  Algebraic distribution enforces:

$$
  \mathcal{T}_{\text{dist}}(A \lor (B \land C)) = \mathcal{T}_{\text{dist}}(A \lor B) \land \mathcal{T}_{\text{dist}}(A \lor C)
$$

  When projected clauses exceed the threshold ($`K_{\text{proj}} \gt K_{\text{dist\_max}}`$), the Tseitin definitional transformation introduces auxiliary variables $`p_{\psi} \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{aux}}`$:

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  p_{\psi} \iff (\psi_1 \lor \psi_2) &\equiv (\neg p_{\psi} \lor \psi_1 \lor \psi_2) \land (\neg \psi_1 \lor p_{\psi}) \land (\neg \psi_2 \lor p_{\psi}) \\
  p_{\psi} \iff (\psi_1 \land \psi_2) &\equiv (p_{\psi} \lor \neg \psi_1 \lor \neg \psi_2) \land (\psi_1 \lor \neg p_{\psi}) \land (\psi_2 \lor \neg p_{\psi})
  \end{aligned}
$$

  4. Phase 4: Algebraic Simplification, Subsumption & Constant Folding ($`\mathcal{T}_{\text{simp}}`$):

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \text{Tautology}(C) &\iff (\exists l : l \in C \land \neg l \in C) \lor (\text{true} \in C) \implies C \equiv \text{true} \\
  \text{DropFalse}(C) &\iff C \cup \{ \text{false} \} \rightsquigarrow C \\
  \text{Contradiction}(\phi) &\iff (\emptyset \in \phi) \lor (\exists l : \{ l \} \in \phi \land \{ \neg l \} \in \phi) \implies \phi \equiv \text{false} \\
  \text{Subsumption}(C_a, C_b) &\iff C_a \subseteq C_b \implies C_a \land C_b \equiv C_a
  \end{aligned}
$$

  5. Canonical Total Ordering & Bitwise Parity Invariant:
  Let $`\prec_{\text{lit}}`$ denote the strict total ordering over literals:

$$
  l_1 \prec_{\text{lit}} l_2 \iff \begin{cases}
  \text{FQN}(\text{atom}(l_1)) \lt_{\text{lex}} \text{FQN}(\text{atom}(l_2)) \\
  \text{FQN}(\text{atom}(l_1)) = \text{FQN}(\text{atom}(l_2)) \land \text{polarity}(l_1) \lt \text{polarity}(l_2)
  \end{cases}
$$

  Let $`\prec_{\text{clause}}`$ denote the strict total ordering over disjunctive clauses:

$$
  C_1 \prec_{\text{clause}} C_2 \iff \begin{cases}
  |C_1| \lt |C_2| \\
  |C_1| = |C_2| \land \text{LexCompare}_{\prec_{\text{lit}}}(C_1, C_2) \lt 0
  \end{cases}
$$

  For any two valid execution schedules $`\pi_1, \pi_2 \in \Pi(P_{\text{workers}})`$ across different thread allocations:

$$
  \forall \pi_1, \pi_2 \in \Pi(P_{\text{workers}}), \quad \text{Serialize}(\phi_{\text{CNF}}, \pi_1) = \text{Serialize}(\phi_{\text{CNF}}, \pi_2)
$$

  6. Deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 Anchor Invariant (Directive 1):

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \forall e \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{expr}}, &\quad \Delta_e = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(e) \mathbin{\Vert} \text{"::"} \mathbin{\Vert} \text{StructuralHash}(e)) \\
  \forall p_{\text{aux}} \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{aux}}, &\quad \Delta_{\text{aux}}(p_{\psi}) = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{"aux::"} \mathbin{\Vert} \text{StructuralHash}(\psi))
  \end{aligned}
$$

  7. Deterministic Diagnostic Trigger Rules (REQ-0191):

$$
  \begin{aligned}
  \phi_{\text{CNF}} \equiv \text{false} &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(e, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0400}, \text{UnsatisfiableSafetyInvariant}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  \phi_{\text{CNF}} \equiv \text{true} &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(e, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0400}, \text{TautologicalSafetyInvariant}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  \tau(e) \ne \mathbb{B} &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(e, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0400}, \text{InvalidConstraintExpressionType}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  \text{Depth}(e) \gt D_{\text{max\_depth}} &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(e, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0400}, \text{ConstraintRecursionDepthExceeded}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  \text{Serialize}(\phi, \pi_1) \ne \text{Serialize}(\phi, \pi_2) &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(e, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0400}, \text{NonDeterministicNormalization}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  |\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}| \ge C_{\text{max\_errors}} &\implies \exists D_{\text{term}} \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} : \text{code}(D_{\text{term}}) = \text{DiagnosticCode::E0199}
  \end{aligned}
$$

- **Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds**: Invariant expression construction, connective elimination, De Morgan NNF transformation, bounded CNF conversion, and canonical sorting reside strictly in **Complexity Class P** (Deterministic Polynomial Time).

  **Mathematical Delineation from Algorithmic Bounds NP-4 and NP-8**:
  1. *Delineation from Algorithmic Bound NP-8 (Formal Proof Obligations & Satisfiability, REQ-0197)*:
     Deciding whether an arbitrary CNF formula $`\phi_{\text{CNF}}`$ is satisfiable ($`\exists \sigma : \sigma \models \phi_{\text{CNF}}`$) is the canonical Boolean Satisfiability (SAT) problem, which is **NP-complete** (Cook-Levin theorem). REQ-0138 does NOT solve the satisfiability problem; rather, REQ-0138 executes syntax tree generation, equivalence rewriting, De Morgan normalization, and CNF lowering in deterministic polynomial time (Complexity Class P), preparing structured, normalized formulas for downstream SMT solvers (REQ-0197 NP-8).
  2. *Delineation from Algorithmic Bound NP-4 (STPA Minimal Hazard Cut-Sets, REQ-0135)*:
     Selecting a minimal hitting set of safety constraints to cover a hypergraph of system hazards is **NP-complete**. REQ-0138 operates on individual synthesized constraints and safety invariants, transforming their internal expression ASTs into canonical CNF in deterministic polynomial time without combinatorial cut-set optimization.

  **Asymptotic Complexity Analysis**:
  Let $`N = |\text{Subnodes}(e)|`$ denote the number of AST nodes in the initial `ConstraintExpr` tree, let $`m`$ denote the number of disjunctive clauses in the resulting CNF, and let $`L = \max_i |C_i|`$ denote the maximum clause cardinality.
  1. *Phase 1: Connective Elimination*:
     Traversing the AST and expanding $`\implies, \iff, \oplus`$ into basic connectives visits each node exactly once:

$$
     T_{\text{elim}}(N) \in O(N)
$$

  2. *Phase 2: NNF & De Morgan Normalization*:
     Pushing negations down to leaf literals and inverting relational operators executes via a single-pass recursive post-order traversal:

$$
     T_{\text{NNF}}(N) \in O(N)
$$

     The resulting NNF tree has size $`N_{\text{NNF}} \le 2 N`$.
  3. *Phase 3: CNF Lowering*:
     - When using the Tseitin definitional transformation (triggered whenever $`K_{\text{proj}} \gt K_{\text{dist\_max}}`$), allocating auxiliary variables and emitting definitional clauses requires visiting each subexpression once:

$$
       T_{\text{Tseitin}}(N) \in O(N), \quad \text{Space}_{\text{Tseitin}}(N) \in O(N)
$$

     - When using direct distributive expansion on bounded expressions ($`K_{\text{proj}} \le K_{\text{dist\_max}}`$):

$$
       T_{\text{dist}}(N) \in O(N \cdot K_{\text{dist\_max}}) \subseteq O(N)
$$

       as $`K_{\text{dist\_max}}`$ is a constant bound (default 256).
  4. *Phase 4: Algebraic Simplification, Subsumption & Canonical Sorting*:
     - Sorting literals within $`m`$ clauses: $`O(m \cdot L \log L)`$.
     - Pairwise subsumption check: For $`m`$ clauses, signature bitset filtering achieves $`O(m \cdot L)`$ average time, bounded by $`O(m^2 \cdot L)`$ worst-case.
     - Sorting clauses in the top-level conjunction: $`O(m \log m \cdot L)`$.
  5. *Aggregate Time Complexity*:
     Summing all normalization phases:

$$
     T_{\text{total}}(N) \in O(N \log N + m^2 \cdot L)
$$

     For bounded expressions, $`T_{\text{total}}(N) \in O(N \log N)`$. The entire normalization algorithm operates deterministically without backtracking or recursion stack overflow.
  6. *Bounded Work-Stealing Scheduling Bounds (Directive 2)*:
     When normalizing a set of $`M`$ independent invariants across $`P = |P_{\text{workers}}| = \min(C_{\text{logical}}, N_{\text{max\_workers}})`$ worker threads, expected parallel execution time satisfies:

$$
     T_P \le \frac{T_1}{P} + O(T_\infty) \in O\left( \frac{M \cdot N \log N}{P} + \log(M) \right)
$$

     where critical span $`T_\infty \in O(\log M)`$ bounds work-stealing deque operations and task joining.
