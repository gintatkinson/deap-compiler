### REQ-0104: Rational Power Evaluation for Fractional Exponents ($`A^{p/q}`$)

#### 1. Normative Statement

The physical metrology engine, semantic type checker, constraint evaluator, and AST lowering pipeline of Subsystem 5 shall compute, propagate, and validate physical quantity dimension vectors and exact unit scale factors for all power, root, and exponentiation expressions ($`A^k`$`, `$`\operatorname{pow}(A, k)`$`, `$`\operatorname{sqrt}(A)`$`, `$`\operatorname{cbrt}(A)`$`, `$`\operatorname{root}(A, n)`$`) where base expression `$`A`$` possesses an inferred dimension vector `$`\mathbf{d}_A \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$` and exponent `$`k = \frac{p}{q} \in \mathbb{Q}`$` is a rational constant (`$`p \in \mathbb{Z}, q \in \mathbb{Z}^+, \gcd(|p|, q) = 1`$`). The metrology engine shall compute the resulting dimension vector `$`\mathbf{d}_{A^k}`$` by multiplying the 7-dimensional rational base vector `$`\mathbf{d}_A`$` by scalar rational exponent `$`k`$:

```math
  \mathbf{d}_{A^k} = k \cdot \mathbf{d}_A = \left( k \alpha, k \beta, k \gamma, k \delta, k \epsilon, k \zeta, k \eta \right) \in \mathbb{Q}^7
```

  where each coordinate $`k \cdot x`$` must evaluate to an exact irreducible fraction `$`\frac{a'}{b'}`$` with `$`\gcd(|a'|, b') = 1`$` and `$`b' \gt 0`$.

  Specifically, the metrology engine, type checker, and lowering pipeline shall strictly enforce the following structural, algebraic, and semantic invariants:
  1. **Seven-Dimensional Rational Vector Space Representation ($`\mathbb{Q}^7`$) & Scalar Multiplication**:
     - The metrology engine shall represent every physical quantity dimension as a rational 7-vector $`\mathbf{d} = (\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \epsilon, \zeta, \eta) \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$` over the ordered basis of 7 International System of Quantities (ISQ) base dimensions: Mass (`$`M`$`), Length (`$`L`$`), Time (`$`T`$`), Electric Current (`$`I`$`), Thermodynamic Temperature (`$`\Theta`$`), Amount of Substance (`$`N`$`), and Luminous Intensity (`$`J`$).
     - For any quantity expression $`A`$` with dimension `$`\mathbf{d}_A = (\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \epsilon, \zeta, \eta)`$` raised to rational constant power `$`k = \frac{p}{q} \in \mathbb{Q}`$`, the dimension of the resulting expression shall evaluate to scalar-vector multiplication `$`k \cdot \mathbf{d}_A`$` in `$`\mathbb{Q}^7`$.
     - Each rational coordinate product $`d'_i = k \cdot d_i = \frac{p}{q} \cdot \frac{a_i}{b_i}`$` shall be reduced to canonical irreducible form `$`\frac{a'_i}{b'_i}`$` via integer division by `$`g_i = \gcd(|p \cdot a_i|, q \cdot b_i)`$`, guaranteeing `$`b'_i \gt 0`$` and `$`\gcd(|a'_i|, b'_i) = 1`$.
     - The use of IEEE 754 floating-point representations (`f32`, `f64`) in dimensional vector coordinates, rational exponent calculations, and scale factor derivations is strictly prohibited to ensure cross-platform numerical determinism and 0-bit drift.
  2. **Power & Radical Operator Syntax Classification**:
     - The compiler shall classify and govern all syntactic forms of rational exponentiation across quantity expressions:
       * Binary power operator expressions: $`A^{\wedge} k`$`, `$`A \text{ ** } k`$`, and explicit invocation `$`\operatorname{pow}(A, k)`$` where `$`k \in \mathbb{Q}`$ is a compile-time rational constant.
       * Unary square root function: $`\operatorname{sqrt}(A) \equiv A^{1/2}`$`, multiplying `$`\mathbf{d}_A`$` by scalar factor `$`\frac{1}{2}`$.
       * Unary cube root function: $`\operatorname{cbrt}(A) \equiv A^{1/3}`$`, multiplying `$`\mathbf{d}_A`$` by scalar factor `$`\frac{1}{3}`$.
       * Arbitrary integer radical function: $`\operatorname{root}(A, n) \equiv A^{1/n}`$` where `$`n \in \mathbb{Z} \setminus \{0\}`$`, multiplying `$`\mathbf{d}_A`$` by scalar factor `$`\frac{1}{n}`$.
       * Integer powers: positive integer powers ($`k \in \mathbb{Z}^+`$`), zero power (`$`k = 0`$`), and negative integer powers (`$`k \in \mathbb{Z}^-`$).
       * Fractional rational powers: general irreducible fractions $`k = \frac{p}{q}`$` (`$`p \in \mathbb{Z}, q \in \mathbb{Z}^+, \gcd(|p|, q) = 1`$).
  3. **Unit Scale Factor Transformation & Radical Algebraic Preservation**:
     - For any physical quantity $`A`$` with coherent dimension `$`\mathbf{d}_A`$` and exact positive rational conversion scale factor `$`c_A \in \mathbb{Q}^+`$ relative to the coherent base unit system:
       * Integer Power Scale Factor: When exponent $`k \in \mathbb{Z}`$`, the resulting scale factor shall evaluate to exact rational power `$`c_{A^k} = c_A^k \in \mathbb{Q}^+`$.
       * Rational Root Scale Factor: When exponent $`k = \frac{p}{q} \notin \mathbb{Z}`$`, if scale factor `$`c_A = \frac{u}{v}`$` is a perfect `$`q`$`-th rational power (such that `$`\exists r, s \in \mathbb{Z}^+`$` with `$`r^q = u`$` and `$`s^q = v`$`), the scale factor shall evaluate to exact irreducible rational number `$`c_{A^k} = (r/s)^p \in \mathbb{Q}^+`$.
       * Algebraic Radical Preservation: If $`c_A`$` is not a perfect `$`q`$`-th power in `$`\mathbb{Q}`$`, the compiler shall preserve the conversion scale factor symbolically as an exact algebraic radical factor `$`\sqrt[q]{c_A^p} = c_A^{p/q}`$ within the unit descriptor to prevent loss of precision during downstream code generation and intermediate representations.
  4. **Metrological Invariants & Domain Restrictions**:
     - *Zero Exponent Invariant ($`k = 0`$`)*: For any quantity expression `$`A`$` whose base value is non-zero, raising to power `$`0`$` shall evaluate strictly to the dimensionless null vector `$`\mathbf{d}_{A^0} = 0 \cdot \mathbf{d}_A = \mathbf{0} = (0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0) \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$` with scale factor `$`1`$.
     - *Zero Denominator Protection ($`q \ne 0`$`)*: Any power specification where exponent denominator `$`q = 0`$` (such as `$`A^{p/0}`$) is mathematically undefined and shall be rejected deterministically at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`RationalArithmeticDomainError`).
     - *Non-Rational Exponent on Dimensioned Base Rejection*: If base expression $`A`$` possesses a non-zero physical dimension (`$`\mathbf{d}_A \ne \mathbf{0}`$`) and the exponent expression `$`E_{\text{exp}}`$ is not a compile-time rational constant (e.g., a runtime variable, dynamic parameter, or transcendental expression), the expression violates dimensional homogeneity and shall be rejected deterministically at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`NonRationalExponentOnDimensionedBase`). If base $`A`$` is dimensionless (`$`\mathbf{d}_A \equiv \mathbf{0}`$), arbitrary power expressions are permitted and governed by REQ-0103.
     - *Affine Unit Exponentiation Rejection*: Physical quantities defined on affine scales with non-zero additive offset $`o \ne 0`$` (such as degree Celsius with offset `$`273.15`$`, or degree Fahrenheit) cannot be raised to non-trivial powers (`$`k \ne 1`$`) without thermodynamic inconsistency. Any power expression `$`A^k`$` where `$`A`$` has an affine unit and `$`k \ne 1`$ shall be rejected at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`AffineUnitExponentiationError`) unless explicitly converted to an absolute thermodynamic ratio-scale unit (such as Kelvin) or declared as an affine difference quantity.
     - *Compile-Time Singularity & Negative Exponent on Zero Base*: If base expression $`A`$` evaluates at compile time to a constant zero scalar or zero quantity and exponent `$`k \le 0`$, the compiler shall reject the expression with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`CompileTimeZeroBaseNegativeExponent`).
     - *Even Root of Negative Constant*: If base expression $`A`$` evaluates at compile time to a negative real constant and exponent denominator `$`q`$` is even (`$`q \equiv 0 \pmod 2`$`, such as `$`\operatorname{sqrt}(-4.0)`$), the expression is undefined over the real scalar domain and shall be rejected deterministically at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`EvenRootOfNegativeQuantity`).
     - *Coordinate Exponent Bounds & Overflow Protection*: The numerator $`a'_i`$` and denominator `$`b'_i`$` of each resulting dimensional coordinate `$`d'_i = \frac{a'_i}{b'_i}`$` shall be strictly bounded within signed 16-bit integer ranges: `$`a'_i \in [-2^{15}, 2^{15}-1]`$` and `$`b'_i \in [1, 2^{15}-1]`$. Any rational coordinate multiplication that overflows these bounds shall be detected and rejected at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`DimensionalExponentOverflow`).
  5. **Architectural Directives Compliance**:
     - *Directive 1 (Deterministic UUIDv5 Anchors)*: Every lowered power expression AST node, radical function node, and synthesized derived unit descriptor shall be assigned an immutable deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 identifier derived from the compiler namespace OID and canonical fully qualified path ($`\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(n))`$) in accordance with REQ-0010.
     - *Directive 2 (Bounded Work-Stealing Parallelism)*: Dimensional inference and rational exponent evaluation across independent compilation units, packages, or equations shall execute concurrently on a bounded work-stealing thread pool with worker bound $`N_{\text{max\_workers}}`$ in accordance with REQ-0012.
     - *Directive 3 (String Interner Allocation Exception)*: Synthesized radical unit symbols (e.g., `m^(1/2)`, `kg^(1/3)*s^(-1)`), function identifiers (`pow`, `sqrt`, `cbrt`, `root`), and exponent string tokens shall be interned into the deduplicated global string interner pool per REQ-0036 as the sole permitted exception to zero-copy memory management.
     - *Directive 4 (Synchronization Token Error Recovery)*: The type checker and metrology lowering engine shall implement synchronization token error recovery on statement delimiters (`;`) and block boundaries (`}`). When semantic violations occur (`E0300`), diagnostics shall accumulate in error sink buffer $`\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}`$`, substitute typed error sentinel `$`\bot_{\text{dim}}`$` (with `$`\mathbf{d}(\bot_{\text{dim}}) = \bot`$`), attach precise source byte spans, and continue checking subsequent expressions. If accumulated diagnostics reach `$`C_{\text{max\_errors}}`$, terminal diagnostic `E0199` shall be emitted and compilation halted gracefully per REQ-0013, REQ-0016, and REQ-0191.
     - *Directive 5 (Panic-Free Release Isolation)*: The rational power evaluation pipeline shall strictly enforce a deterministic, non-panicking execution contract with total-function error handling. Verification latency ($`\lt 25\text{ ms}`$`) and peak memory RSS (`$`\lt 100\text{ MB}`$` for `$`10^5`$ power expressions) shall be evaluated exclusively in compiled Release mode (`--release`) under dedicated benchmark harnesses isolated from CI logic tests in accordance with REQ-0011 and REQ-0196.

  All diagnostic messages, test models, and illustrative examples shall strictly employ synthetic abstract MBSE placeholders (`Package_0`, `Package_1`, `Classifier_Alpha`, `Classifier_Beta`, `Quantity_Alpha`, `Quantity_Beta`, `Unit_Alpha`, `Unit_Beta`, `param_x : Real`, `param_y : Real`, `Expr_Alpha`, `Expr_Beta`), strictly prohibiting hardcoded domain concepts, mock data, and non-ASCII dashes.

#### 2. Formal Invariant

Let $`\mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}`$` denote the universe of allocated AST nodes in contiguous bump arena storage. Let `$`\mathcal{E} \subset \mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}`$ denote the set of all quantity and value expression AST nodes.

  Let $`\mathbb{Q}`$ denote the set of canonical irreducible rational numbers:

```math
  \mathbb{Q} = \left\{ \frac{a}{b} \;\middle|\; a \in \mathbb{Z}, \, b \in \mathbb{Z}^+, \, \gcd(|a|, b) = 1 \right\}
```

  The canonical reduction function $`\operatorname{reduce} : \mathbb{Z} \times (\mathbb{Z} \setminus \{0\}) \to \mathbb{Q}`$ is formalized as:

```math
  \operatorname{reduce}(a, b) = \left\langle \frac{a \cdot \operatorname{sgn}(b)}{\gcd(|a|, |b|)}, \, \frac{|b|}{\gcd(|a|, |b|)} \right\rangle
```

  The physical quantity dimension space is formalized as a 7-dimensional rational vector space:

```math
  \mathcal{D}_{\mathbb{Q}} = (\mathbb{Q}^7, +, \cdot, \mathbb{Q})
```

  over the ordered basis of 7 ISQ base dimensions:

```math
  \mathcal{B}_{\text{SI}} = \{ \mathbf{e}_M, \mathbf{e}_L, \mathbf{e}_T, \mathbf{e}_I, \mathbf{e}_\Theta, \mathbf{e}_N, \mathbf{e}_J \}
```

  Every physical dimension vector $`\mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$ is defined as an irreducible rational 7-tuple:

```math
  \mathbf{d} = (\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \epsilon, \zeta, \eta) \in \mathbb{Q}^7
```

  corresponding to the formal ISQ base quantity power product:

```math
  \mathbf{d} \iff [M]^\alpha [L]^\beta [T]^\gamma [I]^\delta [\Theta]^\epsilon [N]^\zeta [J]^\eta
```

  where each coordinate $`d_i = \frac{a_i}{b_i} \in \mathbb{Q}`$` for `$`i \in \{1, \dots, 7\}`$` satisfies `$`a_i \in \mathbb{Z}, b_i \in \mathbb{Z}^+, \gcd(|a_i|, b_i) = 1`$.

  The dimensionless identity vector $`\mathbf{0} \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$ is defined as:

```math
  \mathbf{0} = (0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0)
```

  Scalar Multiplication Invariant for Rational Exponentiation: For expression $`A \in \mathcal{E}`$` with dimension vector `$`\mathbf{d}_A \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$` raised to rational constant power `$`k = \frac{p}{q} \in \mathbb{Q}`$:

```math
  \begin{aligned}
  \mathbf{d}_{A^k} &= k \cdot \mathbf{d}_A \\
  &= \left( k \alpha, k \beta, k \gamma, k \delta, k \epsilon, k \zeta, k \eta \right) \\
  &= \left( \operatorname{reduce}(p \cdot a_\alpha, q \cdot b_\alpha), \; \dots, \; \operatorname{reduce}(p \cdot a_\eta, q \cdot b_\eta) \right) \in \mathbb{Q}^7
  \end{aligned}
```

  Radical Function Formal Equivalence Invariants:

```math
  \begin{aligned}
  \mathbf{d}(\operatorname{sqrt}(A)) &= \frac{1}{2} \cdot \mathbf{d}_A = \left( \frac{\alpha}{2}, \frac{\beta}{2}, \frac{\gamma}{2}, \frac{\delta}{2}, \frac{\epsilon}{2}, \frac{\zeta}{2}, \frac{\eta}{2} \right) \\
  \mathbf{d}(\operatorname{cbrt}(A)) &= \frac{1}{3} \cdot \mathbf{d}_A = \left( \frac{\alpha}{3}, \frac{\beta}{3}, \frac{\gamma}{3}, \frac{\delta}{3}, \frac{\epsilon}{3}, \frac{\zeta}{3}, \frac{\eta}{3} \right) \\
  \mathbf{d}(\operatorname{root}(A, n)) &= \frac{1}{n} \cdot \mathbf{d}_A = \left( \frac{\alpha}{n}, \frac{\beta}{n}, \frac{\gamma}{n}, \frac{\delta}{n}, \frac{\epsilon}{n}, \frac{\zeta}{n}, \frac{\eta}{n} \right), \quad n \in \mathbb{Z} \setminus \{0\}
  \end{aligned}
```

  Algebraic Homomorphism & Identity Invariants:

```math
  \begin{aligned}
  \mathbf{d}(A^1) &= 1 \cdot \mathbf{d}_A = \mathbf{d}_A \\
  \mathbf{d}(A^0) &= 0 \cdot \mathbf{d}_A = \mathbf{0} \quad (\text{for } A \ne 0) \\
  \mathbf{d}((A^k)^m) &= (k \cdot m) \cdot \mathbf{d}_A = k \cdot (m \cdot \mathbf{d}_A) \\
  \mathbf{d}((A \cdot B)^k) &= k \cdot (\mathbf{d}_A + \mathbf{d}_B) = k \cdot \mathbf{d}_A + k \cdot \mathbf{d}_B \\
  \mathbf{d}((A / B)^k) &= k \cdot (\mathbf{d}_A - \mathbf{d}_B) = k \cdot \mathbf{d}_A - k \cdot \mathbf{d}_B
  \end{aligned}
```

  Scale Factor Transformation Invariant: For quantity $`A`$` with exact positive rational scale factor `$`c_A \in \mathbb{Q}^+`$` and additive offset `$`o_A \in \mathbb{Q}`$:

```math
  c(A^k) = \begin{cases} c_A^k, & \text{if } k \in \mathbb{Z} \\ \left( \frac{r}{s} \right)^p, & \text{if } k = \frac{p}{q} \land c_A = \frac{u}{v} \land (\exists r, s \in \mathbb{Z}^+ : r^q = u \land s^q = v) \\ \operatorname{Radical}(c_A, p, q), & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}
```

  Affine Exponentiation Rejection Predicate:

```math
  \Phi_{\text{affine\_pow\_valid}}(A, k) \iff (o_A = 0 \lor k = 1)
```

  Dimensioned Exponent Constant Rationality Predicate:

```math
  \Phi_{\text{exp\_valid}}(A, E_{\text{exp}}) \iff (\mathbf{d}_A = \mathbf{0} \lor E_{\text{exp}} \in \mathbb{Q})
```

  Deterministic Diagnostic Emission Invariant (REQ-0191): Let $`\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}`$ denote the preallocated diagnostic error sink buffer. Semantic violations of rational power and radical evaluation deterministically append structured diagnostic records:

```math
  \begin{aligned}
  \exists n = \langle \text{Pow}, A, \frac{p}{q} \rangle \in \mathcal{E} : q = 0 &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(n, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0300}, \text{RationalArithmeticDomainError}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  \exists n = \langle \text{Pow}, A, E_{\text{exp}} \rangle \in \mathcal{E} : \mathbf{d}_A \ne \mathbf{0} \land E_{\text{exp}} \notin \mathbb{Q} &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(n, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0300}, \text{NonRationalExponentOnDimensionedBase}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  \exists n = \langle \text{Pow}, A, k \rangle \in \mathcal{E} : o_A \ne 0 \land k \ne 1 &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(n, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0300}, \text{AffineUnitExponentiationError}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  \exists n = \langle \text{Pow}, A, k \rangle \in \mathcal{E} : \text{is\_const\_zero}(A) \land k \le 0 &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(n, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0300}, \text{CompileTimeZeroBaseNegativeExponent}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  \exists n = \langle \text{Pow}, A, \frac{p}{q} \rangle \in \mathcal{E} : \text{is\_const\_neg}(A) \land q \equiv 0 \pmod 2 &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(n, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0300}, \text{EvenRootOfNegativeQuantity}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  \exists i \in \{1, \dots, 7\} : (|a'_i| \gt 32767 \lor b'_i \gt 32767) &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(n, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0300}, \text{DimensionalExponentOverflow}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  |\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}| \ge C_{\text{max\_errors}} &\implies \exists D_{\text{term}} \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} : \text{code}(D_{\text{term}}) = \text{DiagnosticCode::E0199}
  \end{aligned}
```

  Deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 Anchor Invariant (Architectural Directive 1):

```math
  \forall n \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}, \quad \Delta_n = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(n))
```

#### 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

Rational power evaluation, fractional exponent multiplication, radical lowering, and affine unit validation execute strictly within Complexity Class $`\mathbf{P}`$.

  For an abstract MBSE compilation unit with $`N = |\mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}|`$` total AST nodes containing `$`K_{\text{pow}}`$ power and radical expressions:
  1. *Per-Coordinate Rational Exponent Multiplication*:
     - For each of the 7 coordinates in $`\mathbb{Q}^7`$`, computing `$`d'_i = k \cdot d_i = \frac{p \cdot a_i}{q \cdot b_i}`$` performs 2 integer multiplications (`$`p \cdot a_i`$` and `$`q \cdot b_i`$`) and 1 greatest common divisor reduction via the Euclidean algorithm `$`\gcd(|p \cdot a_i|, q \cdot b_i)`$.
     - For bounded 16-bit and 32-bit integer representations, the Euclidean algorithm terminates in at most $`2 \log_2(32767) \le 30`$` division steps, executing in deterministic `$`O(1)`$ constant time and requiring zero auxiliary allocations:

```math
T_{\text{coord\_eval}} \in O(1)
```

  2. *Per-Node Vector Exponentiation Complexity*:
     - Multiplying the entire 7-dimensional rational vector $`\mathbf{d}_A`$` by scalar `$`k = \frac{p}{q}`$ performs exactly 7 independent coordinate multiplications:

```math
T_{\text{node}} = 7 \cdot T_{\text{coord\_eval}} \in O(1)
```

  3. *Scale Factor & Radical Derivation*:
     - Evaluating scale factor exponentiation $`c_A^k`$` involves integer power or radical symbol creation, requiring deterministic `$`O(1)`$` time and `$`O(1)`$ auxiliary space.
  4. *AST Traversal & Aggregate Compilation Complexity*:
     - For an expression syntax tree $`E`$` with `$`|E|`$ AST nodes, bottom-up post-order traversal evaluates dimensions of leaves and sub-expressions in linear time.
     - Across an entire compilation unit containing $`N`$` total AST nodes and `$`K_{\text{pow}}`$ power expressions, total dimensional inference time satisfies:

```math
  T_{\text{total}}(N, K_{\text{pow}}) \in O(N + K_{\text{pow}}) \subseteq O(N)
```

  5. *Parallel Work-Stealing Scaling (Architectural Directive 2)*:
     - When dimensional inference across $`M`$` independent packages or compilation units is scheduled onto a bounded work-stealing thread pool with worker bound `$`N_{\text{max\_workers}}`$:

```math
  T_{\text{parallel}}(N, N_{\text{max\_workers}}) \in O\left( \frac{N}{N_{\text{max\_workers}}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}} \right)
```

  6. *Space Complexity & Memory Bounds*:
     - Contiguous bump arena allocation requires fixed-size AST descriptor storage bounded by $`O(N)`$ bytes.
     - Each rational 7-vector occupies $`7 \times 8 = 56`$ bytes (two 32-bit integers per coordinate).
     - The deduplicated string interner pool stores $`O(1)`$ unique function identifiers (`pow`, `sqrt`, `cbrt`, `root`) and synthesized radical unit symbol strings.
     - The preallocated diagnostic error sink buffer is strictly bounded by $`O(C_{\text{max\_errors}}) = O(1)`$.
