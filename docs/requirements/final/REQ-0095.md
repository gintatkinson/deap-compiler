### REQ-0095: SysML v2 Quantities Library: `Quantities` & Measurement Scale Lowering

#### 1. Normative Statement

The KerML and SysML v2 standard library lowering engine and metrology semantic type checker of Subsystem 4 shall bootstrap the normative SysML v2 `Quantities` standard library package, formalizing physical quantities as structured numerical magnitude-unit pairs and distinguishing measurement scales into ratio scales and interval scales within the core KerML classifier subtyping lattice $`(\mathcal{K}, \le_{\text{sub}})`$.

  The lowering engine shall strictly enforce the following structural, semantic, and typing invariants during quantities and measurement scale lowering:
  1. **Standard Library Bootstrapping & Metamodel Classifier Hierarchy**:
     - The compiler shall bootstrap the normative `Quantities` standard library package into the root compiler lexical scope and symbol table.
     - `QuantityValue` shall specialize KerML `DataType` and transitively KerML `Classifier` and `Type` ($`\text{QuantityValue} \le_{\text{sub}} \text{DataType} \le_{\text{sub}} \text{Classifier} \le_{\text{sub}} \text{Type}`$). Every `QuantityValue` shall represent an immutable structured value comprising a numerical magnitude value and a bound measurement unit.
     - `ScalarQuantityValue` shall specialize `QuantityValue` and represent a one-dimensional physical quantity.
     - `MeasurementScale` shall specialize KerML `DataType` as the abstract base classifier for all measurement scale definitions.
     - `RatioScale` and `IntervalScale` shall specialize `MeasurementScale` ($`\text{RatioScale} \le_{\text{sub}} \text{MeasurementScale}`$`, `$`\text{IntervalScale} \le_{\text{sub}} \text{MeasurementScale}`$) as mutually disjoint measurement scale kinds.
     - `Unit` shall specialize KerML `DataType`, representing a standardized reference measurement quantity with a coherent physical dimension mapping $`\text{dim}(u) \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$` and an associated measurement scale `$`S(u) \in \mathcal{MS}`$.
     - **Disjointness & Invalid Classifier Specialization**: `QuantityValue`, `MeasurementScale`, and `Unit` definitions are pure value types and must never specialize behavioral actions (`ActionDefinition`), structural parts (`PartDefinition`), or interface classifiers (`InterfaceDefinition`). Any declaration where a quantity or scale specializes an incompatible classifier kind shall be deterministically rejected at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0200` in accordance with REQ-0191.
  2. **Quantity Value Structure & Lexical Provenance**:
     - Every lowered `QuantityValue` AST node in contiguous arena storage shall record:
       - A numerical magnitude attribute $`v \in \mathbb{R}`$ (represented as an exact rational or double-precision floating-point value).
       - A unit reference attribute $`u \in \mathcal{U}`$ resolving to a valid `Unit` definition in lexical scope.
       - An associated measurement scale reference $`S \in \mathcal{MS}`$ characterizing the scale kind (`RatioScale` or `IntervalScale`).
       - An associated physical dimension tuple $`\mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$` inherited from unit `$`u`$.
     - Unresolved unit references or missing magnitude values in quantity expressions shall be rejected with diagnostic error code `E0200` (`UnresolvedUnitReference`).
  3. **Measurement Scale Semantics & Admissible Transformations**:
     - `RatioScale` Semantics:
       - Pertains to physical quantities possessing an absolute, natural zero origin ($`0 \cdot u = \mathbf{0}_{\text{abs}}`$), such that the zero value signifies complete absence of the physical quantity.
       - Invariant under the similarity transformation group $`\mathcal{G}_{\text{ratio}} = \{ \phi_m(v) = m \cdot v \mid m \in \mathbb{R}^+, \; m \gt 0 \}`$.
       - Admissible operations on ratio scale quantities:
         - Scalar scaling: $`k \cdot (v \cdot u) = (k \cdot v) \cdot u`$` for any scalar `$`k \in \mathbb{R}`$.
         - Dimensionless ratios: $`(v_1 \cdot u) / (v_2 \cdot u) = (v_1 / v_2) \in \mathbb{R}`$ between quantities of identical physical dimension.
         - Addition and subtraction: $`(v_1 \cdot u) \pm (v_2 \cdot u) = (v_1 \pm v_2) \cdot u`$ between ratio scale quantities of identical physical dimension.
         - Multiplication and division: $`(v_1 \cdot u_1) \cdot (v_2 \cdot u_2) = (v_1 \cdot v_2) \cdot (u_1 \cdot u_2)`$`, producing compound physical dimensions `$`\mathbf{d}_{\text{res}} = \mathbf{d}_1 + \mathbf{d}_2`$.
     - `IntervalScale` Semantics:
       - Pertains to physical quantities possessing an arbitrary, conventional reference origin ($`0 \cdot u \ne \mathbf{0}_{\text{abs}}`$), where zero does not represent the absence of the quantity.
       - Invariant under the one-dimensional positive affine transformation group $`\mathcal{G}_{\text{interval}} = \{ \psi_{m, c}(v) = m \cdot v + c \mid m \in \mathbb{R}^+, \; c \in \mathbb{R} \}`$`, where `$`m`$` represents the scaling factor and `$`c`$ represents origin translation.
       - Admissible operations on interval scale quantities:
         - Subtraction of two interval scale position quantities produces a ratio scale displacement delta: $`Q_1 - Q_2 = (v_1 - v_2) \cdot u_{\Delta}`$`, where `$`\text{Scale}(Q_1 - Q_2) \equiv \text{RatioScale}`$.
         - Addition of a ratio scale displacement delta to an interval scale position produces an interval scale position: $`Q_{\text{pos}} + Q_{\Delta} = (v_{\text{pos}} + v_{\Delta}) \cdot u`$`, where `$`\text{Scale}(Q_{\text{pos}} + Q_{\Delta}) \equiv \text{IntervalScale}`$.
         - Affine unit conversion between compatible interval scale units: $`v' = m_{1 \to 2} \cdot v + c_{1 \to 2}`$.
     - **Forbidden Interval Scale Operations**:
       - Direct addition of two interval scale position quantities ($`Q_1 + Q_2`$` for `$`Q_1, Q_2 \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{interval}}`$) is mathematically undefined and shall be rejected at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0200` (`InvalidIntervalScaleAddition`).
       - Direct scalar scaling of an interval scale position ($`k \cdot Q`$` for `$`Q \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{interval}}, k \ne 1`$) without an explicit origin reference shall be rejected with diagnostic error code `E0200` (`InvalidIntervalScaleScaling`).
       - Multiplication or division of two interval scale position quantities ($`Q_1 \cdot Q_2`$` or `$`Q_1 / Q_2`$` for `$`Q_1, Q_2 \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{interval}}`$) shall be rejected with diagnostic error code `E0200` (`InvalidIntervalScaleMultiplication`).
  4. **Dimensional Homogeneity Enforcement**:
     - For any binary additive operation $`Q_1 \pm Q_2`$`, the semantic type checker shall verify that the operand physical dimensions are strictly identical: `$`\text{dim}(Q_1) \equiv \text{dim}(Q_2)`$.
     - Any additive expression involving dimensionally heterogeneous operands shall be deterministically rejected at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`DimensionalHomogeneityViolation`) in accordance with REQ-0191.
  5. **Unit Conversion Lowering**:
     - For ratio scale quantities, unit conversion between coherent units $`u_1`$` and `$`u_2`$` possessing identical physical dimension `$`\text{dim}(u_1) \equiv \text{dim}(u_2)`$` shall be lowered to a linear multiplication by the exact rational scale factor: `$`v_2 = v_1 \cdot \sigma(u_1 \to u_2)`$.
     - For interval scale quantities, unit conversion between units $`u_1`$` and `$`u_2`$` possessing identical physical dimension `$`\text{dim}(u_1) \equiv \text{dim}(u_2)`$` shall be lowered to an affine transformation: `$`v_2 = m_{1 \to 2} \cdot v_1 + c_{1 \to 2}`$`, with exact conversion parameters `$`m_{1 \to 2} \in \mathbb{Q}^+`$` and `$`c_{1 \to 2} \in \mathbb{Q}`$.
     - Attempted unit conversion between units of differing physical dimensions ($`\text{dim}(u_1) \ne \text{dim}(u_2)`$) shall be rejected with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`IncompatibleUnitConversion`).
  6. **Architectural Directives Compliance**:
     - *Directive 1 (Deterministic UUIDv5 Anchors)*: Every lowered `QuantityValue`, `MeasurementScale`, `RatioScale`, `IntervalScale`, `Unit`, and unit conversion expression AST node shall be assigned an immutable deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 identifier derived from the canonical compiler namespace OID and its fully qualified topological path ($`\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(n))`$) in accordance with REQ-0010.
     - *Directive 2 (Bounded Work-Stealing Parallelism)*: Parallel lowering, AST node arena allocation, scale classification, and unit conversion validation of independent quantity expressions across distinct packages shall execute strictly on a bounded work-stealing thread pool with worker bound $`N_{\text{max\_workers}}`$ in accordance with REQ-0012.
     - *Directive 3 (String Interner Allocation Exception)*: String bytes representing unit names, symbol strings, scale names, quantity identifiers, and fully qualified paths shall be interned into the global deduplicated symbol interner pool during lexical analysis per REQ-0036 as the sole permitted exception to zero-copy memory management.
     - *Directive 4 (Synchronization Token Error Recovery)*: The lowering engine and semantic type checker shall implement synchronization token error recovery on statement delimiters (`;`) and block boundaries (`}`). Upon encountering malformed quantity expressions, invalid scale operations (`E0200`), or dimensional homogeneity violations (`E0300`), the engine shall record the diagnostic into the preallocated error sink buffer $`\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}`$`, substitute typed error sentinels `$`\bot_{\text{quantity}}`$`, and resynchronize on the next declaration delimiter without aborting compilation. If accumulated diagnostics reach `$`C_{\text{max\_errors}}`$, the compiler shall emit terminal diagnostic `E0199` and halt gracefully.
     - *Directive 5 (Panic-Free Release Isolation)*: Production compiler library code shall enforce a deterministic, non-panicking execution contract with total-function error handling across all valid and malformed inputs. Execution latency ($`\lt 25\text{ ms}`$`) and peak memory RSS (`$`\lt 100\text{ MB}`$` for `$`10^5`$ quantity expressions) performance gates shall be evaluated exclusively in compiled Release mode (`--release`) under dedicated benchmark harnesses isolated from CI logic tests in accordance with REQ-0011 and REQ-0196.

  All diagnostic messages, test models, and illustrative examples shall strictly employ synthetic abstract MBSE placeholders (`Package_0`, `Package_1`, `Classifier_Alpha`, `QuantityValue_Alpha`, `QuantityValue_Beta`, `RatioScale_Alpha`, `IntervalScale_Alpha`, `Unit_Alpha`, `Unit_Beta`, `param_x : Real`), strictly prohibiting hardcoded domain concepts, mock data, and non-ASCII dashes.

#### 2. Formal Invariant

Let $`\mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}`$` denote the universe of allocated AST nodes in contiguous bump arena storage. Let `$`\mathcal{QV} \subset \mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}`$` denote the set of quantity value nodes, `$`\mathcal{MS} \subset \mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}`$` the set of measurement scale definitions, and `$`\mathcal{U} \subset \mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}`$ the set of measurement unit definitions.

  Each quantity value $`QV \in \mathcal{QV}`$ is formalized as a 9-tuple:

```math
  QV = \langle \text{id}_{QV}, \text{name}_{QV}, \text{owner}_{QV}, v_{QV}, u_{QV}, S_{QV}, \mathbf{d}_{QV}, \Delta_{QV}, \text{span}_{QV} \rangle
```

  where:

```math
  \begin{aligned}
  \text{id}_{QV} &\in \mathbb{N}_{32} \quad (\text{contiguous arena node handle}) \\
  \text{name}_{QV} &\in \mathcal{S}_{\text{intern}} \cup \{ \bot_{\text{name}} \} \quad (\text{interned symbol identifier or anonymous literal}) \\
  \text{owner}_{QV} &\in \mathcal{K} \cup \mathcal{P}_{\text{pkg}} \quad (\text{owning classifier, feature, or package}) \\
  v_{QV} &\in \mathbb{R} \quad (\text{numerical magnitude value}) \\
  u_{QV} &\in \mathcal{U} \quad (\text{reference to measurement unit definition}) \\
  S_{QV} &\in \{ \text{RatioScale}, \text{IntervalScale} \} \quad (\text{measurement scale classification}) \\
  \mathbf{d}_{QV} &\in \mathbb{Q}^7 \quad (\text{physical dimension vector}) \\
  \Delta_{QV} &\in \{0, 1\}^{128} \quad (\text{deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 topological anchor}) \\
  \text{span}_{QV} &= \langle \text{file\_id}, p_{\text{start}}, p_{\text{end}}, \text{line}, \text{col} \rangle \quad (\text{source lexical provenance})
  \end{aligned}
```

  Each measurement scale $`MS \in \mathcal{MS}`$ is formalized as a 6-tuple:

```math
  MS = \langle \text{id}_{MS}, \text{name}_{MS}, \kappa_{MS}, \mathcal{G}_{MS}, \Delta_{MS}, \text{span}_{MS} \rangle
```

  where:

```math
  \begin{aligned}
  \text{id}_{MS} &\in \mathbb{N}_{32} \quad (\text{contiguous arena node handle}) \\
  \text{name}_{MS} &\in \mathcal{S}_{\text{intern}} \quad (\text{interned scale identifier}) \\
  \kappa_{MS} &\in \{ \text{Ratio}, \text{Interval} \} \quad (\text{scale kind discriminant}) \\
  \mathcal{G}_{MS} &\in \{ \mathcal{G}_{\text{ratio}}, \mathcal{G}_{\text{interval}} \} \quad (\text{transformation group invariance}) \\
  \Delta_{MS} &\in \{0, 1\}^{128} \quad (\text{deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 topological anchor}) \\
  \text{span}_{MS} &= \langle \text{file\_id}, p_{\text{start}}, p_{\text{end}}, \text{line}, \text{col} \rangle \quad (\text{source lexical provenance})
  \end{aligned}
```

  Each measurement unit $`U \in \mathcal{U}`$ is formalized as a 9-tuple:

```math
  U = \langle \text{id}_{U}, \text{name}_{U}, \text{symbol}_{U}, \mathbf{d}_{U}, S_{U}, \sigma_{U}, c_{U}, \Delta_{U}, \text{span}_{U} \rangle
```

  where:

```math
  \begin{aligned}
  \text{id}_{U} &\in \mathbb{N}_{32} \quad (\text{contiguous arena node handle}) \\
  \text{name}_{U} &\in \mathcal{S}_{\text{intern}} \quad (\text{interned unit name identifier}) \\
  \text{symbol}_{U} &\in \mathcal{S}_{\text{intern}} \quad (\text{interned unit symbol token}) \\
  \mathbf{d}_{U} &\in \mathbb{Q}^7 \quad (\text{physical dimension vector in } \mathbb{Q}^7) \\
  S_{U} &\in \mathcal{MS} \quad (\text{associated measurement scale handle}) \\
  \sigma_{U} &\in \mathbb{Q}^+ \quad (\text{multiplicative scale factor relative to coherent base unit}) \\
  c_{U} &\in \mathbb{Q} \quad (\text{additive origin offset relative to coherent base unit}) \\
  \Delta_{U} &\in \{0, 1\}^{128} \quad (\text{deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 topological anchor}) \\
  \text{span}_{U} &= \langle \text{file\_id}, p_{\text{start}}, p_{\text{end}}, \text{line}, \text{col} \rangle \quad (\text{source lexical provenance})
  \end{aligned}
```

  Metamodel Classifier Specialization Invariants:

```math
  \begin{aligned}
  \text{QuantityValue} &\le_{\text{sub}} \text{DataType} \le_{\text{sub}} \text{Classifier} \le_{\text{sub}} \text{Type} \\
  \text{MeasurementScale} &\le_{\text{sub}} \text{DataType} \le_{\text{sub}} \text{Classifier} \le_{\text{sub}} \text{Type} \\
  \text{RatioScale} &\le_{\text{sub}} \text{MeasurementScale} \\
  \text{IntervalScale} &\le_{\text{sub}} \text{MeasurementScale} \\
  \text{Unit} &\le_{\text{sub}} \text{DataType} \le_{\text{sub}} \text{Classifier} \le_{\text{sub}} \text{Type}
  \end{aligned}
```

  Disjointness Invariants:

```math
  \begin{aligned}
  \llbracket \text{RatioScale} \rrbracket \cap \llbracket \text{IntervalScale} \rrbracket &= \emptyset \\
  \llbracket \text{QuantityValue} \rrbracket \cap (\llbracket \text{ActionDef} \rrbracket \cup \llbracket \text{PartDef} \rrbracket \cup \llbracket \text{InterfaceDef} \rrbracket) &= \emptyset
  \end{aligned}
```

  Measurement Scale Transformation Invariants:

```math
  \begin{aligned}
  \mathcal{G}_{\text{ratio}} &= \{ \phi_m : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R} \mid \phi_m(v) = m \cdot v, \; m \in \mathbb{R}^+, \; m \gt 0 \} \\
  \mathcal{G}_{\text{interval}} &= \{ \psi_{m, c} : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R} \mid \psi_{m, c}(v) = m \cdot v + c, \; m \in \mathbb{R}^+, \; c \in \mathbb{R} \}
  \end{aligned}
```

  Ratio Scale Algebraic Operations Invariants:

```math
  \begin{aligned}
  k \cdot (v \cdot u) &= (k \cdot v) \cdot u \quad (\forall k \in \mathbb{R}) \\
  (v_1 \cdot u) \pm (v_2 \cdot u) &= (v_1 \pm v_2) \cdot u \quad (\text{where } \mathbf{d}(u_1) = \mathbf{d}(u_2)) \\
  (v_1 \cdot u_1) \cdot (v_2 \cdot u_2) &= (v_1 \cdot v_2) \cdot (u_1 \cdot u_2), \quad \mathbf{d}_{\text{res}} = \mathbf{d}_1 + \mathbf{d}_2 \\
  \frac{v_1 \cdot u_1}{v_2 \cdot u_2} &= \left(\frac{v_1}{v_2}\right) \cdot (u_1 / u_2), \quad \mathbf{d}_{\text{res}} = \mathbf{d}_1 - \mathbf{d}_2
  \end{aligned}
```

  Interval Scale Algebraic Operations Invariants:

```math
  \begin{aligned}
  Q_1, Q_2 \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{interval}} &\implies Q_1 - Q_2 = (v_1 - v_2) \cdot u_{\Delta} \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{ratio}} \\
  Q_{\text{pos}} \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{interval}}, \; Q_{\Delta} \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{ratio}} &\implies Q_{\text{pos}} + Q_{\Delta} = (v_{\text{pos}} + v_{\Delta}) \cdot u \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{interval}}
  \end{aligned}
```

  Affine Unit Conversion Invariant:

```math
  \mathcal{T}_{u_1 \to u_2}(v) = \frac{\sigma_{u_1}}{\sigma_{u_2}} \cdot v + \frac{c_{u_1} - c_{u_2}}{\sigma_{u_2}} \quad (\text{where } \mathbf{d}_{u_1} = \mathbf{d}_{u_2})
```

  Dimensional Homogeneity Invariant:

```math
  \forall Q_1, Q_2 \in \mathcal{QV}, \quad (Q_1 \pm Q_2) \implies \mathbf{d}(Q_1) \equiv \mathbf{d}(Q_2)
```

  Deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 Anchor Invariant (Architectural Directive 1):

```math
  \begin{aligned}
  \forall QV \in \mathcal{QV}, &\quad \Delta_{QV} = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(QV)) \\
  \forall MS \in \mathcal{MS}, &\quad \Delta_{MS} = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(MS)) \\
  \forall U \in \mathcal{U}, &\quad \Delta_{U} = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(U))
  \end{aligned}
```

  Diagnostic Trigger Rules: In accordance with REQ-0191, semantic and typing violations deterministically emit structured diagnostic records:

```math
  \begin{aligned}
  \exists Q_1, Q_2 \in \mathcal{QV} : \mathbf{d}(Q_1) \ne \mathbf{d}(Q_2) \wedge \text{Op}(Q_1, Q_2) \in \{ +, - \} &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(Q_1, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0300}, \text{DimensionalHomogeneityViolation}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  \exists Q_1, Q_2 \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{interval}} : \text{Op}(Q_1, Q_2) = + &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(Q_1, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0200}, \text{InvalidIntervalScaleAddition}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  \exists Q \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{interval}}, \; k \ne 1 : \text{Op}(k, Q) = \times &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(Q, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0200}, \text{InvalidIntervalScaleScaling}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  \exists Q_1, Q_2 \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{interval}} : \text{Op}(Q_1, Q_2) \in \{ \times, / \} &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(Q_1, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0200}, \text{InvalidIntervalScaleMultiplication}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  \exists U_1, U_2 \in \mathcal{U} : \mathbf{d}(U_1) \ne \mathbf{d}(U_2) \wedge \text{Convert}(U_1, U_2) &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(U_1, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0300}, \text{IncompatibleUnitConversion}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  \exists QV \in \mathcal{QV} : u_{QV} \notin \mathcal{U} &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(QV, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0200}, \text{UnresolvedUnitReference}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
  |\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}| \ge C_{\text{max\_errors}} &\implies \exists D_{\text{term}} \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} : \text{code}(D_{\text{term}}) = \text{DiagnosticCode::E0199}
  \end{aligned}
```

#### 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

Lowering, scale classification, unit conversion, and dimensional homogeneity validation of `Quantities` library elements execute strictly within Complexity Class $`\mathbf{P}`$.

  For an abstract MBSE model compilation unit containing $`N_Q = |\mathcal{QV}|`$` quantity instances, `$`N_U = |\mathcal{U}|`$` unit definitions, and `$`N_S = |\mathcal{MS}|`$ measurement scale definitions:
  1. *Quantity Node Construction & Arena Allocation*: Allocating a `QuantityValue` AST node in contiguous bump arena storage, interning symbol tokens, and computing its RFC 4122 UUIDv5 identifier requires amortized $`O(1)`$` time and `$`O(1)`$` auxiliary space per node, yielding `$`O(N_Q)`$ total allocation time.
  2. *Scale Classification & Invariant Verification*: Determining scale kind (`RatioScale` vs `IntervalScale`) checks the scale enum tag in $`O(1)`$ scalar operations.
  3. *Unit Conversion Evaluation*: Evaluating a multiplicative scale factor $`\sigma(u_1 \to u_2)`$` or an affine transformation `$`\mathcal{T}_{u_1 \to u_2}(v) = m_{1 \to 2} \cdot v + c_{1 \to 2}`$` executes in `$`O(1)`$ exact rational or floating-point operations.
  4. *Dimensional Homogeneity Check*: Comparing two 7D dimension vectors $`\mathbf{d}_1, \mathbf{d}_2 \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$` executes exactly 7 rational coordinate comparisons in `$`O(1)`$ operations.
  5. *Aggregate Compilation Unit Complexity*: The total sequential execution time for lowering, classifying, converting, and verifying all quantity elements in a compilation unit is strictly linear in the total number of metrology AST nodes:

```math
  T_{\text{total}}(N_Q, N_U, N_S) \in O(N_Q + N_U + N_S) \subseteq O(N_Q)
```

  6. *Parallel Work-Stealing Scaling (Architectural Directive 2)*: When independent quantity expressions across distinct packages are evaluated concurrently on a bounded work-stealing thread pool with worker bound $`N_{\text{max\_workers}}`$:

```math
  T_{\text{parallel}}(N_Q, N_{\text{max\_workers}}) \in O\left( \frac{N_Q}{N_{\text{max\_workers}}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}} \right)
```

  7. *Space Complexity & Memory Bounds*: Contiguous arena memory is bounded by $`O(N_Q + N_U + N_S)`$ bytes. Each lowered `QuantityValue` node occupies a compact fixed-size struct in contiguous storage. The preallocated diagnostic error sink buffer is strictly bounded by $`O(C_{\text{max\_errors}}) = O(1)`$ space.

#### 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria

Automated test suites shall deterministically verify compliance against synthetic abstract MBSE models (`Package_0`, `Package_1`, `Classifier_Alpha`, `QuantityValue_Alpha`, `QuantityValue_Beta`, `RatioScale_Alpha`, `IntervalScale_Alpha`, `Unit_Alpha`, `Unit_Beta`, `param_x : Real`) across the following verification gates:

  1. **Canonical `Quantities` Package Bootstrapping Gate**: Bootstrap the normative `Quantities` standard library package. Assert that `QuantityValue`, `MeasurementScale`, `RatioScale`, `IntervalScale`, and `Unit` definitions are lowered into contiguous bump arena storage, registered in the root lexical scope, and emit zero diagnostics.
  2. **Canonical `QuantityValue` Lowering Gate**: Lower synthetic quantity expression `QuantityValue_Alpha = 10.5 [Unit_Alpha]`. Assert that the lowered AST node specializes KerML `DataType`, contains magnitude $`10.5`$, binds unit `Unit_Alpha`, inherits its physical dimension $`\mathbf{d}_{\text{Unit\_Alpha}}`$, and assigns a valid RFC 4122 UUIDv5 identifier with zero diagnostics.
  3. **Ratio Scale Construction & Scalar Scaling Gate**: Declare ratio scale `RatioScale_Alpha` and unit `Unit_Alpha`. Evaluate scalar multiplication $`k \cdot Q`$` where `$`k = 2.5`$` and `$`Q = 10.0 \cdot \text{Unit\_Alpha}`$`. Assert that the resulting quantity magnitude is `$`25.0`$, unit remains `Unit_Alpha`, scale classification remains `RatioScale_Alpha`, and zero diagnostics are emitted.
  4. **Ratio Scale Addition & Homogeneity Gate**: Ingest addition of two ratio scale quantities $`Q_1 + Q_2`$ with identical unit `Unit_Alpha` and matching dimensions. Assert that the resulting quantity has magnitude $`v_1 + v_2`$, scale `RatioScale_Alpha`, and emits zero diagnostics.
  5. **Interval Scale Construction & Affine Transformation Gate**: Declare interval scale `IntervalScale_Alpha` with unit `Unit_Beta` and affine conversion parameters $`(m = 1.8, c = 32.0)`$ relative to reference unit `Unit_Alpha`. Assert that lowering affine conversion $`\mathcal{T}_{\text{Unit\_Alpha} \to \text{Unit\_Beta}}(100.0)`$` produces exact numerical value `$`212.0`$ with zero diagnostics.
  6. **Interval Scale Subtraction to Ratio Delta Gate**: Ingest subtraction of two interval scale positions $`Q_1 - Q_2`$` where `$`Q_1, Q_2 \in \text{IntervalScale\_Alpha}`$`. Assert that the resulting difference quantity `$`Q_{\Delta}`$` has magnitude `$`v_1 - v_2`$ and is strictly classified as `RatioScale` ($`Q_{\Delta} \in \mathcal{Q}_{\text{ratio}}`$).
  7. **Interval Scale Point-Delta Translation Gate**: Ingest addition of ratio delta $`Q_{\Delta}`$` to interval position `$`Q_{\text{pos}}`$. Assert that the resulting quantity is classified as `IntervalScale` with magnitude $`v_{\text{pos}} + v_{\Delta}`$ and zero diagnostics.
  8. **Invalid Interval Scale Addition Rejection Gate (`E0200`)**: Ingest a model attempting direct addition of two interval scale position quantities: `QuantityValue_Alpha + QuantityValue_Beta` where both belong to `IntervalScale_Alpha`. Assert deterministic compile-time rejection with diagnostic error code `E0200` (`InvalidIntervalScaleAddition`), binding sentinel $`\bot_{\text{quantity}}`$, and halting downstream verification without panicking.
  9. **Invalid Interval Scale Scaling Rejection Gate (`E0200`)**: Ingest a model attempting scalar multiplication of an interval scale position: $`2.0 \cdot \text{QuantityValue\_Alpha}`$ where `QuantityValue_Alpha` belongs to `IntervalScale_Alpha`. Assert deterministic compile-time rejection with diagnostic error code `E0200` (`InvalidIntervalScaleScaling`).
