## 1. Normative Statement

Subsystem 12 SHALL execute 4-pass semantic parity verification via `ParityVerifier_Alpha`.

Pipeline MUST enforce:
1. **Pass 1 (YAML Frontmatter)**: `FrontmatterPass_0` SHALL verify key equality ($\text{Keys}(\text{FM}_A)=\text{Keys}(\text{FM}_B)$) and value equivalence ($\text{FM}_A[k]\equiv\text{FM}_B[k]$). Divergence MUST emit `E0502`, binding $\bot_{\text{parity}}$.
2. **Pass 2 (Mermaid Topology)**: `TopologyPass_1` SHALL assert isomorphism ($\mathcal{G}_A\cong\mathcal{G}_B$) preserving labels ($\ell_V$), shapes, edges, and styles ($\ell_E$). Mismatches MUST emit `E0500`.
3. **Pass 3 (Table Congruence)**: `TablePass_2` SHALL assert shape equality ($R(T_A)=R(T_B)$, $C(T_A)=C(T_B)$), header normalization, and trimmed cell equivalence. Disparity MUST emit `E0502`.
4. **Pass 4 (Numerical Tolerances)**: `NumericPass_3` SHALL assert $\mathbb{Q}^7$ homogeneity, relative error bound $10^{-6}$ (floor $10^{-12}$), and absolute bound $10^{-9}$. Violations MUST emit `E0300`.
5. **Conjunctive Verification**: Predicate $\Phi$ SHALL evaluate strict conjunction $\Phi_{\text{Frontmatter}}\land\Phi_{\text{Topology}}\land\Phi_{\text{Table}}\land\Phi_{\text{Numeric}}$, recording failures in $\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}$.
6. **Directives**: UUIDv5 anchors (Directive 1); work-stealing $N_{\text{max\_workers}}$ (Directive 2); symbol interning (Directive 3); delimiter recovery halting at $C_{\text{max\_errors}}$ with `E0199` (Directive 4); Release budget ($< 25\text{ ms}$, $< 100\text{ MB}$) (Directive 5). Placeholders (`ParityVerifier_Alpha`, `FrontmatterPass_0`, `TopologyPass_1`, `TablePass_2`, `NumericPass_3`, `Package_0`, `Classifier_Alpha`, `Port_1`, `param_x : Real`) used.

## 2. Formal Invariant

The 4-pass conjunctive equivalence, tolerance thresholds, and diagnostic bindings satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\Phi(\text{Doc}_A,\text{Doc}_B)&=\Phi_{\text{Frontmatter}}\land\Phi_{\text{Topology}}\land\Phi_{\text{Table}}\land\Phi_{\text{Numeric}} \\
\Phi_{\text{Frontmatter}}&\iff\text{Keys}(\text{FM}_A)=\text{Keys}(\text{FM}_B)\land(\forall k\in\text{Keys}(\text{FM}_A),\;\text{FM}_A[k]\equiv\text{FM}_B[k]) \\
\Phi_{\text{Topology}}&\iff|\mathcal{M}_A|=|\mathcal{M}_B|\land(\forall m,\;\exists f:V_{A,m}\leftrightarrow V_{B,m}\text{ preserving }\ell_V,\ell_E,\text{ and edges}) \\
\Phi_{\text{Table}}&\iff|\mathcal{T}_A|=|\mathcal{T}_B|\land(\forall i,\;R(T_{A,i})=R(T_{B,i})\land C(T_{A,i})=C(T_{B,i})\land\text{CellEq}(T_{A,i},T_{B,i})) \\
\Phi_{\text{Numeric}}&\iff\forall(v_A,v_B)\in\mathcal{N}_A\times\mathcal{N}_B,\;\text{Dim}(v_A)=\text{Dim}(v_B)\land\text{TolEq}(v_A,v_B) \\
\text{TolEq}(v_A,v_B)&\iff\begin{cases}
|v_A-v_B|<10^{-9},&\text{if }|v_A|<10^{-12}\land|v_B|<10^{-12} \\
\frac{|v_A-v_B|}{\max(|v_A|,|v_B|,10^{-12})}<10^{-6},&\text{otherwise}
\end{cases} \\
\neg\Phi_{\text{Frontmatter}}\lor\neg\Phi_{\text{Table}}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0502})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land\text{Doc}\gets\bot_{\text{parity}} \\
\neg\Phi_{\text{Topology}}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0500})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land\text{Doc}\gets\bot_{\text{parity}} \\
\neg\Phi_{\text{Numeric}}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0300})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land\text{Doc}\gets\bot_{\text{parity}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
\Delta_{\text{parity}}&=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},p_A\mathbin{\Vert}\text{"::"}\mathbin{\Vert}p_B\mathbin{\Vert}\text{"::ParityRun"})
\end{aligned}
```

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$.
- **Asymptotic Bound:** Verification executes in $O(|V| + |E| + |T|)$ time and space; sorting takes $O(|V| \log |V| + |E| \log |E|)$; work-stealing scales as $O(N_{\text{total}} / N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})$.
- **Tractable Subclass and Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Avoids NP-1 (REQ-0117) and NP-8 (REQ-0197).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Full 4-Pass Semantic Parity Conformance
- **Given:** Documents `Doc_A`, `Doc_B` with identical frontmatter, graphs of `Package_0`, and tables.
- **When:** `ParityVerifier_Alpha` evaluates verification.
- **Then:** Predicate $\Phi(\text{Doc}_A, \text{Doc}_B)$ evaluates $\text{true}$ across all facets.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Boundary Numerical Tolerance Threshold and Absolute Floor
- **Given:** Pairs $v_A = 1.0000000$, $v_B = 1.0000005$ (error $5 \times 10^{-7} < 10^{-6}$), and $10^{-13}$, $2 \times 10^{-13}$ (difference $10^{-13} < 10^{-9}$).
- **When:** `NumericPass_3` evaluates tolerance relations.
- **Then:** Both pairs satisfy $\text{TolEq}$, yielding $\Phi_{\text{Numeric}} = \text{true}$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Rejection of Graph Topology Divergence and Dimension Mismatch
- **Given:** `Doc_B` with reversed edge `Classifier_Alpha --> Port_1` or incompatible $\mathbb{Q}^7$ dimensions.
- **When:** `TopologyPass_1` and `NumericPass_3` validate invariants.
- **Then:** Halts lowering, binds $\bot_{\text{parity}}$, emitting `E0500` or `E0300`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0500`, `E0300`.

### AC-04: Delimiter Error Recovery and Diagnostic Saturation Limit
- **Given:** 150 discrepancies across delimiter `|` and fence `---`.
- **When:** `ParityVerifier_Alpha` ingests and resynchronizes.
- **Then:** Accumulates up to $C_{\text{max\_errors}} = 100$, emitting terminal `E0199`, halting compilation.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0502`, `E0199`.

### AC-05: Bitwise Parity Anchor Determinism and Release Budget
- **Given:** 100 document pairs ($10^3$ nodes/cells) across $N_{\text{max\_workers}}$ threads.
- **When:** Benchmarking in Release mode (`--release`).
- **Then:** Anchors $\Delta_{\text{parity}}$ match bitwise; latency $< 25\text{ ms}$, RSS $< 100\text{ MB}$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.
