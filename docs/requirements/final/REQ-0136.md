### REQ-0136: STPA Combinatorial Unsafe Control Action Tensor Expansion & Pruning

- **Normative Statement**: The safety compiler, hazard analysis engine, and formal verification framework of Subsystem 7 shall execute a combinatorial tensor expansion $U = A \times G \times S_{\text{context}}$ across extracted control actions, universal System-Theoretic Process Analysis (STPA) guide words, and operational context states, evaluating candidate hazard contexts and pruning physically infeasible, inactive, or non-hazardous tuples using a centralized, bounded work-stealing worker pool ($P_{\text{workers}} \le \min(C_{\text{logical}}, N_{\text{max\_workers}})$) while strictly prohibiting unbounded dynamic thread allocation.

  Specifically, the STPA hazard expansion engine, context evaluator, and parallel scheduler shall strictly enforce the following structural, algorithmic, and operational invariants:
  1. **Control Action Extraction & Universe Formulation ($A$)**:
     - The compiler shall extract all directed control actions $A = \{ a_1, \dots, a_m \}$ from the Hierarchical Control Structure (HCS) model graph (REQ-0134) or behavioral action and port flow specifications (REQ-0038, REQ-0078).
     - Each control action $a \in A$ shall possess a unique qualified name, an originating controlling entity, an execution interface or port, and an intended controlled process target.
     - If the extracted control action universe $A$ is empty ($|A| = 0$) within a compilation unit requiring STPA safety verification, the compiler shall halt hazard expansion and emit diagnostic error code `E0400` (`EmptyControlActionSet`).
  2. **Universal STPA Guide Word Alphabet Formulation ($G$)**:
     - The compiler shall bind the universal four-element STPA guide word alphabet $G = \{ g_1, g_2, g_3, g_4 \}$:
       - $g_1 = \text{NotProviding}$: Control action is not provided in a hazardous context;
       - $g_2 = \text{Providing}$: Control action is provided in an unsafe context;
       - $g_3 = \text{TooEarlyTooLateWrongOrder}$: Control action is provided too early, too late, or in the wrong operational sequence;
       - $g_4 = \text{StoppedTooSoonAppliedTooLong}$: Control action duration is stopped too soon or applied too long, violating system envelope bounds.
     - The guide word alphabet cardinality shall remain strictly invariant: $|G| = 4$.
     - Declaring an unresolvable, non-standard, or malformed guide word shall trigger compile-time rejection with diagnostic error code `E0400` (`InvalidGuideWord`).
  3. **Operational Context State Universe Formulation ($S_{\text{context}}$)**:
     - The compiler shall extract and construct the operational context state universe $S_{\text{context}} = \{ s_1, \dots, s_p \}$ from discrete operational modes, environmental envelopes, lifecycle phases, and component states declared in the AST model.
     - Context states shall be represented as discrete valuation vectors $s = \langle v_1, \dots, v_k \rangle$ over finite state domains $D_1 \times \dots \times D_k$.
     - If any declared operational context state contains logically contradictory state predicates or mutually exclusive mode assignments, the compiler shall reject the context declaration with diagnostic error code `E0400` (`InconsistentContextState`).
  4. **Combinatorial Candidate Tensor Expansion ($U = A \times G \times S_{\text{context}}$)**:
     - The compiler shall synthesize the candidate Unsafe Control Action (UCA) tensor as the full Cartesian product:
       $$
       U = A \times G \times S_{\text{context}} = \{ (a, g, s) \mid a \in A, \, g \in G, \, s \in S_{\text{context}} \}
       $$
     - The candidate tensor cardinality shall satisfy the exact identity:
       $$
       |U| = |A| \cdot |G| \cdot |S_{\text{context}}| = 4 \cdot |A| \cdot |S_{\text{context}}|
       $$
     - Each candidate tuple $u = (a, g, s) \in U$ shall be indexed deterministically and assigned an immutable deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 identifier.
  5. **Context Evaluation & Pruning Filter ($\mathcal{U}_{\text{pruned}}$)**:
     - The compiler shall evaluate candidate tuples against three formal pruning predicates:
       - State Reachability ($\Phi_{\text{reach}}(s)$): Verifies whether context state $s$ is reachable from initial states under the state machine transition system (REQ-0123).
       - Action Activity ($\Phi_{\text{active}}(a, s)$): Verifies whether control action $a$ is enabled and executable within context $s$.
       - Hazard Causality ($\Phi_{\text{hazard}}(a, g, s)$): Evaluates whether applying guide word $g$ to action $a$ in context state $s$ triggers or contributes to an identified system hazard $H \in \mathcal{H}$.
     - Candidate tuples failing reachability ($\neg \Phi_{\text{reach}}(s)$) or failing activity ($\neg \Phi_{\text{active}}(a, s)$) shall be pruned from the active hazard tensor.
     - The resulting pruned unsafe control action set $\mathcal{U}_{\text{pruned}} \subseteq U$ shall consist strictly of those candidate tuples satisfying all three predicates:
       $$
       \mathcal{U}_{\text{pruned}} = \{ (a, g, s) \in U \mid \Phi_{\text{reach}}(s) \land \Phi_{\text{active}}(a, s) \land \Phi_{\text{hazard}}(a, g, s) \}
       $$
  6. **Bounded Work-Stealing Parallel Evaluation Engine (Architectural Directive 2)**:
     - Parallel evaluation of candidate hazard tuples $(a, g, s) \in U$ across worker threads shall execute exclusively through a centralized, bounded work-stealing thread pool (REQ-0012).
     - Worker thread allocation shall be strictly bounded by $P_{\text{workers}} = \min(C_{\text{logical}}, N_{\text{max\_workers}})$, where $C_{\text{logical}} \in \mathbb{N}_{\ge 1}$ is the detected host logical CPU core count and $N_{\text{max\_workers}} \in \mathbb{N}_{\ge 1}$ is a configurable runtime worker ceiling.
     - Unbounded, ad-hoc, or manual operating system thread creation is strictly prohibited. If any module attempts to spawn an unmanaged thread or if total active threads exceed $P_{\text{workers}} + 1$, the compiler runtime shall immediately halt and emit diagnostic error code `E0400` (`UnboundedThreadAllocationDetected`).
     - Worker threads must operate in complete memory isolation without shared mutable state across thread boundaries. Shared data (AST structures, context transition graphs, hazard rule tables) shall be accessed strictly via immutable references or frozen AST views.
     - Task workloads shall be partitioned into data-parallel chunks enqueued into worker-local double-ended deques, enabling lock-free local pop operations and work-stealing from remote deques when idle.
  7. **Deterministic Reduction & Bitwise Parity Invariant**:
     - Candidate tuple evaluation results gathered in task-private accumulators shall be merged into the global pruned UCA set $\mathcal{U}_{\text{pruned}}$ using a deterministic reduction operator.
     - The compiler shall sort all pruned UCAs into a canonical deterministic sequence based on tuple keys $(\text{FQN}(a), \text{index}(g), \text{FQN}(s))$.
     - Compilation across varying thread pool configurations ($P \in \{1, 2, 4, 8, 16\}$) and arbitrary scheduling interleavings shall produce 100% bitwise identical output models and hazard catalogs with zero diff churn. Any reduction divergence shall trigger diagnostic error code `E0400` (`NonDeterministicTensorReduction`).
  8. **The 5 Executive Architectural Directives Compliance**:
     - *Directive 1 (Deterministic UUIDv5 Anchors)*: Every candidate tuple $u \in U$, pruned UCA record in $\mathcal{U}_{\text{pruned}}$, and synthesized safety rule shall bind an immutable deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 identifier derived from the canonical DEAP namespace OID and tuple fully qualified path ($\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(a) \,\|\, \text{"::"} \,\|\, g \,\|\, \text{"::"} \,\|\, \text{FQN}(s))$) in accordance with REQ-0010. Time-based UUIDs are strictly banned.
     - *Directive 2 (Bounded Work-Stealing Parallelism)*: Multithreaded execution for STPA tensor expansion and context evaluation shall be implemented exclusively via a bounded work-stealing thread pool with worker ceiling $N_{\text{max\_workers}}$ in accordance with REQ-0012.
     - *Directive 3 (String Interner Memory Allocation Exception)*: Control action names, guide word tokens, context state identifiers, and hazard labels shall be interned into the deduplicated global symbol interner pool during lexical analysis per REQ-0036 as the sole permitted exception to zero-copy memory management.
     - *Directive 4 (Synchronization Token Error Recovery)*: The safety compiler shall implement synchronization token error recovery on statement delimiters (`;`) and block boundaries (`}`). When semantic violations occur (`E0400`), diagnostics shall accumulate in the error sink buffer $\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}$, substitute typed error sentinels ($\bot_{\text{uca}}$), and continue validating subsequent declarations. If accumulated diagnostics reach capacity $C_{\text{max\_errors}}$, terminal diagnostic `E0199` shall be emitted and compilation halted gracefully in accordance with REQ-0013 and REQ-0016.
     - *Directive 5 (Panic-Free Release Paths & Benchmarking Isolation)*: The tensor expansion engine shall strictly enforce a deterministic, non-panicking execution contract with total-function error handling. Verification latency ($\lt 25\text{ ms}$ for $10^4$ candidate UCA tuples) and peak RSS memory ($\lt 100\text{ MB}$) performance gates shall be evaluated exclusively in compiled Release mode (`--release`) under dedicated benchmark harnesses isolated from standard CI logic tests in accordance with REQ-0011 and REQ-0196.

  All diagnostic messages, test models, and illustrative examples shall strictly employ synthetic abstract MBSE placeholders (`Package_0`, `Package_1`, `Controller_0`, `Controller_1`, `Process_0`, `Action_0`, `Action_1`, `State_0`, `State_1`, `Context_0`, `Context_1`, `Hazard_0`, `Hazard_1`, `Invariant_0`, `Port_1`, `Flow_A`, `param_x : Real`), strictly prohibiting hardcoded domain concepts, mock data, and non-ASCII dashes.

  $$
  \mathcal{M}_{\text{STPA}} = \langle A, G, S_{\text{context}}, U, \Phi_{\text{reach}}, \Phi_{\text{active}}, \Phi_{\text{hazard}}, \mathcal{U}_{\text{pruned}}, P_{\text{workers}} \rangle
  $$

  where:

  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  A &\subset \mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}} \quad (\text{finite set of control actions}, \, |A| = m \ge 1) \\
  G &= \{ g_1, g_2, g_3, g_4 \} \quad (\text{universal STPA guide word alphabet}, \, |G| = 4) \\
  S_{\text{context}} &\subset \mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}} \quad (\text{finite set of operational context states}, \, |S_{\text{context}}| = p \ge 1) \\
  U &= A \times G \times S_{\text{context}} \quad (\text{combinatorial candidate UCA tensor}) \\
  \Phi_{\text{reach}} &: S_{\text{context}} \to \{ \text{true}, \text{false} \} \quad (\text{context state reachability predicate}) \\
  \Phi_{\text{active}} &: A \times S_{\text{context}} \to \{ \text{true}, \text{false} \} \quad (\text{action activity predicate}) \\
  \Phi_{\text{hazard}} &: A \times G \times S_{\text{context}} \to \{ \text{true}, \text{false} \} \quad (\text{hazard causality predicate}) \\
  \mathcal{U}_{\text{pruned}} &\subseteq U \quad (\text{pruned active unsafe control action set}) \\
  P_{\text{workers}} &\quad (\text{bounded work-stealing worker thread pool})
  \end{aligned}
  $$

  $$
  \mathcal{U}_{\text{pruned}} = \{ (a, g, s) \in U \mid \Phi_{\text{reach}}(s) \land \Phi_{\text{active}}(a, s) \land \Phi_{\text{hazard}}(a, g, s) \}

  5. Bounded Work-Stealing Parallel Execution Invariant (Directive 2):
  Let $C_{\text{logical}}$ denote the detected logical CPU core count and $N_{\text{max\_workers}}$ denote the configured maximum worker thread limit. The active worker thread pool cardinality satisfies the strict bounded ceiling:

  $$
  |P_{\text{workers}}| = N_{\text{workers}} = \min(C_{\text{logical}}, N_{\text{max\_workers}}) \le N_{\text{max\_workers}}
  $$

  Let $\mathcal{T}_{\text{spawned}}$ denote the set of all operating system threads instantiated by the compiler execution process, and let $N_{\text{coord}} \in \{0, 1\}$ denote the master coordination thread. The total spawned thread invariant enforces:

  $$
  |\mathcal{T}_{\text{spawned}}| \le |P_{\text{workers}}| + N_{\text{coord}}
  $$

  Every candidate tuple $u = (a, g, s) \in U$ is dispatched onto the bounded worker pool for parallel evaluation:

  $$
  \forall u \in U, \quad \text{EvaluateContext}(u) \implies \text{WorkerPoolExecution}(P_{\text{workers}})
  $$

  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  \text{LocalExecution}(\tau_i) &: \quad Q_i \ne \emptyset \implies \text{PopBottom}(Q_i) \\
  \text{StealRule}(\tau_i) &: \quad Q_i = \emptyset \implies \text{StealTop}(Q_j), \quad j \ne i, \; Q_j \ne \emptyset
  \end{aligned}
  $$

  6. Thread Memory Isolation Invariant:
  Let $\text{Memory}(\tau)$ denote the set of memory addresses accessed by worker thread $\tau \in P_{\text{workers}}$. Concurrency safety enforces pairwise disjointness for all distinct concurrent workers $\tau_i \ne \tau_j \in P_{\text{workers}}$:

  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  \text{Writes}(\tau_i) \cap \text{Writes}(\tau_j) &= \emptyset \\
  \text{Writes}(\tau_i) \cap \text{Reads}(\tau_j) &= \emptyset \\
  \text{Reads}(\tau_i) \cap \text{Writes}(\tau_j) &= \emptyset
  \end{aligned}
  $$

  yielding zero shared mutable state across the thread pool:

  $$
  \text{SharedMutableState}(P_{\text{workers}}) = \bigcup_{\tau_i \ne \tau_j} \left( \text{Writes}(\tau_i) \cap \text{Memory}(\tau_j) \right) = \emptyset
  $$

- **Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds**: Combinatorial tensor expansion, context evaluation, pruning, and canonical sorting reside strictly in **Complexity Class P** (Deterministic Polynomial Time).

  **Mathematical Delineation from Algorithmic Bound NP-4**:
  The STPA analysis pipeline partitions into two distinct algorithmic phases:
  1. *Candidate UCA Tensor Expansion & Pruning (REQ-0136)*:
     REQ-0136 executes the Cartesian product expansion $U = A \times G \times S_{\text{context}}$, evaluates individual candidate tuples against deterministic reachability and hazard predicates, prunes invalid tuples, and sorts the resulting set $\mathcal{U}_{\text{pruned}}$. Because evaluating $\Phi_{\text{reach}}$, $\Phi_{\text{active}}$, and $\Phi_{\text{hazard}}$ for each tuple involves constant-time table lookups or polynomial statechart checks without combinatorial branching, REQ-0136 executes strictly in deterministic polynomial time ($O(|A| \cdot |S_{\text{context}}| \log(|A| \cdot |S_{\text{context}}|))$).
  2. *Minimal Hazard Cut-Sets & UCA Minimization (Algorithmic Bound NP-4, REQ-0135)*:
     In contrast, once candidate UCAs and hazard scenarios $\mathcal{H}$ are identified, selecting a minimal-cardinality subset of safety constraints $C^* \subseteq \mathcal{C}$ that mitigates every hazard scenario ($\forall H_i \in \mathcal{H}, \, C^* \cap H_i \ne \emptyset$) is isomorphic to the Minimal Hitting Set / Set Cover problem, which is **NP-complete**. While REQ-0135 employs greedy logarithmic approximations or bounded Min-Unsat solvers with cutoff timeout $\kappa_{\text{safety}}$, REQ-0136 remains strictly in Complexity Class $\mathbf{P}$.

  **Asymptotic Complexity Analysis**:
  Let $m = |A|$ denote the number of control actions, let $g = |G| = 4$ denote the fixed guide word alphabet size, let $p = |S_{\text{context}}|$ denote the number of operational context states, and let $N = |U| = 4 \cdot m \cdot p$ denote total candidate tuples in tensor $U$.
  1. *Tensor Product Generation*:
     Iterating through $A \times G \times S_{\text{context}}$ to allocate candidate tuple headers in contiguous arena storage requires:
     $$
     T_{\text{expand}}(m, p) \in O(m \cdot p)
     $$
  2. *Parallel Context Evaluation Work ($T_1$)*:
     Evaluating predicates $\Phi_{\text{reach}}(s)$, $\Phi_{\text{active}}(a, s)$, and $\Phi_{\text{hazard}}(a, g, s)$ on each tuple $u \in U$ requires $O(1)$ operations via pre-indexed state reachability bitsets and rule lookup maps. Total sequential work is:
     $$
     T_1(m, p) \in O(m \cdot p)
     $$
  3. *Bounded Work-Stealing Scheduling Bounds (Directive 2)*:
     With $P = |P_{\text{workers}}| = \min(C_{\text{logical}}, N_{\text{max\_workers}})$ worker threads, the bounded work-stealing scheduler achieves an expected parallel execution time of:
     $$
     T_P \le \frac{T_1}{P} + O(T_\infty) \in O\left( \frac{m \cdot p}{P} + \log(m \cdot p) \right)
     $$
     where the critical-path span $T_\infty \in O(\log(m \cdot p))$ bounds task deque decomposition, work-stealing attempts, and parallel reduction. Local deque push/pop operations execute in $O(1)$ time, and atomic work-stealing attempts are bounded by $O(P \cdot T_\infty)$ with high probability.
  4. *Canonical Deterministic Sorting*:
     Sorting the retained pruned set $\mathcal{U}_{\text{pruned}}$ of cardinality $K \le 4 \cdot m \cdot p$ by lexicographical key requires:
     $$
     T_{\text{sort}}(K) \in O(K \log K) \subseteq O((m \cdot p) \log(m \cdot p))
     $$
  5. *Aggregate Time Complexity*:
     Summing tensor generation, parallel evaluation, and canonical sorting:
     $$
     T_{\text{total}}(m, p) \in O((m \cdot p) \log(m \cdot p))
     $$
     The algorithm is quasi-linear in candidate tensor cardinality, operating deterministically without backtracking or recursion stack overflow.
  6. *Space Complexity & Arena Memory Bounds*:
     Candidate and pruned UCA tuples are allocated within contiguous bump arena memory, consuming $O(m \cdot p)$ bytes. Worker-local task deques and thread accumulators consume $O(P \cdot S_1) \in O(P \cdot \log(m \cdot p))$ auxiliary space, strictly bounded by the worker ceiling $N_{\text{max\_workers}}$, guaranteeing bounded memory footprint.
