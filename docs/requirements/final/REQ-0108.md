### REQ-0108: Generalized Kirchhoff Flow Law ($`\sum \text{Through} = 0`$) at Conservative Junctions

#### 1. Normative Statement

The physical metrology, abstract flow conservation engine, and network constraint synthesis pipeline of Subsystem 5 shall synthesize generalized Kirchhoff Flow Law (KFL) continuity equations across all conservative junction nodes within abstract MBSE physical flow networks. The constraint synthesis engine shall mandate that the algebraic sum of all through variables (flow rates, fluxes, generalized currents) entering and exiting each conservative topological junction node sums to zero identically at every instant of time for ideal conservative junctions, or equals the exact time rate of change of internal stored state for dynamic accumulating storage junctions.

  Specifically, the metrology engine, network solver, and constraint synthesis pipeline shall strictly enforce the following structural, algebraic, topological, and diagnostic invariants:
  1. **Abstract Flow Conservation Network Topology & Junction Definition**:
     - The physical flow network shall be modeled as a directed topological multigraph $`\mathcal{G}_{\text{flow}} = (\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}, \mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}})`$`, where `$`\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}`$` denotes the set of `$`N_V = |\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}|`$` junction nodes and `$`\mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}`$` denotes the set of `$`N_E = |\mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}|`$ directed flow branches.
     - Each conservative junction node $`J \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}`$ shall represent a 0-dimensional topological interface where two or more conservative flow branches connect.
     - Each directed flow branch $`e = (u, v) \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}`$` shall couple a source junction node `$`u \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}`$` to a target junction node `$`v \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}`$`, carrying a through variable `$`f_e(t)`$` (flow rate / flux) and an across variable `$`\Delta e_e(t) = e_u(t) - e_v(t)`$ (effort / potential difference).
     - The incidence orientation of branch $`e`$` relative to junction `$`J_i`$` shall be defined by the incidence coefficient `$`\sigma_{i, e} \in \{-1, 0, +1\}`$`, where `$`\sigma_{i, e} = +1`$` if branch `$`e`$` enters junction `$`J_i`$` (incoming flow), `$`\sigma_{i, e} = -1`$` if branch `$`e`$` exits junction `$`J_i`$` (outgoing flow), and `$`\sigma_{i, e} = 0`$` if branch `$`e`$` is not incident upon junction `$`J_i`$.
  2. **Generalized Kirchhoff Flow Law (Flow Continuity Formulation)**:
     - For every ideal conservative junction node $`J_i \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}`$` without dynamic internal accumulation (`$`\frac{d S_i}{dt} = 0`$`), the constraint engine shall synthesize a flow continuity equation requiring the oriented algebraic sum of all incident through variables to equal zero identically at every time instant `$`t \ge 0`$:

```math
\sum_{e \in \text{Incident}(J_i)} \sigma_{i, e} \cdot f_e(t) = 0, \quad \forall t \ge 0
```

     - In global network matrix formulation, the continuity constraints across all ideal junctions shall satisfy the linear algebraic relation:

```math
\mathbf{A}_{\text{ideal}} \mathbf{f}(t) = \mathbf{0}
```

       where $`\mathbf{A} \in \{-1, 0, +1\}^{N_V \times N_E}`$` is the network node-branch incidence matrix and `$`\mathbf{f}(t) = [f_1(t), \dots, f_{N_E}(t)]^T`$ is the column vector of branch through variables.
  3. **Dynamic Capacitive Storage & Accumulation Junctions**:
     - For conservative junction nodes designated with internal dynamic accumulation or storage capacity (where $`S_i(t)`$ represents the accumulated conserved quantity, such as generalized fluid volume, electric charge, mass, or thermal energy), the constraint engine shall synthesize the generalized continuity equation balancing incident net flow with the time rate of internal storage:

```math
\sum_{e \in \text{Incident}(J_i)} \sigma_{i, e} \cdot f_e(t) = \frac{d S_i}{dt}(t), \quad \forall t \ge 0
```

     - The accumulated quantity $`S_i(t)`$` shall possess a physical dimension vector in `$`\mathbb{Q}^7`$` that satisfies `$`\mathbf{d}(S_i) = \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}} + [T]^1`$`, ensuring that the time derivative `$`\frac{d S_i}{dt}`$` possesses the exact physical dimension of the through variable: `$`\mathbf{d}\left( \frac{d S_i}{dt} \right) \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}`$.
  4. **Through Variable Dimensional Homogeneity Invariant**:
     - All through variables $`f_e`$` incident upon a common conservative junction node `$`J_i`$` must share an identically equal physical dimension vector in the 7-dimensional rational vector space `$`\mathbb{Q}^7`$ over the International System of Quantities (ISQ) base dimensions:

```math
\forall e_a, e_b \in \text{Incident}(J_i), \quad \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(e_a) \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(e_b)
```

     - Any conservative junction connecting branches with mismatched through dimensions (such as attempting to connect an electric current branch $`[I]^1`$` with a volumetric flow branch `$`[L]^3 [T]^{-1}`$` or mass flow branch `$`[M]^1 [T]^{-1}`$) shall be deterministically rejected at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`DimensionalMismatch`) in accordance with REQ-0191.
  5. **Commensurability Verification & Exact Rational Unit Scaling**:
     - When incident flow branches share identical physical dimension vectors ($`\mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(e_a) \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(e_b)`$`) but are declared using disparate measurement units `$`u_a \ne u_b`$ (e.g., litres per second vs cubic metres per second), the compiler shall verify unit commensurability.
     - The constraint engine shall automatically scale each through variable by its exact rational conversion factor $`c_e \in \mathbb{Q}^+`$ relative to the coherent SI base unit, synthesizing the normalized flow continuity equation:

```math
\sum_{e \in \text{Incident}(J_i)} \sigma_{i, e} \cdot c_e \cdot f_e(t) = 0
```

     - Floating-point scale factors in unit conversion are strictly prohibited; conversions shall be computed using irreducible rational numbers $`\frac{p}{q}`$` (`$`p \in \mathbb{Z}^+`$`, `$`q \in \mathbb{Z}^+`$`, `$`\gcd(p, q) = 1`$).
  6. **Topological Rank Defect, Connected Components & Datum Designation**:
     - For any connected flow network component $`\mathcal{K}_c = (\mathcal{V}_c, \mathcal{E}_c) \subseteq \mathcal{G}_{\text{flow}}`$`, the algebraic sum of all rows in the incidence submatrix `$`\mathbf{A}_c`$` equals the zero vector (`$`\sum_{i \in \mathcal{V}_c} A_{i, e} = 0`$`), establishing a rank defect of exactly 1 per connected component: `$`\text{rank}(\mathbf{A}_c) = |\mathcal{V}_c| - 1`$.
     - The network solver shall partition the network multigraph into its $`|\mathcal{K}|`$` connected components, designate exactly one datum/reference junction node `$`J_{\text{datum}, c} \in \mathcal{V}_c`$` per component, and emit exactly `$`|\mathcal{V}_c| - 1`$ linearly independent continuity equations.
     - The redundant continuity equation for the datum node shall be eliminated from the active linear solver system to ensure non-singularity and deterministic matrix solvability.
  7. **Static Flow Verification & Compile-Time Conservation Checking**:
     - For networks with statically declared constant through flow rates or fixed boundary conditions, the compiler shall evaluate Kirchhoff flow continuity at compile time.
     - Any conservative junction exhibiting a non-zero static algebraic flow imbalance ($`|\sum \sigma_{i, e} c_e f_e| \gt 0`$) without an internal accumulation term shall be deterministically rejected at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0301` (`KirchhoffConservationViolation`).
     - Conservative junctions with degree 0 (isolated junction) or degree 1 (dead-end branch with non-zero through flow) shall be flagged with diagnostic error code `E0220` (`UnconnectedInterface`) or `E0301` (`KirchhoffConservationViolation`).
  8. **Dynamic Symbolic Constraint AST Synthesis**:
     - For dynamic and parametric models where through flows are symbolic time-varying functions, state expressions, or governed by constitutive element laws ($`f_e = g(\Delta e_e)`$), the engine shall synthesize formal equality constraint AST nodes representing the Kirchhoff flow continuity equations.
     - Synthesized constraint expressions shall be lowered into the intermediate representation (LUMI IR SSA basic blocks, SMT-LIB2 verification assertions per REQ-0188, and continuous simulation solver equation systems per REQ-0179).
  9. **Architectural Directives Compliance**:
     - *Directive 1 (Deterministic UUIDv5 Anchors)*: Every lowered junction node, flow branch handle, and synthesized continuity constraint AST node shall be assigned an immutable deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 identifier hashed from the canonical DEAP namespace OID and the node fully qualified topological path ($`\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(n))`$) in accordance with REQ-0010.
     - *Directive 2 (Bounded Work-Stealing Parallelism)*: Parallel graph traversal, incidence matrix construction, dimensional verification, and constraint equation synthesis across independent packages and network subgraphs shall execute on a bounded work-stealing thread pool with worker bound $`N_{\text{max\_workers}}`$ in accordance with REQ-0012.
     - *Directive 3 (String Interner Allocation Exception)*: Junction identifiers, branch names, port symbols, and quantity unit tokens shall be interned into the deduplicated global symbol interner pool during lexical analysis per REQ-0036 as the sole permitted exception to zero-copy memory management.
     - *Directive 4 (Synchronization Token Error Recovery)*: The parser and network validator shall implement synchronization token error recovery on statement delimiters (`;`) and block boundaries (`}`). When semantic violations occur (`E0300`, `E0301`), diagnostics shall accumulate in the error sink buffer $`\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}`$`, substitute typed error sentinels (`$`\bot_{\text{junc}}`$`, `$`\bot_{\text{flow}}`$`, `$`\bot_{\text{dim}}`$`), and continue auditing subsequent junctions. If accumulated diagnostics reach `$`C_{\text{max\_errors}}`$, terminal diagnostic `E0199` shall be emitted and compilation halted gracefully in accordance with REQ-0013 and REQ-0016.
     - *Directive 5 (Panic-Free Release Isolation)*: The flow conservation pipeline shall enforce a deterministic, non-panicking execution contract with total-function error handling. Performance latency ($`\lt 25\text{ ms}`$`) and peak RSS memory (`$`\lt 100\text{ MB}`$` for `$`10^5`$ network junctions) shall be evaluated exclusively in compiled Release mode (`--release`) under dedicated benchmark harnesses isolated from CI logic tests in accordance with REQ-0011 and REQ-0196.

  All diagnostic messages, test models, and illustrative examples shall strictly employ synthetic abstract MBSE placeholders (`Package_0`, `Package_1`, `Classifier_Alpha`, `Junction_Alpha`, `Junction_Beta`, `FlowBranch_1`, `FlowBranch_2`, `Port_1`, `Port_2`, `param_x : Real`, `Quantity_1`, `Network_Alpha`), strictly prohibiting hardcoded domain concepts, mock data, and non-ASCII dashes.

#### 2. Formal Invariant

Let $`\mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}`$ denote the universe of allocated AST nodes in contiguous bump arena storage.

  The 7-dimensional physical quantity dimension space is formalized as a rational vector space over the field $`\mathbb{Q}`$:

```math
\mathbb{Q}^7 = \{ \mathbf{d} = (\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \epsilon, \zeta, \eta) \mid \alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \epsilon, \zeta, \eta \in \mathbb{Q} \}
```

  where coordinate positions correspond to the canonical ISQ basis $`\mathcal{B} = \{ [M], [L], [T], [I], [\Theta], [N], [J] \}`$.

  An abstract physical flow conservation network is formalized as a directed multigraph:

```math
\mathcal{G}_{\text{flow}} = (\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}, \mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}})
```

  where:

```math
\begin{aligned}
|\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}| &= N_V \quad (\text{number of junction nodes}) \\
|\mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}| &= N_E \quad (\text{number of directed flow branches})
\end{aligned}
```

  Each junction node $`J \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}`$ is formalized as an 8-tuple:

```math
J = \langle \text{id}_J, \text{name}_J, \text{kind}_J, \text{Incident}(J), \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}, S_J, \Delta_J, \text{span}_J \rangle
```

  where:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\text{id}_J &\in \mathbb{N}_{32} \quad (\text{contiguous arena node handle}) \\
\text{name}_J &\in \mathcal{S}_{\text{intern}} \quad (\text{interned junction name identifier}) \\
\text{kind}_J &\in \{ \text{IdealConservative}, \text{AccumulatingStorage} \} \\
\text{Incident}(J) &\subseteq \mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}} \quad (\text{set of incident directed flow branches}) \\
\mathbf{d}_{\text{through}} &\in \mathbb{Q}^7 \cup \{ \bot_{\text{dim}} \} \quad (\text{inferred physical dimension of through variable}) \\
S_J &\in \mathcal{S}_{\text{state}} \cup \{ \bot_{\text{state}} \} \quad (\text{internal dynamic storage state variable}) \\
\Delta_J &\in \{0, 1\}^{128} \quad (\text{deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 topological anchor}) \\
\text{span}_J &= \langle \text{file\_id}, p_{\text{start}}, p_{\text{end}}, \text{line}, \text{col} \rangle \quad (\text{source lexical provenance})
\end{aligned}
```

  Each directed flow branch $`e \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}`$ is formalized as a 10-tuple:

```math
e = \langle \text{id}_e, u_e, v_e, f_e, \Delta e_e, \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}, \mathbf{d}_{\text{across}}, c_e, \Delta_e, \text{span}_e \rangle
```

  where:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\text{id}_e &\in \mathbb{N}_{32} \quad (\text{contiguous arena branch handle}) \\
u_e, v_e &\in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}} \quad (\text{source and target junction handles}) \\
f_e &\in \mathcal{F}_{\text{var}} \quad (\text{branch through variable / flow rate}) \\
\Delta e_e &\in \mathcal{E}_{\text{var}} \quad (\text{branch across variable / potential difference}) \\
\mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}, \mathbf{d}_{\text{across}} &\in \mathbb{Q}^7 \quad (\text{conjugate physical dimension vectors}) \\
c_e &\in \mathbb{Q}^+ \quad (\text{exact rational unit conversion scale factor to coherent SI base}) \\
\Delta_e &\in \{0, 1\}^{128} \quad (\text{deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 topological anchor}) \\
\text{span}_e &= \langle \text{file\_id}, p_{\text{start}}, p_{\text{end}}, \text{line}, \text{col} \rangle \quad (\text{source lexical provenance})
\end{aligned}
```

  The node-branch incidence matrix $`\mathbf{A} \in \{-1, 0, +1\}^{N_V \times N_E}`$ is defined by:

```math
A_{i, e} = \begin{cases} +1, & \text{if branch } e \text{ enters junction } J_i \\ -1, & \text{if branch } e \text{ leaves junction } J_i \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}
```

  Generalized Kirchhoff Flow Law (KFL / Flow Continuity Invariant) for Ideal Conservative Junctions:

```math
\forall J_i \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}} \text{ with } \text{kind}(J_i) = \text{IdealConservative}, \quad \sum_{e \in \text{Incident}(J_i)} A_{i, e} \cdot c_e \cdot f_e(t) = 0, \quad \forall t \ge 0
```

  In global matrix notation:

```math
\mathbf{A}_{\text{ideal}} \mathbf{C}_{\text{unit}} \mathbf{f}(t) = \mathbf{0}
```

  where $`\mathbf{C}_{\text{unit}} = \operatorname{diag}(c_1, \dots, c_{N_E})`$ is the diagonal matrix of exact rational unit conversion scale factors.

  Generalized Kirchhoff Flow Law for Dynamic Accumulating Storage Junctions:

```math
\forall J_i \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}} \text{ with } \text{kind}(J_i) = \text{AccumulatingStorage}, \quad \sum_{e \in \text{Incident}(J_i)} A_{i, e} \cdot c_e \cdot f_e(t) = \frac{d S_i}{dt}(t), \quad \forall t \ge 0
```

  Dimensional Homogeneity Invariants across Conservative Junctions:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\forall J_i \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}, \; \forall e_1, e_2 \in \text{Incident}(J_i) &\implies \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(e_1) \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(e_2) \\
\forall J_i \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}} \text{ with } \text{kind}(J_i) = \text{AccumulatingStorage} &\implies \mathbf{d}\left( \frac{d S_i}{dt} \right) \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(J_i)
\end{aligned}
```

  Topological Rank Defect & Datum Elimination Invariant: Let $`\mathcal{K}_1, \dots, \mathcal{K}_p`$` denote the connected components of `$`\mathcal{G}_{\text{flow}}`$:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\forall c \in \{1, \dots, p\}, \quad &\sum_{i \in \mathcal{V}(\mathcal{K}_c)} A_{i, e} = 0, \quad \forall e \in \mathcal{E}(\mathcal{K}_c) \\
\text{rank}(\mathbf{A}_c) &= |\mathcal{V}(\mathcal{K}_c)| - 1 \\
\text{rank}(\mathbf{A}) &= N_V - p = N_V - |\mathcal{K}|
\end{aligned}
```

  Designation of exactly one datum node $`J_{\text{datum}, c} \in \mathcal{V}(\mathcal{K}_c)`$` per component yields the reduced incidence matrix `$`\mathbf{A}_{\text{reduced}}`$:

```math
\mathbf{A}_{\text{reduced}} \in \{-1, 0, +1\}^{(N_V - |\mathcal{K}|) \times N_E}, \quad \text{rank}(\mathbf{A}_{\text{reduced}}) = N_V - |\mathcal{K}|
```

  Deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 Anchor Invariant (Architectural Directive 1):

```math
\begin{aligned}
\forall J \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}, &\quad \Delta_J = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(J)) \\
\forall e \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}, &\quad \Delta_e = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(e)) \\
\forall K_{\text{kfl}} \in \mathcal{K}_{\text{constraints}}, &\quad \Delta_{K_{\text{kfl}}} = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(K_{\text{kfl}}))
\end{aligned}
```

  Diagnostic Trigger Rules: In accordance with REQ-0191, flow conservation and metrology violations deterministically emit structured diagnostic records:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\exists J_i, (e_1, e_2) \in \text{Incident}(J_i) : \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(e_1) \not\equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(e_2) &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(J_i, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0300}, \text{DimensionalMismatch}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
\exists J_i \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}} : \text{kind}(J_i) = \text{IdealConservative} \wedge \left| \sum_{e \in \text{Incident}(J_i)} A_{i, e} \cdot c_e \cdot f_e \right| \gt 0 &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(J_i, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0301}, \text{KirchhoffConservationViolation}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
\exists J_i \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}} : |\text{Incident}(J_i)| = 1 \wedge f_{\text{incident}} \ne 0 &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(J_i, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0220}, \text{UnconnectedInterface}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
\exists J_i \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}} : (\exists e \in \text{Incident}(J_i) : \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(e) = \bot_{\text{dim}}) &\implies \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(J_i) = \bot_{\text{dim}} \quad (\text{cascade diagnostic suppressed}) \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}| \ge C_{\text{max\_errors}} &\implies \exists D_{\text{term}} \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} : \text{code}(D_{\text{term}}) = \text{DiagnosticCode::E0199}
\end{aligned}
```

#### 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

Generalized Kirchhoff Flow Law continuity equation synthesis, incidence matrix assembly, and dimensional validation execute strictly within Complexity Class $`\mathbf{P}`$.

  For an MBSE compilation unit containing $`N_V = |\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}|`$` junction nodes and `$`N_E = |\mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}|`$` flow branches in an abstract network graph `$`\mathcal{G}_{\text{flow}}`$:
  1. *Incidence Matrix Assembly & Orientation Mapping*:
     - Allocating and populating the sparse node-branch incidence matrix $`\mathbf{A} \in \{-1, 0, +1\}^{N_V \times N_E}`$` visits each directed flow branch `$`e = (u, v) \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}`$` exactly once, writing source (`$`-1`$`) and target (`$`+1`$) entries.
     - This construction executes in deterministic linear time and space:

```math
T_{\text{incidence}}(N_V, N_E) \in O(N_V + N_E)
```

  2. *Incident Branch Dimensional Homogeneity Validation*:
     - Verifying dimensional equality in $`\mathbb{Q}^7`$` across all incident branches at junction `$`J_i`$` requires `$`\text{deg}(J_i) - 1`$ coordinate-wise rational comparisons.
     - Summing across all $`N_V`$` junctions, the total number of comparisons is bounded by `$`\sum_{i=1}^{N_V} \text{deg}(J_i) = 2 N_E`$.
     - Total dimensional validation executes in deterministic linear time:

```math
T_{\text{dim\_check}}(N_E) \in O(N_E)
```

  3. *Connected Component Partitioning & Datum Node Selection*:
     - Partitioning $`\mathcal{G}_{\text{flow}}`$` into its `$`|\mathcal{K}|`$ connected components using breadth-first search (BFS) or depth-first search (DFS) visits each junction and branch exactly once.
     - Selecting one datum node per connected component executes in deterministic linear time:

```math
T_{\text{components}}(N_V, N_E) \in O(N_V + N_E)
```

  4. *Kirchhoff Flow Continuity Constraint AST Synthesis*:
     - Synthesizing $`N_V - |\mathcal{K}|`$ linearly independent continuity constraint equations allocates AST expressions in contiguous bump arena storage.
     - Each constraint expression references $`\text{deg}(J_i)`$ branch flow terms, requiring total allocation and construction time bounded by:

```math
T_{\text{synth}}(N_V, N_E) \in O(N_V + N_E)
```

  5. *Static Flow Balance Evaluation*:
     - For static network models, calculating the sparse matrix-vector product $`\mathbf{A}_{\text{ideal}} \mathbf{C}_{\text{unit}} \mathbf{f}`$` performs `$`2 N_E`$ multiplications and additions, executing in deterministic linear time:

```math
T_{\text{static\_eval}}(N_E) \in O(N_E)
```

  6. *Aggregate Algorithmic Complexity*:
     - Total sequential execution time across all network stages is strictly linear with respect to the network graph size:

```math
T_{\text{total}}(N_V, N_E) \in O(N_V + N_E)
```

  7. *Parallel Work-Stealing Scaling (Architectural Directive 2)*:
     - When verifying and synthesizing constraints across $`M`$` independent network components or packages over `$`N_{\text{max\_workers}}`$ threads on a bounded work-stealing thread pool:

```math
T_{\text{parallel}}(N_V, N_E, N_{\text{max\_workers}}) \in O\left( \frac{N_V + N_E}{N_{\text{max\_workers}}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}} \right)
```

  8. *Space Complexity & Memory Bounds*:
     - Contiguous arena storage for junction nodes, flow branches, sparse incidence matrices, and synthesized continuity constraint AST nodes is strictly bounded by $`O(N_V + N_E)`$ bytes.
     - Deduplicated string interner memory is bounded by the lengths of unique junction and variable identifiers.
     - Diagnostic buffer memory is strictly bounded by $`O(C_{\text{max\_errors}}) = O(1)`$.

#### 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria

Automated test suites shall deterministically verify compliance against synthetic abstract MBSE models (`Package_0`, `Package_1`, `Classifier_Alpha`, `Junction_Alpha`, `Junction_Beta`, `FlowBranch_1`, `FlowBranch_2`, `Port_1`, `Port_2`, `param_x : Real`, `Quantity_1`, `Network_Alpha`) across the following verification gates:

  1. **Ideal Two-Branch Pass-Through Continuity Gate**: Ingest a synthetic flow network `Network_Alpha` containing two branches coupled at an ideal conservative junction `Junction_Alpha` with one branch entering ($`f_1`$`) and one exiting (`$`f_2`$`). Assert that the synthesized continuity equation evaluates to `$`f_1 - f_2 = 0 \iff f_1 = f_2`$ and generates zero diagnostics.
  2. **Multi-Branch Junction Flow Balance Gate**: Ingest a synthetic model containing a conservative junction `Junction_Beta` with 3 incoming branches ($`f_1, f_2, f_3`$`) and 2 outgoing branches (`$`f_4, f_5`$`). Assert that the synthesized continuity equation evaluates to `$`f_1 + f_2 + f_3 - f_4 - f_5 = 0`$ with exact coefficient mapping.
  3. **Branch Orientation Sign Inversion Gate**: Invert the reference direction of a flow branch from entering to leaving at a conservative junction. Assert that the incidence matrix entry switches from $`+1`$` to `$`-1`$ and that the synthesized continuity equation updates its algebraic sign accordingly.
  4. **Commensurable Unit Conversion Scaling Gate**: Ingest a conservative junction connecting branches with identical dimensions in $`\mathbb{Q}^7`$` but disparate units: branch 1 carrying litres per second (`$`c_1 = \frac{1}{1000}`$`) and branch 2 carrying cubic metres per second (`$`c_2 = 1`$`). Assert that the compiler synthesizes exact rational scale multipliers into the continuity equation: `$`\frac{1}{1000} f_1 - f_2 = 0`$.
  5. **Dynamic Capacitive Storage Accumulation Gate**: Ingest a synthetic junction `Junction_Alpha` designated with dynamic accumulation storage ($`S_1`$`). Assert that the compiler synthesizes the dynamic continuity equation `$`f_1 - f_2 = \frac{d S_1}{dt}`$.
  6. **Storage Dimension Time-Derivative Homogeneity Gate**: Ingest a synthetic junction where storage quantity $`S_1`$` has dimensions mismatched with the incident through variable (such that `$`\mathbf{d}(S_1) - [T]^1 \not\equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}`$). Assert deterministic compile-time rejection with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`DimensionalMismatch`).
  7. **Incident Through Variable Dimensional Mismatch Gate (`E0300`)**: Ingest a synthetic model attempting to connect branches with mismatched through dimensions to a single conservative junction (e.g., branch 1 carrying electric current $`[I]^1`$` and branch 2 carrying volumetric flow `$`[L]^3 [T]^{-1}`$). Assert deterministic compile-time rejection with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`DimensionalMismatch`), recording source byte spans isolating the mismatched coordinates.
  8. **Static Flow Continuity Violation Gate (`E0301`)**: Ingest a synthetic network with statically declared branch flow rates where incoming flow ($`10\text{ units}`$`) does not equal outgoing flow (`$`8\text{ units}`$) at an ideal conservative junction.
