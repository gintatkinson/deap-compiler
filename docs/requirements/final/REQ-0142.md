### REQ-0142: Smooth State Transition Blending & $C^1$-Continuity Bounds

- **Normative Statement**: The safety compiler, formal verification framework, and run-time assurance synthesis engine of Subsystem 7 shall synthesize smooth state transition interpolation functions and control arbitration blending logic for Run-Time Assurance (RTA) switching transitions, smoothly interpolating control output signals $\mathbf{u}(t)$ across a configured switching duration $T_{\text{switch}}$ to enforce $C^1$-continuity bounds and eliminate signal transients.

  Specifically, the transition blending engine, polynomial synthesizer, and rate envelope verifier shall strictly enforce the following structural, algebraic, and operational invariants across all compilation units:
  1. **Smooth Transition Blending Scope & Controller Arbitration**:
     - The compiler shall synthesize deterministic transition blending logic bridging control output signals between an active unverified complex controller ($C_{\text{complex}}$) and a formally certified recovery controller ($C_{\text{recovery}}$) instantiated under REQ-0141.
     - The synthesized blending logic shall eliminate step discontinuities, actuator chatter, impulse jolts, and high-frequency harmonics in the control output vector $\mathbf{u}(t) \in \mathcal{U}_{\text{ctrl}} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^m$ during control authority handover.
     - Blending shall operate bidirectionally:
       * *Complex-to-Recovery Transition (`Dir::ComplexToRecovery`)*: Triggered upon safety monitor envelope violations ($\mathcal{M}(\mathbf{x}(t)) \equiv \text{Unsafe}$) or Duplex cross-channel divergence, smoothly transferring control authority from $\mathbf{u}_{\text{complex}}(t)$ to $\mathbf{u}_{\text{recovery}}(t)$ across transition duration $T_{\text{switch}}$.
       * *Recovery-to-Complex Transition (`Dir::RecoveryToComplex`)*: Permitted under reversible latching policies (`LatchingPolicy::Reversible`, REQ-0141) after continuous dwelling in the interior safe return set $\mathcal{S}_{\text{return}}$, smoothly restoring authority from $\mathbf{u}_{\text{recovery}}(t)$ to $\mathbf{u}_{\text{complex}}(t)$ across duration $T_{\text{switch}}$.
     - Preemptive Recovery Snap Invariant: If a secondary safety violation or envelope breach occurs while executing a reverse transition (`Dir::RecoveryToComplex`), the switching logic shall immediately abort reverse blending and assert full recovery authority ($\lambda \leftarrow 1$) in zero delay steps ($O(1)$) to guarantee immediate forward invariance (REQ-0143).
  2. **Canonical Blending Polynomials & Continuity Classes**:
     - The compiler shall support two deterministic blending polynomial families parameterized by normalized transition time $\tau = \frac{t - t_{\text{switch}}}{T_{\text{switch}}} \in [0, 1]$:
       * **Cubic Hermite Polynomial (`Order::Cubic`, $C^1$-continuity)**:
         Synthesized as $\lambda_3(\tau) = 3\tau^2 - 2\tau^3$, satisfying the boundary value constraints:
         $$\lambda_3(0) = 0, \quad \lambda_3(1) = 1, \quad \left.\frac{d\lambda_3}{d\tau}\right|_{\tau=0} = 0, \quad \left.\frac{d\lambda_3}{d\tau}\right|_{\tau=1} = 0$$
         Enforcing first-derivative continuity ($C^1$) matching the boundary derivatives of the active controller signals at both endpoints $\tau = 0$ and $\tau = 1$.
       * **Quintic Hermite Polynomial (`Order::Quintic`, $C^2$-continuity)**:
         Synthesized as $\lambda_5(\tau) = 10\tau^3 - 15\tau^4 + 6\tau^5$, satisfying the boundary value constraints:
         $$\lambda_5(0) = 0, \quad \lambda_5(1) = 1, \quad \left.\frac{d\lambda_5}{d\tau}\right|_{\tau=0,1} = 0, \quad \left.\frac{d^2\lambda_5}{d\tau^2}\right|_{\tau=0,1} = 0$$
         Enforcing both velocity ($C^1$) and acceleration ($C^2$) continuity across switching boundaries for dynamic systems sensitive to control jerk.
     - Strict Monotonicity Invariant: For all $\tau \in [0, 1]$, the blending function must satisfy $\frac{d\lambda}{d\tau} \ge 0$, guaranteeing that weight transfer proceeds monotonically without overshoot ($\forall \tau \in [0, 1], \, 0 \le \lambda(\tau) \le 1$).
  3. **Switching Duration & Forward Invariance Safety Latency Bounds**:
     - The switching duration $T_{\text{switch}} \in \mathbb{Q}^+$ must be an explicitly specified, strictly positive rational quantity ($T_{\text{switch}} \gt 0$).
     - Maximum Transition Latency Gate: $T_{\text{switch}}$ must satisfy the safety reaction bound:
       $$T_{\text{switch}} \le T_{\text{max\_switch}} = \frac{\delta_{\text{margin}}}{L_h \cdot \sup_{\mathbf{x} \in \mathcal{S}_{\text{safe}}, \, \mathbf{u} \in \mathcal{U}_{\text{ctrl}}} \|f(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{u})\|}$$
       where $\delta_{\text{margin}}$ is the safety boundary margin established in REQ-0141, ensuring that the blended control trajectory does not permit state $\mathbf{x}(t)$ to exit the admissible safe set $\mathcal{S}_{\text{safe}}$ before recovery control reaches full authority.
     - If $T_{\text{switch}} \le 0$ or if $T_{\text{switch}} \gt T_{\text{max\_switch}}$, compilation shall fail with diagnostic error code `E0400` (`InvalidSwitchingDuration`).
  4. **Actuator Domain Envelope & Slew-Rate Conformance**:
     - Convexity Preservation: Since $\forall \tau \in [0, 1], \, 0 \le \lambda(\tau) \le 1$, if both candidate control vectors satisfy the actuator saturation envelope ($\mathbf{u}_{\text{complex}}(t) \in [u_{\min}, u_{\max}]$ and $\mathbf{u}_{\text{recovery}}(t) \in [u_{\min}, u_{\max}]$), the linear convex combination $\mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}}(t)$ is guaranteed to lie within $[u_{\min}, u_{\max}]$.
     - Actuator Slew-Rate Limit Verification: The compiler shall compute the upper bound on the control signal time derivative:
       $$\left\|\frac{d\mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}}}{dt}\right\| \le (1 - \lambda(\tau)) \left\|\dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{complex}}\right\| + \lambda(\tau) \left\|\dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{recovery}}\right\| + \frac{1}{T_{\text{switch}}} \left|\frac{d\lambda}{d\tau}\right| \|\mathbf{u}_{\text{recovery}}(t) - \mathbf{u}_{\text{complex}}(t)\|$$
       Evaluating the maximum polynomial derivative ($\max_{\tau \in [0, 1]} \frac{d\lambda_3}{d\tau} = 1.5$ for cubic; $\max_{\tau \in [0, 1]} \frac{d\lambda_5}{d\tau} = 1.875$ for quintic), the compiler shall verify statically against the declared actuator slew-rate bound $\dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\max} \in \mathbb{Q}^+$.
     - If the worst-case derivative breaches the slew-rate bound ($\sup \|\dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{blend}}\| \gt \dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\max}$), the compiler shall emit diagnostic error code `E0440` (`SlewRateEnvelopeViolation`).
     - If the blended output vector violates the actuator output domain bounds $[u_{\min}, u_{\max}]$, the compiler shall emit diagnostic error code `E0440` (`BlendedOutputEnvelopeViolation`).
  5. **Discrete-Time Fixed-Step Realization & Numerics**:
     - For downstream simulation (REQ-0179, REQ-0181) and target code generation (REQ-0175, REQ-0184, REQ-0185), the compiler shall discretize the continuous blending function over discrete execution step size $T_s \in \mathbb{Q}^+$, synthesizing an exact finite sequence of $N_{\text{steps}} = \lceil T_{\text{switch}} / T_s \rceil$ interpolation weights.
     - Stepping Evaluation: Weights shall be evaluated using Horner's polynomial evaluation form:
       $$\lambda_3(\tau_k) = \tau_k^2 (3 - 2\tau_k), \quad \tau_k = \frac{k}{N_{\text{steps}}}, \quad k \in \{0, 1, \dots, N_{\text{steps}}\}$$
       with zero dynamic heap allocation and strictly bounded roundoff error ($\epsilon_{\text{roundoff}} \le 10^{-7}$).
     - The discrete-time realization shall guarantee bitwise deterministic reproduction across execution cycles and compiler targets.
  6. **Transition Blender Structural AST Component & Port Binding**:
     - The compiler shall allocate a composite `TransitionBlender` component record in contiguous memory arena storage:
       * Input ports: primary control command $\mathbf{u}_{\text{complex}}$, recovery control command $\mathbf{u}_{\text{recovery}}$, switching event trigger $\text{ev}_{\text{switch}}$, and direction mode $\text{dir} \in \{ \text{Dir::ComplexToRecovery}, \text{Dir::RecoveryToComplex} \}$.
       * Output ports: blended control command $\mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}}$, blending active flag $\text{is\_blending} \in \{0, 1\}$, and normalized progress fraction $\tau \in [0, 1]$.
     - If any required input or output port of the transition blender block is unmapped or dangling, the compiler shall emit diagnostic error code `E0400` (`UnboundBlendingSignal`).

     - *Directive 2 (Bounded Work-Stealing Parallelism)*: Parallel validation of transition blending configurations, slew-rate envelope verification, and polynomial generation across independent packages shall execute strictly on a centralized, bounded work-stealing thread pool with worker bound $N_{\text{max\_workers}}$ in accordance with REQ-0012. Unbounded manual thread spawning is strictly banned.
     - *Directive 3 (String Interner Memory Allocation Exception)*: Blender block identifiers, port labels, polynomial family tags, and parameter strings shall be interned into the deduplicated global symbol interner pool during lexical analysis per REQ-0036 as the sole permitted exception to zero-copy memory management.

- **Formal Invariant**: Let $\mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}$ denote the universe of allocated AST nodes in contiguous bump arena storage, and let $\mathcal{S}_{\text{intern}}$ denote the deduplicated global symbol interner pool.

  An abstract transition blender specification is formalized as a 10-tuple:

  $$
  \text{Blender} = \langle \text{id}_B, \text{name}_B, C_{\text{complex}}, C_{\text{recovery}}, T_{\text{switch}}, \Pi_{\text{poly}}, \dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\max}, [u_{\min}, u_{\max}], \Delta_B, \text{span}_B \rangle
  $$

  where:

  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  \text{id}_B &\in \mathbb{N}_{32} \quad (\text{contiguous arena node handle}) \\
  \text{name}_B &\in \mathcal{S}_{\text{intern}} \quad (\text{interned symbol identifier}) \\
  C_{\text{complex}} &\in \mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}} \quad (\text{complex controller node handle}) \\
  C_{\text{recovery}} &\in \mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}} \quad (\text{verified recovery controller node handle}) \\
  T_{\text{switch}} &\in \mathbb{Q}^+ \quad (\text{switching transition duration}) \\
  \Pi_{\text{poly}} &\in \{ \text{Order::Cubic}, \text{Order::Quintic} \} \quad (\text{blending polynomial order}) \\
  \dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\max} &\in (\mathbb{Q}^+)^m \quad (\text{actuator slew-rate saturation bound vector}) \\
  [u_{\min}, u_{\max}] &\subset \mathbb{R}^m \quad (\text{admissible actuator output envelope}) \\

  \end{aligned}
  $$

  Let $\tau \in [0, 1]$ denote the normalized transition progress variable:

  $$
  \tau(t) = \frac{t - t_{\text{switch}}}{T_{\text{switch}}}
  $$

  1. Continuous Blended Control Output Law:
  For transition direction $\text{Dir::ComplexToRecovery}$, the blended control command vector $\mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}}(t) \in \mathbb{R}^m$ satisfies:

  $$
  \mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}}(t) = (1 - \lambda(\tau)) \cdot \mathbf{u}_{\text{complex}}(t) + \lambda(\tau) \cdot \mathbf{u}_{\text{recovery}}(t)
  $$

  For reverse transition direction $\text{Dir::RecoveryToComplex}$, the blended control command vector satisfies:

  $$
  \mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}}(t) = \lambda(\tau) \cdot \mathbf{u}_{\text{complex}}(t) + (1 - \lambda(\tau)) \cdot \mathbf{u}_{\text{recovery}}(t)
  $$

  2. Blending Polynomial Invariants:
  Under Cubic Hermite blending ($\Pi_{\text{poly}} = \text{Order::Cubic}$):

  $$
  \lambda_3(\tau) = 3\tau^2 - 2\tau^3
  $$

  satisfying endpoint value and derivative constraints:

  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  \lambda_3(0) &= 0, \quad \lambda_3(1) = 1 \\
  \left.\frac{d\lambda_3}{d\tau}\right|_{\tau=0} &= 0, \quad \left.\frac{d\lambda_3}{d\tau}\right|_{\tau=1} = 0
  \end{aligned}
  $$

  with non-negative derivative ensuring strict monotonicity:

  $$
  \frac{d\lambda_3}{d\tau} = 6\tau(1 - \tau) \ge 0 \quad \forall \tau \in [0, 1], \quad \max_{\tau \in [0, 1]} \frac{d\lambda_3}{d\tau} = \frac{3}{2}
  $$

  Under Quintic Hermite blending ($\Pi_{\text{poly}} = \text{Order::Quintic}$):

  $$
  \lambda_5(\tau) = 10\tau^3 - 15\tau^4 + 6\tau^5
  $$

  satisfying endpoint value, first-derivative, and second-derivative constraints:

  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  \lambda_5(0) &= 0, \quad \lambda_5(1) = 1 \\
  \left.\frac{d\lambda_5}{d\tau}\right|_{\tau=0} &= 0, \quad \left.\frac{d\lambda_5}{d\tau}\right|_{\tau=1} = 0 \\
  \left.\frac{d^2\lambda_5}{d\tau^2}\right|_{\tau=0} &= 0, \quad \left.\frac{d^2\lambda_5}{d\tau^2}\right|_{\tau=1} = 0
  \end{aligned}
  $$

  with first derivative:

  $$
  \frac{d\lambda_5}{d\tau} = 30\tau^2(1 - \tau)^2 \ge 0 \quad \forall \tau \in [0, 1], \quad \max_{\tau \in [0, 1]} \frac{d\lambda_5}{d\tau} = \frac{15}{8}
  $$

  3. Derivative Matching & $C^1$-Continuity Invariant:
  Differentiating the blended control signal with respect to real time $t$:

  $$
  \frac{d\mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}}}{dt} = \frac{1}{T_{\text{switch}}} \frac{d\lambda}{d\tau} (\mathbf{u}_{\text{recovery}}(t) - \mathbf{u}_{\text{complex}}(t)) + (1 - \lambda(\tau)) \frac{d\mathbf{u}_{\text{complex}}}{dt} + \lambda(\tau) \frac{d\mathbf{u}_{\text{recovery}}}{dt}
  $$

  Evaluating at switching boundaries:

  $$
  \begin{aligned}
  \lim_{t \to t_{\text{switch}}^+} \frac{d\mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}}}{dt} &= \left.\frac{d\mathbf{u}_{\text{complex}}}{dt}\right|_{t=t_{\text{switch}}} \\
  \lim_{t \to (t_{\text{switch}} + T_{\text{switch}})^-} \frac{d\mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}}}{dt} &= \left.\frac{d\mathbf{u}_{\text{recovery}}}{dt}\right|_{t=t_{\text{switch}} + T_{\text{switch}}}
  \end{aligned}
  $$

  guaranteeing that $\mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}} \in C^1([t_{\text{switch}}, t_{\text{switch}} + T_{\text{switch}}])$ across the entire switching duration without derivative discontinuities.
  When $\Pi_{\text{poly}} = \text{Order::Quintic}$, second derivatives also match at both boundaries, guaranteeing $\mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}} \in C^2([t_{\text{switch}}, t_{\text{switch}} + T_{\text{switch}}])$.

  4. Actuator Domain Convexity & Slew-Rate Bound Invariants:
  Since $\forall \tau \in [0, 1], \, 0 \le \lambda(\tau) \le 1$:

  $$
  \mathbf{u}_{\text{complex}}(t) \in [u_{\min}, u_{\max}] \;\land\; \mathbf{u}_{\text{recovery}}(t) \in [u_{\min}, u_{\max}] \implies \forall \tau \in [0, 1], \, \mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}}(t) \in [u_{\min}, u_{\max}]
  $$

  The peak time derivative of the blended output is bounded by:

  $$
  \sup_{t \in [t_{\text{switch}}, t_{\text{switch}} + T_{\text{switch}}]} \left\| \frac{d\mathbf{u}_{\text{blend}}}{dt} \right\| \le \dot{\mathbf{u}}_{\max}
  $$

  5. Discrete-Time Fixed-Step Stepping Invariant:
  For discrete simulation step size $T_s$ and step count $N_{\text{steps}} = \lceil T_{\text{switch}} / T_s \rceil$:

  $$
  \tau_k = \frac{k}{N_{\text{steps}}}, \quad k \in \{0, 1, \dots, N_{\text{steps}}\}
  $$

  evaluated in Horner polynomial form:

  $$
  \lambda_3(\tau_k) = \tau_k^2 (3 - 2\tau_k), \quad \lambda_5(\tau_k) = \tau_k^3 (10 + \tau_k(6\tau_k - 15))
  $$
