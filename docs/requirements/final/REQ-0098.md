### REQ-0098: Formal $`\mathbb{Q}^7`$ Rational Vector Space over SI Base Dimensions

- **Normative Statement**: The metrology engine, semantic type system, and AST validation pipeline of Subsystem 5 shall model all physical dimensions as a formal 7-dimensional vector space $`(\mathbb{Q}^7, +, \cdot)`$ over the algebraic field of rational numbers $`\mathbb{Q}`$. The basis vectors of the vector space shall correspond strictly and bijectively to the seven International System of Units (SI) base quantities in canonical coordinate order: Mass ($`M`$), Length ($`L`$), Time ($`T`$), Base Flow Quantity ($`I`$, electric current or through-flow rate), Thermodynamic Temperature ($`\Theta`$), Amount of Substance ($`N`$), and Luminous Intensity ($`J`$).

  The compiler shall strictly enforce the following algebraic, structural, and metrological invariants:
  1. **Canonical Basis Vectors & 7D Coordinate Representation**:
     - The metrology engine shall define the canonical basis $`\mathcal{B}_{\text{SI}} = \{ \mathbf{e}_M, \mathbf{e}_L, \mathbf{e}_T, \mathbf{e}_I, \mathbf{e}_\Theta, \mathbf{e}_N, \mathbf{e}_J \}`$ where each basis vector represents an orthogonal elementary physical dimension possessing coordinate value 1 at its assigned dimension index and 0 across all other coordinates.
     - Every physical dimension $`\mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$ shall be represented internally as an irreducible rational 7-tuple:

$$
\mathbf{d} = (\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \epsilon, \zeta, \eta) \in \mathbb{Q}^7
$$

       corresponding to the formal base quantity power product $`[M]^\alpha [L]^\beta [T]^\gamma [I]^\delta [\Theta]^\epsilon [N]^\zeta [J]^\eta`$.
     - Each rational coordinate $`d_i = \frac{p_i}{q_i}`$ ($`i \in \{1, \dots, 7\}`$) must be stored as an exact irreducible rational number with signed integer numerator $`p_i \in \mathbb{Z}`$ and positive integer denominator $`q_i \in \mathbb{Z}^+`$ such that $`\gcd(|p_i|, q_i) = 1`$.
     - Pure Dimensionless Identity: The zero vector $`\mathbf{0} = (0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0) \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$ shall model pure dimensionless quantities, scalar numbers, counts, and dimensionless ratios.
  2. **Vector Space Structure & Algebraic Homomorphisms**:
     - The algebraic structure $`(\mathbb{Q}^7, +, \cdot)`$ shall satisfy all eight vector space axioms over the field of rational numbers $`\mathbb{Q}`$: vector addition associativity, vector addition commutativity, additive identity element ($`\mathbf{0}`$), additive inverse elements ($`-\mathbf{d}`$), scalar multiplication associativity, scalar multiplicative identity ($`1 \cdot \mathbf{d} = \mathbf{d}`$), scalar distributivity over vector addition, and vector distributivity over field addition.
     - Dimensional Multiplication: For any two physical quantity expressions $`Q_1, Q_2`$, the dimension of their algebraic product shall evaluate to the vector sum of their dimension vectors:

$$
\text{dim}(Q_1 \cdot Q_2) = \text{dim}(Q_1) + \text{dim}(Q_2)
$$

     - Dimensional Division & Inversion: The dimension of quantity reciprocals and quotients shall evaluate to vector negation and subtraction:

$$
\text{dim}(1 / Q) = -\text{dim}(Q), \quad \text{dim}(Q_1 / Q_2) = \text{dim}(Q_1) - \text{dim}(Q_2)
$$

     - Rational Power Exponentiation: For any rational scalar exponent $`k = \frac{r}{s} \in \mathbb{Q}`$ ($`s \in \mathbb{Z}^+`$, $`\gcd(|r|, s) = 1`$), the dimension of quantity power exponentiation $`Q^k`$ shall evaluate to scalar multiplication:

$$
\text{dim}(Q^k) = k \cdot \text{dim}(Q)
$$

  3. **Exact Rational Arithmetic & Zero Floating-Point Drift**:
     - All dimensional operations (vector addition, vector subtraction, scalar multiplication by rational powers) shall be executed strictly in exact rational arithmetic using bounded 64-bit integer words for numerators and denominators.
     - The use of IEEE 754 floating-point representations (`f32`, `f64`) in dimensional vectors and metrology calculations is strictly prohibited to guarantee total absence of rounding drift, non-determinism, and platform-dependent floating-point divergence.
     - Zero Denominator Protection: Any rational exponent coordinate or scalar divisor with denominator $`q = 0`$ is mathematically undefined and shall be rejected deterministically at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`ZeroDenominatorRationalDimension`).
     - Overflow Protection: All intermediate coordinate additions and multiplications must perform checked integer arithmetic; any integer overflow beyond 64-bit capacity shall be intercepted and rejected with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`RationalArithmeticOverflow`).
  4. **Dimensional Homogeneity & Commensurability Gate**:
     - Two physical quantities $`Q_1, Q_2`$ are commensurable if and only if their rational dimension vectors are strictly identical:

$$
Q_1 \sim_{\text{dim}} Q_2 \iff \text{dim}(Q_1) = \text{dim}(Q_2)
$$

     - Additive Homogeneity: The metrology engine shall strictly require that operands of additive expressions ($`A + B`$, $`A - B`$) share identical rational dimensions: $`\text{dim}(A) = \text{dim}(B)`$. Mismatched additive dimensions shall be deterministically rejected at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`DimensionalMismatch`) in accordance with REQ-0191.
     - Relational Comparison Homogeneity: Operands of relational comparisons ($`A == B`$, $`A \ne B`$, $`A \lt B`$, $`A \le B`$, $`A \gt B`$, $`A \ge B`$) must share identical rational dimensions: $`\text{dim}(A) = \text{dim}(B)`$. Relational comparisons across disparate dimensions shall be deterministically rejected with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`DimensionalMismatch`).
  5. **Architectural Directives Compliance**:
     - *Directive 1 (Deterministic UUIDv5 Anchors)*: Every dimension definition node, base dimension symbol, and metrological quantity AST node shall be assigned an immutable deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 identifier derived from the compiler namespace OID and canonical fully qualified path ($`\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(n))`$) in accordance with REQ-0010.
     - *Directive 2 (Bounded Work-Stealing Parallelism)*: Parallel dimensional analysis, vector arithmetic validation, and metrological type inference across independent compilation units or packages shall execute on a bounded work-stealing thread pool with worker bound $`N_{\text{max\_workers}}`$ in accordance with REQ-0012.
     - *Directive 3 (String Interner Allocation Exception)*: Dimension names, base quantity symbols (`Mass`, `Length`, `Time`), and unit labels shall be interned into the deduplicated global string interner pool during lexical analysis per REQ-0036 as the sole permitted exception to zero-copy memory management.
     - *Directive 4 (Synchronization Token Error Recovery)*: The metrology parser and type checker shall implement synchronization token error recovery on statement delimiters (`;`) and block boundaries (`}`). When dimensional faults (`E0300`) occur, diagnostics shall accumulate in error sink buffer $`\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}`$, substitute typed error dimension sentinel $`\bot_{\text{dim}}`$, and continue processing subsequent declarations. If accumulated diagnostics reach $`C_{\text{max\_errors}}`$, terminal diagnostic `E0199` shall be emitted and compilation halted gracefully per REQ-0013, REQ-0016, and REQ-0191.
     - *Directive 5 (Panic-Free Release Isolation)*: Metrology vector arithmetic and dimensional verification routines shall enforce a deterministic, non-panicking execution contract with total-function error handling. Performance latency ($`\lt 25\text{ ms}`$) and peak RSS memory ($`\lt 100\text{ MB}`$ for $`10^5`$ dimensional operations) shall be evaluated exclusively in compiled Release mode (`--release`) under dedicated benchmark harnesses isolated from CI logic tests in accordance with REQ-0011 and REQ-0196.

  All diagnostic messages, test models, and illustrative examples shall strictly employ synthetic abstract MBSE placeholders (`Package_0`, `Package_1`, `Classifier_Alpha`, `Dimension_Alpha`, `Dimension_Beta`, `Quantity_1`, `Quantity_2`, `param_x : Real`, `param_y : Real`), strictly prohibiting hardcoded domain concepts, mock data, and non-ASCII dashes.

$$
\begin{aligned}

\mathbf{d} = \alpha \mathbf{e}_M + \beta \mathbf{e}_L + \gamma \mathbf{e}_T + \delta \mathbf{e}_I + \epsilon \mathbf{e}_\Theta + \zeta \mathbf{e}_N + \eta \mathbf{e}_J
$$

  Irreducible Rational Coordinate Invariant: Each coordinate $`d_i`$ ($`i \in \{1, \dots, 7\}`$) is an irreducible fraction:

$$
\forall i \in \{1, \dots, 7\}, \quad d_i = \frac{p_i}{q_i}, \quad p_i \in \mathbb{Z}, \; q_i \in \mathbb{Z}^+, \; \gcd(|p_i|, q_i) = 1
$$

  Each dimension definition node $`D \in \mathcal{D}`$ is formalized as a 5-tuple:

$$
D = \langle \text{id}_D, \text{name}_D, \mathbf{d}_D, \Delta_D, \text{span}_D \rangle

  where:

$$
\begin{aligned}

  Vector Addition and Subtraction Operations: For any $`\mathbf{d}_1, \mathbf{d}_2 \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{d}_1 + \mathbf{d}_2 &= (\alpha_1 + \alpha_2, \beta_1 + \beta_2, \gamma_1 + \gamma_2, \delta_1 + \delta_2, \epsilon_1 + \epsilon_2, \zeta_1 + \zeta_2, \eta_1 + \eta_2) \\
\mathbf{d}_1 - \mathbf{d}_2 &= (\alpha_1 - \alpha_2, \beta_1 - \beta_2, \gamma_1 - \gamma_2, \delta_1 - \delta_2, \epsilon_1 - \epsilon_2, \zeta_1 - \zeta_2, \eta_1 - \eta_2)
\end{aligned}
$$

  where rational coordinate addition and subtraction evaluate via canonical reduction:

$$
\frac{a}{b} \pm \frac{c}{d} = \frac{(a \cdot d \pm c \cdot b) / \gcd(|a \cdot d \pm c \cdot b|, b \cdot d)}{(b \cdot d) / \gcd(|a \cdot d \pm c \cdot b|, b \cdot d)}
$$

  Additive Identity and Inversion:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{0} &= (0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0) \in \mathbb{Q}^7 \implies \mathbf{d} + \mathbf{0} = \mathbf{d} \\
-\mathbf{d} &= (-\alpha, -\beta, -\gamma, -\delta, -\epsilon, -\zeta, -\eta) \implies \mathbf{d} + (-\mathbf{d}) = \mathbf{0}
\end{aligned}
$$

  Scalar Multiplication by Rational Scalar $`k = \frac{r}{s} \in \mathbb{Q}`$ ($`s \in \mathbb{Z}^+`$, $`\gcd(|r|, s) = 1`$):

$$
k \cdot \mathbf{d} = (k \cdot \alpha, k \cdot \beta, k \cdot \gamma, k \cdot \delta, k \cdot \epsilon, k \cdot \zeta, k \cdot \eta)
$$

  where rational coordinate multiplication evaluates via canonical reduction:

$$
\frac{r}{s} \cdot \frac{p}{q} = \frac{(r \cdot p) / \gcd(|r \cdot p|, s \cdot q)}{(s \cdot q) / \gcd(|r \cdot p|, s \cdot q)}
$$

  Vector Space Axioms over Field $`\mathbb{Q}`$: For all $`\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v}, \mathbf{w} \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$ and all $`a, b \in \mathbb{Q}`$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{u} + (\mathbf{v} + \mathbf{w}) &= (\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v}) + \mathbf{w} \quad (\text{Addition Associativity}) \\
\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v} &= \mathbf{v} + \mathbf{u} \quad (\text{Addition Commutativity}) \\
\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{0} &= \mathbf{u} \quad (\text{Addition Identity}) \\
\mathbf{u} + (-\mathbf{u}) &= \mathbf{0} \quad (\text{Addition Inverse}) \\
a \cdot (b \cdot \mathbf{u}) &= (a \cdot b) \cdot \mathbf{u} \quad (\text{Scalar Multiplication Associativity}) \\
1 \cdot \mathbf{u} &= \mathbf{u} \quad (\text{Scalar Multiplication Identity}) \\
a \cdot (\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v}) &= a \cdot \mathbf{u} + a \cdot \mathbf{v} \quad (\text{Distributivity over Vector Addition}) \\
(a + b) \cdot \mathbf{u} &= a \cdot \mathbf{u} + b \cdot \mathbf{u} \quad (\text{Distributivity over Field Addition})
\end{aligned}
$$

  Physical Quantity Dimension Homomorphism: Let $`\mathcal{Q}`$ denote the set of physical quantity expressions. The dimension evaluation function $`\text{dim} : \mathcal{Q} \to \mathbb{Q}^7`$ satisfies:

$$
\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}
$$

- **Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds**: Vector space operations, rational dimensional arithmetic, and commensurability type checking execute strictly within Complexity Class $`\mathbf{P}`$.

  For an abstract MBSE model containing $`N`$ quantity expressions, $`D`$ dimension definitions, and rational coordinates represented in 64-bit integer words:
  1. *Rational Coordinate Arithmetic & Euclidean GCD Reduction*:
     - Adding or subtracting two rational numbers $`\frac{a}{b} \pm \frac{c}{d}`$ computes intermediate products $`a \cdot d \pm c \cdot b`$ and $`b \cdot d`$, followed by Euclidean algorithm greatest common divisor ($`\gcd`$) reduction.
     - For 64-bit integer words, the Euclidean algorithm terminates in at most $`2 \log_2(\min(|p|, q)) \le 128`$ division steps. Thus, coordinate rational arithmetic requires deterministic $`O(1)`$ operations and $`O(1)`$ auxiliary space:

$$
T_{\text{rational\_op}} \in O(1), \quad S_{\text{rational\_op}} \in O(1)
$$

  2. *7D Vector Addition, Subtraction & Inversion*:
     - Adding or subtracting two dimension vectors $`\mathbf{d}_1, \mathbf{d}_2 \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$ executes exactly 7 independent rational coordinate operations. The computational bound is deterministic $`O(1)`$ time and $`O(1)`$ auxiliary space:

$$
T_{\text{vector\_add}} \in O(1), \quad S_{\text{vector\_add}} \in O(1)
$$

  3. *Scalar Rational Multiplication*:
     - Multiplying a dimension vector $`\mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$ by a rational scalar exponent $`k = \frac{r}{s} \in \mathbb{Q}`$ executes 7 rational coordinate multiplications and reductions, requiring deterministic $`O(1)`$ operations and $`O(1)`$ auxiliary space:

$$
T_{\text{scalar\_mul}} \in O(1), \quad S_{\text{scalar\_mul}} \in O(1)
$$

  4. *Dimensional Homogeneity & Commensurability Verification*:
     - Checking dimensional equality between two quantities $`\text{dim}(Q_1) == \text{dim}(Q_2)`$ performs 7 coordinate equality comparisons (or cross-multiplications for irreducible representations), terminating in deterministic $`O(1)`$ operations and requiring zero heap allocations:

$$
T_{\text{homogeneity}} \in O(1), \quad S_{\text{homogeneity}} \in O(1)
$$

  5. *Aggregate Compilation Unit Complexity*:
     - For an AST compilation unit containing $`N`$ quantity expressions and $`D`$ dimension declarations:

$$
T_{\text{total}}(N, D) \in O(N + D) \subseteq O(N)
$$

$$
S_{\text{total}}(N, D) \in O(N + D)
$$

  6. *Parallel Work-Stealing Scaling (Architectural Directive 2)*:
     - When $`M`$ independent packages or compilation units evaluate dimensional expressions across $`N_{\text{max\_workers}}`$ threads in a bounded work-stealing thread pool:

$$
T_{\text{parallel}}(N, N_{\text{max\_workers}}) \in O\left( \frac{N}{N_{\text{max\_workers}}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}} \right)
$$

  7. *Space Complexity & Memory Bounds*:
     - Dimension definition nodes and rational vectors are allocated in contiguous memory arena storage, strictly bounded by $`O(D)`$ bytes.
     - Deduplicated string interner memory is bounded by the sum of lengths of unique dimension identifiers.
     - The diagnostic error sink buffer is strictly bounded by $`O(C_{\text{max\_errors}}) = O(1)`$.
