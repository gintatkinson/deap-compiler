## 1. Normative Statement

The downstream projection engine of Subsystem 9 SHALL project formal Use Case specifications from the compilation unit abstract syntax tree.

Projections MUST formalize use cases $UC \in \mathcal{U}_{\text{proj}}$ (`UseCase_Alpha`) as structured contracts:
1. **Preconditions**: Initial conditions $P_{\text{pre}} \in \text{Expr}_{\text{bool}}$ (`Precondition_1`) SHALL specify system state predicates. Missing or ungrounded precondition expressions emit `E0311`.
2. **Primary Success Path**: The primary nominal scenario $S_{\text{primary}}$ (`Scenario_0`) SHALL formalize an ordered sequence of operational steps $\langle \text{Step}_0, \dots, \text{Step}_k \rangle$ ($k \ge 1$). Steps bind initiating actors (`Actor_0`), actions (`Action_0`), and expected state predicates. Disconnected steps or cycles emit `E0311`. Unresolved actor bindings (`Actor_Missing`) emit `E0300`.
3. **Alternate Extensions**: Extension flows $x \in \mathcal{X}_{\text{alt}}$ (`Extension_0`) SHALL branch from step $s_{\text{anchor}} \in S_{\text{primary}}$ under guard $g_{\text{alt}} \in \text{Expr}_{\text{bool}}$, resuming at step $s_{\text{resume}} \in S_{\text{primary}}$ or terminating at postcondition $Q_{\text{alt}}$. Dangling anchor references or invalid resumption targets emit `E0311`.
4. **Postconditions**: Execution of $S_{\text{primary}}$ SHALL satisfy nominal postcondition $Q_{\text{post}}$ (`Postcondition_1`). Violations bind sentinels $\bot_{\text{uc}}$ or $\bot_{\text{step}}$ in error sink $\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}$.
5. **Directives**: UUIDv5 anchors (Directive 1); work-stealing ceiling $N_{\text{max\_workers}}$ (Directive 2); symbol interning in $\mathcal{S}_{\text{intern}}$ (Directive 3); recovery on `;`, `}`, and markdown fences, halting at $C_{\text{max\_errors}}$ with `E0199` (Directive 4); Release budget ($< 25\text{ ms}$ for $10^4$ steps, RSS $< 100\text{ MB}$) (Directive 5). MBSE placeholders (`Package_0`, `UseCase_Alpha`, `Scenario_0`, `Precondition_1`, `Postcondition_1`, `Actor_0`, `Step_0`, `Extension_0`, `param_x : Real`) used.

## 2. Formal Invariant

Projected use case specifications, scenario step sequences, and alternate branches satisfy:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\forall UC\in\mathcal{U}_{\text{proj}},&\quad UC=\langle\text{name}_{UC},\mathbf{A}_{UC},P_{\text{pre}},S_{\text{primary}},\mathcal{X}_{\text{alt}},Q_{\text{post}},\Delta_{UC}\rangle \\
\forall s\in S_{\text{primary}},&\quad\text{actor}(s)\in\mathbf{A}_{UC}\land\text{action}(s)\in\mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}\land\text{post}(s)\in\text{Expr}_{\text{bool}} \\
\forall x\in\mathcal{X}_{\text{alt}},&\quad x=\langle s_{\text{anchor}}(x),g_{\text{alt}}(x),S_{\text{alt}}(x),s_{\text{resume}}(x),Q_{\text{alt}}(x)\rangle \\
&\quad\land s_{\text{anchor}}(x)\in S_{\text{primary}}\land(s_{\text{resume}}(x)\in S_{\text{primary}}\lor s_{\text{resume}}(x)=\bot_{\text{term}}) \\
(P_{\text{pre}}=\bot\lor\exists x,s_{\text{anchor}}(x)\notin S_{\text{primary}})&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(UC,\text{E0311})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land UC\gets\bot_{\text{uc}} \\
\exists a\in\mathbf{A}_{UC}\text{ s.t. }a\notin\mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(UC,\text{E0300})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}\land UC\gets\bot_{\text{uc}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}|\ge C_{\text{max\_errors}}&\implies\text{Diagnostic}(\text{E0199})\in\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}},\quad\Delta_{UC}=\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}},\text{FQN}(UC))
\end{aligned}
```

| $\mathbf{A}_{UC}$ | Participating actors allocated in $\mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}$ |
| $P_{\text{pre}}, Q_{\text{post}}$ | Precondition and primary postcondition expressions |
| $S_{\text{primary}}, s$ | Primary success path step sequence and step |
| $\mathcal{X}_{\text{alt}}, x$ | Alternate extensions and branch tuple |
| $s_{\text{anchor}}, s_{\text{resume}}$ | Primary branch anchor and resumption step target |

## 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

- **Complexity Class:** Class $\mathbf{P}$ (Deterministic Polynomial Time).
- **Asymptotic Bound:** $O(|UC| \cdot \text{steps})$ use case scenario trace ($|UC| = |\mathcal{U}_{\text{proj}}|$, $\text{steps} = \max |S_{\text{primary}}|$). AST traversal executes in $O(|UC|)$ time; linear step validation and branch target resolution execute in $O(\text{steps})$ time. Parallel pool: $O((|UC| \cdot \text{steps})/N_{\text{max\_workers}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}})$. Arena memory: $O(|UC| \cdot \text{steps})$.
- **Tractable Subclass and Frontier:** N/A (Deterministic P). Linear scenario tracing bypasses NP-1 interval solving (REQ-0117), NP-5 state reachability (REQ-0124), and NP-8 SMT solving (REQ-0197).

## 4. Verification & Conformance Criteria (IEEE 29148 Acceptance Criteria)

### AC-01: Canonical Use Case and Primary Success Path Projection
- **Given:** `Package_0` declaring `UseCase_Alpha` with actor `Actor_0`, precondition `Precondition_1`, primary scenario `Scenario_0` with steps $\langle \text{Step}_0, \text{Step}_1 \rangle$, and postcondition `Postcondition_1`.
- **When:** Projecting use case specifications.
- **Then:** Projects `UseCase_Alpha` with verified step order and actor associations.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-02: Alternate Extension Branching and Resumption Boundary
- **Given:** Use case `UseCase_Alpha` declaring alternate extension `Extension_0` branching from `Step_0` under guard $g_{\text{alt}}$ and resuming at `Step_1`.
- **When:** Validating scenario branching topology and step anchors.
- **Then:** Confirms anchor `Step_0` and resumption step `Step_1` reside in `Scenario_0`.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.

### AC-03: Dangling Extension Anchor and Flow Rejection
- **Given:** `UseCase_Alpha` where `Extension_0` specifies unresolvable anchor `Step_Missing` or null precondition $P_{\text{pre}} = \bot$.
- **When:** Validating step connectivity and branch invariants.
- **Then:** Rejects flow, binds sentinel $\bot_{\text{uc}}$, and emits diagnostic `E0311`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0311` (AlternateExtensionViolation).

### AC-04: Unresolved Actor Reference and Delimiter Recovery
- **Given:** Use case referencing undeclared actor `Actor_Missing`, followed by delimiter `;` and valid `UseCase_Beta`.
- **When:** Lowering use case actors with delimiter recovery enabled.
- **Then:** Emits `E0300`, binds $\bot_{\text{uc}}$, recovers across `;`, projects `UseCase_Beta`, and halts at $C_{\text{max\_errors}}$ with `E0199`.
- **Diagnostic:** `E0300`, `E0199`.

### AC-05: Deterministic UUIDv5 Anchors and Performance Bounds
- **Given:** Model with $|UC| \cdot \text{steps} = 10^4$ steps evaluated on $N_{\text{max\_workers}}$ threads.
- **When:** Benchmarking use case projection in Release mode (`--release`).
- **Then:** Emits bitwise identical UUIDv5 anchors; latency $< 25\text{ ms}$, RSS $< 100\text{ MB}$.
- **Diagnostic:** Diagnostic sink remains empty.
