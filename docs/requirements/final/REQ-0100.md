### REQ-0100: Exact Rational Exponent Arithmetic & Kirchhoff Continuity Conservation

#### 1. Normative Statement

The physical metrology and abstract flow conservation engine of Subsystem 5 shall compute all dimensional algebra operations using exact rational arithmetic over the field of rational numbers $`\mathbb{Q}`$` without floating-point representations, and synthesize generalized Kirchhoff conservation constraints enforcing flow continuity (`$`\sum f = 0`$`) at junctions and potential loop conservation (`$`\sum \Delta e = 0`$) across closed topological cycles in abstract conservative networks.

  Specifically, the metrology engine, dimension analyzer, and network constraint synthesizer shall strictly enforce the following structural, algebraic, and topological invariants:
  1. **Exact Rational Representation & Exponent Field Operations**:
     - Every physical quantity dimension shall be modeled as a rational coordinate vector $`\mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$` over the seven base physical dimensions `$`[M]`$` (Mass), `$`[L]`$` (Length), `$`[T]`$` (Time), `$`[I]`$` (Base Flow Quantity / Electric Current), `$`[\Theta]`$` (Thermodynamic Temperature), `$`[N]`$` (Amount of Substance), and `$`[J]`$ (Luminous Intensity).
     - Each rational exponent coordinate $`q \in \mathbb{Q}`$` shall be represented as an irreducible fraction `$`\frac{a}{b}`$` where `$`a \in \mathbb{Z}`$`, `$`b \in \mathbb{Z}^+`$`, `$`\gcd(|a|, b) = 1`$`, and `$`b \gt 0`$`. The zero exponent is uniquely represented by the canonical form `$`\frac{0}{1}`$.
     - Dimensional multiplication of quantities shall compute exact component-wise vector addition in $`\mathbb{Q}^7`$`: `$`\mathbf{d}(Q_1 \cdot Q_2) = \mathbf{d}(Q_1) + \mathbf{d}(Q_2)`$.
     - Dimensional division of quantities shall compute exact component-wise vector subtraction in $`\mathbb{Q}^7`$`: `$`\mathbf{d}(Q_1 / Q_2) = \mathbf{d}(Q_1) - \mathbf{d}(Q_2)`$.
     - Dimensional exponentiation of a quantity to a rational power $`k = \frac{p}{q} \in \mathbb{Q}`$` shall compute exact scalar-vector multiplication in `$`\mathbb{Q}^7`$`: `$`\mathbf{d}(Q^k) = k \cdot \mathbf{d}(Q)`$.
     - The use of IEEE 754 floating-point representations (`f32`, `f64`) in dimensional vector coordinates, rational exponent calculations, and scale factors is strictly prohibited to guarantee cross-platform numerical determinism.
     - Attempted division by zero ($`b = 0`$) during rational arithmetic reduction or fractional power inversion shall be rejected deterministically at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`RationalArithmeticDomainError`) in accordance with REQ-0191.
  2. **Flow Network Topology & Incidence Matrix Construction**:
     - The network constraint engine shall ingest abstract flow network definitions formalized as directed multigraphs $`\mathcal{G}_{\text{flow}} = (\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}, \mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}})`$`, where `$`\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}`$` is the set of junction nodes (`$`|\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}| = N_V`$`) and `$`\mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}`$` is the set of directed flow branches (`$`|\mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}| = N_E`$).
     - Each flow branch $`e = (u, v) \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}`$` shall couple a source junction node `$`u \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}`$` to a target junction node `$`v \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}`$`, carrying a through variable `$`f_e`$` (flow rate / flux) and across variable `$`\Delta e_e = e_u - e_v`$ (effort / potential difference).
     - The network engine shall construct the topological node-branch incidence matrix $`\mathbf{A} \in \{-1, 0, +1\}^{N_V \times N_E}`$ according to standard orientation:

```math
A_{i, e} = \begin{cases} +1, & \text{if branch } e \text{ enters junction } J_i \\ -1, & \text{if branch } e \text{ leaves junction } J_i \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}
```

  3. **Generalized Kirchhoff Flow Law (Flow Continuity)**:
     - At every conservative junction node $`J_i \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}`$, the network engine shall synthesize a flow continuity constraint requiring the algebraic sum of all incident through variables to equal zero identically:

```math
\sum_{e \in \text{Incident}(J_i)} \sigma_{i, e} \cdot f_e = 0 \iff \mathbf{A} \mathbf{f} = \mathbf{0}
```

       where $`\sigma_{i, e} = A_{i, e}`$` is the incidence orientation and `$`\mathbf{f} = [f_1, \dots, f_{N_E}]^T`$.
     - Junction nodes lacking dynamic accumulation terms ($`\frac{d S}{dt} = 0`$) or external non-conservative sources/sinks shall strictly preserve zero net storage.
  4. **Generalized Kirchhoff Potential Law (Cycle Conservation)**:
     - The network engine shall compute a fundamental cycle basis $`\mathcal{C} = \{ \mathcal{L}_1, \dots, \mathcal{L}_{N_C} \}`$` (where `$`N_C = N_E - N_V + 1`$` for a connected graph component) and construct the fundamental cycle matrix `$`\mathbf{B} \in \{-1, 0, +1\}^{N_C \times N_E}`$.
     - Across every fundamental topological cycle $`\mathcal{L}_k \in \mathcal{C}`$, the network engine shall synthesize a loop potential conservation constraint requiring the algebraic sum of all across variable differences along the cycle to equal zero identically:

```math
\sum_{e \in \text{Edges}(\mathcal{L}_k)} \mu_{k, e} \cdot \Delta e_e = 0 \iff \mathbf{B} \mathbf{\Delta e} = \mathbf{0}
```

       where $`\mu_{k, e} = B_{k, e}`$` is the cycle branch traversal orientation and `$`\mathbf{\Delta e} = [\Delta e_1, \dots, \Delta e_{N_E}]^T`$.
     - Topological Orthogonality: The engine shall verify Tellegen's orthogonality condition $`\mathbf{A} \mathbf{B}^T = \mathbf{0}`$` and total abstract power conservation `$`\mathbf{f}^T \mathbf{\Delta e} = 0`$.
  5. **Dimensional Homogeneity across Junctions & Loops**:
     - All through variables $`f_e`$` incident upon a conservative junction node `$`J_i`$` must share an identical physical dimension vector in `$`\mathbb{Q}^7`$`: `$`\forall e_1, e_2 \in \text{Incident}(J_i), \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(e_1) \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(e_2)`$.
     - All across variables $`\Delta e_e`$` traversing branches of a fundamental cycle `$`\mathcal{L}_k`$` must share an identical physical dimension vector in `$`\mathbb{Q}^7`$`: `$`\forall e_1, e_2 \in \text{Edges}(\mathcal{L}_k), \mathbf{d}_{\text{across}}(e_1) \equiv \mathbf{d}_{\text{across}}(e_2)`$.
     - Any dimensional mismatch detected across incident through variables or loop across variables shall be deterministically rejected at compile time with diagnostic error code `E0300` (`DimensionalMismatch`).
  6. **Static Balance Verification & Conservation Failure Diagnostics**:
     - For networks with statically declared flow rates or potential differences, the engine shall evaluate continuity and loop equations at compile time.
     - Any conservative junction violating flow balance ($`\sum \sigma_{i, e} f_e \ne 0`$`) or any closed loop violating potential closure (`$`\sum \mu_{k, e} \Delta e_e \ne 0`$) shall be rejected deterministically with diagnostic error code `E0301` (`KirchhoffConservationViolation`).
  7. **Architectural Directives Compliance**:
     - *Directive 1 (Deterministic UUIDv5 Anchors)*: Every generated junction node, flow branch handle, and conservation constraint AST node shall be assigned an immutable deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 identifier derived from the compiler namespace OID and canonical fully qualified path ($`\text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(n))`$) in accordance with REQ-0010.
     - *Directive 2 (Bounded Work-Stealing Parallelism)*: Parallel extraction of cycle bases, incidence matrix assembly, and dimensional validation across independent network subgraphs shall execute on a bounded work-stealing thread pool with worker bound $`N_{\text{max\_workers}}`$ in accordance with REQ-0012.
     - *Directive 3 (String Interner Allocation Exception)*: Junction identifiers, branch names, and variable labels shall be interned into the deduplicated global string interner pool during lexical analysis per REQ-0036 as the sole permitted exception to zero-copy memory management.
     - *Directive 4 (Synchronization Token Error Recovery)*: The parser and network validator shall implement synchronization token error recovery on statement delimiters (`;`) and block boundaries (`}`). When semantic violations occur (`E0300`, `E0301`), diagnostics shall accumulate in error sink buffer $`\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}`$`, substitute typed error sentinels (`$`\bot_{\text{dim}}`$`, `$`\bot_{\text{flow}}`$`), and continue parsing. If accumulated diagnostics reach `$`C_{\text{max\_errors}}`$, terminal diagnostic `E0199` shall be emitted and compilation halted gracefully.
     - *Directive 5 (Panic-Free Release Isolation)*: The rational arithmetic engine and network conservation pipeline shall strictly enforce a deterministic, non-panicking execution contract with total-function error handling. Performance latency ($`\lt 25\text{ ms}`$`) and peak RSS memory (`$`\lt 100\text{ MB}`$` for `$`10^4`$ branches) shall be evaluated exclusively in compiled Release mode (`--release`) under dedicated benchmark harnesses isolated from CI logic tests per REQ-0011 and REQ-0196.

  All diagnostic messages, test models, and illustrative examples shall strictly employ synthetic abstract MBSE placeholders (`Package_0`, `Package_1`, `Classifier_Alpha`, `Junction_Alpha`, `Junction_Beta`, `FlowBranch_1`, `FlowBranch_2`, `Port_1`, `Port_2`, `param_x : Real`, `Quantity_1`, `Network_Alpha`), strictly prohibiting hardcoded domain concepts, mock data, and non-ASCII dashes.

#### 2. Formal Invariant

Let $`\mathcal{V}_{\text{AST}}`$ denote the universe of allocated AST nodes in contiguous bump arena storage.

  Let $`\mathbb{Q}`$ denote the set of irreducible rational numbers:

```math
\mathbb{Q} = \left\{ \frac{a}{b} \;\middle|\; a \in \mathbb{Z}, \, b \in \mathbb{Z}^+, \, \gcd(|a|, b) = 1 \right\}
```

  For any pair of rational numbers $`q_1 = \frac{a_1}{b_1}, q_2 = \frac{a_2}{b_2} \in \mathbb{Q}`$, exact rational arithmetic operations are defined by:

```math
\begin{aligned}
q_1 + q_2 &= \operatorname{reduce}(a_1 \cdot b_2 + a_2 \cdot b_1, \, b_1 \cdot b_2) \\
q_1 - q_2 &= \operatorname{reduce}(a_1 \cdot b_2 - a_2 \cdot b_1, \, b_1 \cdot b_2) \\
q_1 \cdot q_2 &= \operatorname{reduce}(a_1 \cdot a_2, \, b_1 \cdot b_2) \\
q_1 / q_2 &= \begin{cases} \operatorname{reduce}(a_1 \cdot b_2 \cdot \operatorname{sgn}(a_2), \, b_1 \cdot |a_2|), & \text{if } a_2 \ne 0 \\ \bot_{\text{rat}}, & \text{if } a_2 = 0 \end{cases}
\end{aligned}
```

  where canonical reduction is formalized as:

```math
\operatorname{reduce}(a, b) = \left\langle \frac{a \cdot \operatorname{sgn}(b)}{\gcd(|a|, |b|)}, \, \frac{|b|}{\gcd(|a|, |b|)} \right\rangle
```

  Let $`\mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$ represent the 7-dimensional physical quantity dimension:

```math
\mathbf{d} = (q_M, q_L, q_T, q_I, q_\Theta, q_N, q_J) \in \mathbb{Q}^7
```

  For quantity expressions $`Q_1, Q_2`$` with dimensions `$`\mathbf{d}_1, \mathbf{d}_2 \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$` and rational scalar exponent `$`k = \frac{p}{q} \in \mathbb{Q}`$:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{d}(Q_1 \cdot Q_2) &= \mathbf{d}_1 + \mathbf{d}_2 = (q_{M, 1} + q_{M, 2}, \dots, q_{J, 1} + q_{J, 2}) \\
\mathbf{d}(Q_1 / Q_2) &= \mathbf{d}_1 - \mathbf{d}_2 = (q_{M, 1} - q_{M, 2}, \dots, q_{J, 1} - q_{J, 2}) \\
\mathbf{d}(Q_1^k) &= k \cdot \mathbf{d}_1 = (k \cdot q_{M, 1}, \dots, k \cdot q_{J, 1})
\end{aligned}
```

  Let $`\mathcal{G}_{\text{flow}} = (\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}, \mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}})`$` be an abstract flow conservation network multigraph. Each junction node `$`J \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}`$ is formalized as a 5-tuple:

```math
J = \langle \text{id}_J, \text{name}_J, \text{Incident}(J), \Delta_J, \text{span}_J \rangle
```

  where:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\text{id}_J &\in \mathbb{N}_{32} \quad (\text{contiguous arena node handle}) \\
\text{name}_J &\in \mathcal{S}_{\text{intern}} \quad (\text{interned junction name identifier}) \\
\text{Incident}(J) &\subseteq \mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}} \quad (\text{set of incident directed flow branches}) \\
\Delta_J &\in \{0, 1\}^{128} \quad (\text{deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 topological anchor}) \\
\text{span}_J &= \langle \text{file\_id}, p_{\text{start}}, p_{\text{end}}, \text{line}, \text{col} \rangle \quad (\text{source lexical provenance})
\end{aligned}
```

  Each flow branch $`e \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}`$ is formalized as a 9-tuple:

```math
e = \langle \text{id}_e, u_e, v_e, f_e, \Delta e_e, \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}, \mathbf{d}_{\text{across}}, \Delta_e, \text{span}_e \rangle
```

  where $`u_e, v_e \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}`$`, `$`f_e`$` is the through variable, `$`\Delta e_e`$` is the across variable, and `$`\mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}, \mathbf{d}_{\text{across}} \in \mathbb{Q}^7`$.

  Let $`N_V = |\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}|`$` and `$`N_E = |\mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}|`$`. The incidence matrix `$`\mathbf{A} \in \{-1, 0, +1\}^{N_V \times N_E}`$ is defined by:

```math
A_{i, e} = \begin{cases} +1, & \text{if branch } e \text{ enters junction } J_i \\ -1, & \text{if branch } e \text{ leaves junction } J_i \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}
```

  Let $`\mathcal{C} = \{ \mathcal{L}_1, \dots, \mathcal{L}_{N_C} \}`$` denote the fundamental cycle basis of `$`\mathcal{G}_{\text{flow}}`$` where `$`N_C = N_E - N_V + |\mathcal{K}|`$` for `$`|\mathcal{K}|`$` connected components. The cycle matrix `$`\mathbf{B} \in \{-1, 0, +1\}^{N_C \times N_E}`$ is defined by:

```math
B_{k, e} = \begin{cases} +1, & \text{if branch } e \text{ traverses cycle } \mathcal{L}_k \text{ in the forward reference direction} \\ -1, & \text{if branch } e \text{ traverses cycle } \mathcal{L}_k \text{ in the reverse reference direction} \\ 0, & \text{if branch } e \notin \text{Edges}(\mathcal{L}_k) \end{cases}
```

  Generalized Kirchhoff Flow Law (KFL / Flow Continuity Invariant):

```math
\forall J_i \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}, \quad \sum_{e \in \text{Incident}(J_i)} A_{i, e} \cdot f_e = 0 \iff \mathbf{A} \mathbf{f} = \mathbf{0}
```

  Generalized Kirchhoff Potential Law (KVL / Potential Loop Conservation Invariant):

```math
\forall \mathcal{L}_k \in \mathcal{C}, \quad \sum_{e \in \text{Edges}(\mathcal{L}_k)} B_{k, e} \cdot \Delta e_e = 0 \iff \mathbf{B} \mathbf{\Delta e} = \mathbf{0}
```

  Tellegen Orthogonality & Power Conservation Invariant:

```math
\mathbf{A} \mathbf{B}^T = \mathbf{0}, \quad \mathbf{f}^T \mathbf{\Delta e} = \sum_{e \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}} f_e \cdot \Delta e_e = 0
```

  Dimensional Homogeneity Invariants across Conservative Networks:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\forall J_i \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}, \; \forall e_1, e_2 \in \text{Incident}(J_i) &\implies \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(e_1) = \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(e_2) \\
\forall \mathcal{L}_k \in \mathcal{C}, \; \forall e_1, e_2 \in \text{Edges}(\mathcal{L}_k) &\implies \mathbf{d}_{\text{across}}(e_1) = \mathbf{d}_{\text{across}}(e_2)
\end{aligned}
```

  Deterministic RFC 4122 UUIDv5 Anchor Invariant (Architectural Directive 1):

```math
\begin{aligned}
\forall J \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}, &\quad \Delta_J = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(J)) \\
\forall e \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}, &\quad \Delta_e = \text{UUIDv5}(\text{OID}_{\text{DEAP}}, \text{FQN}(e))
\end{aligned}
```

  Diagnostic Trigger Rules: In accordance with REQ-0191, algebraic and conservation violations deterministically emit structured diagnostic records:

```math
\begin{aligned}
\exists \frac{a}{b} \text{ with } b = 0 &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(\text{node}, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0300}, \text{RationalArithmeticDomainError}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
\exists J_i, (e_1, e_2) \in \text{Incident}(J_i) : \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(e_1) \ne \mathbf{d}_{\text{through}}(e_2) &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(J_i, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0300}, \text{DimensionalMismatch}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
\exists \mathcal{L}_k, (e_1, e_2) \in \text{Edges}(\mathcal{L}_k) : \mathbf{d}_{\text{across}}(e_1) \ne \mathbf{d}_{\text{across}}(e_2) &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(\mathcal{L}_k, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0300}, \text{DimensionalMismatch}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
\exists J_i \in \mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}} : \sum_{e \in \text{Incident}(J_i)} A_{i, e} \cdot f_e \ne 0 &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(J_i, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0301}, \text{KirchhoffConservationViolation}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
\exists \mathcal{L}_k \in \mathcal{C} : \sum_{e \in \text{Edges}(\mathcal{L}_k)} B_{k, e} \cdot \Delta e_e \ne 0 &\implies \text{Diagnostic}(\mathcal{L}_k, \text{DiagnosticCode::E0301}, \text{KirchhoffConservationViolation}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} \\
|\mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}}| \ge C_{\text{max\_errors}} &\implies \exists D_{\text{term}} \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{sink}} : \text{code}(D_{\text{term}}) = \text{DiagnosticCode::E0199}
\end{aligned}
```

#### 3. Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds

Exact rational exponent arithmetic, incidence matrix assembly, fundamental cycle basis extraction, and generalized Kirchhoff conservation constraint synthesis execute strictly within Complexity Class $`\mathbf{P}`$.

  For an MBSE compilation unit containing $`K`$` dimensional expressions, `$`N_V = |\mathcal{V}_{\text{junc}}|`$` junction nodes, and `$`N_E = |\mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}|`$` flow branches in an abstract network graph `$`\mathcal{G}_{\text{flow}}`$:
  1. *Rational Exponent Arithmetic Operations*:
     - Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and canonical reduction of two rational numbers $`\frac{a_1}{b_1}`$` and `$`\frac{a_2}{b_2}`$` compute integer arithmetic products and greatest common divisor (`$`\gcd`$) reduction via the Euclidean algorithm.
     - For bounded machine integer representations (64-bit or 128-bit words), the Euclidean algorithm terminates in at most $`2 \log_2(\min(|a|, |b|))`$` division steps, executing in deterministic `$`O(1)`$ time:

```math
T_{\text{rat\_op}} \in O(1)
```

     - Evaluating $`K`$` dimensional arithmetic expressions over the 7-dimensional rational space `$`\mathbb{Q}^7`$ executes in deterministic linear time:

```math
T_{\text{dim\_eval}}(K) \in O(K)
```

  2. *Topological Incidence Matrix Assembly*:
     - Allocating and populating the sparse node-branch incidence matrix $`\mathbf{A} \in \{-1, 0, +1\}^{N_V \times N_E}`$` inspects each flow branch `$`e \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{flow}}`$` exactly once, writing source (`$`-1`$`) and target (`$`+1`$) entries.
     - This construction executes in deterministic linear time and space:

```math
T_{\text{incidence}}(N_V, N_E) \in O(N_V + N_E)
```

  3. *Fundamental Cycle Basis Extraction*:
     - Constructing a spanning tree $`\mathcal{T}_{\text{span}}`$` over connected components via depth-first search (DFS) or breadth-first search (BFS) executes in `$`O(N_V + N_E)`$ time.
     - Each of the $`N_C = N_E - N_V + 1`$` chord edges `$`e \notin \mathcal{T}_{\text{span}}`$` induces exactly one fundamental cycle `$`\mathcal{L}_k`$` of length bounded by `$`N_V`$`. Cycle path reconstruction per chord requires `$`O(N_V)`$ time.
     - Extracting the complete cycle basis $`\mathcal{C}`$` and constructing sparse cycle matrix `$`\mathbf{B}`$ executes in deterministic polynomial time:

```math
T_{\text{cycle}}(N_V, N_E) \in O(N_V + N_E + N_C \cdot N_V) \subseteq O(N_E \cdot N_V)
```

  4. *Kirchhoff Conservation Constraint Synthesis*:
     - Generating $`N_V`$` flow continuity constraints (`$`\mathbf{A} \mathbf{f} = \mathbf{0}`$`) and `$`N_C`$` potential loop constraints (`$`\mathbf{B} \mathbf{\Delta e} = \mathbf{0}`$`) allocates AST constraint nodes in contiguous arena storage, requiring `$`O(N_V + N_C \cdot N_V)`$ time and space.
  5. *Static Balance & Homogeneity Evaluation*:
     - Evaluating dimensional homogeneity across all incident branches requires verifying equality in $`\mathbb{Q}^7`$` for each branch, executing in `$`O(N_E)`$ comparisons.
     - Static matrix-vector products $`\mathbf{A} \mathbf{f}`$` and `$`\mathbf{B} \mathbf{\Delta e}`$` over sparse matrices execute in `$`O(N_E)`$ additions and scalar multiplications.
  6. *Aggregate Algorithmic Complexity*:

```math
T_{\text{total}}(K, N_V, N_E) \in O(K + N_V + N_E \cdot N_V) \subseteq O(K + N_E^2)
```

     For sparse physical topologies (where node degrees are bounded by a small constant $`d_{\text{max}} \ll N_V`$), total execution time simplifies to strictly linear time:

```math
T_{\text{sparse}}(K, N_V, N_E) \in O(K + N_V + N_E)
```

  7. *Parallel Work-Stealing Scaling (Architectural Directive 2)*:
     When evaluating $`M`$` independent network components or packages across `$`N_{\text{max\_workers}}`$ threads on a bounded work-stealing thread pool:

```math
T_{\text{parallel}}(K, N_V, N_E, N_{\text{max\_workers}}) \in O\left( \frac{K + N_V + N_E \cdot N_V}{N_{\text{max\_workers}}} + \log N_{\text{max\_workers}} \right)
```

  8. *Space Complexity & Memory Bounds*:
     Contiguous arena allocations for junction nodes, flow branches, incidence matrices, and synthesized constraint expressions are strictly bounded by $`O(N_V + N_E + N_C \cdot N_V + K)`$` bytes. Deduplicated string interner memory is bounded by the lengths of unique junction and variable names. Diagnostic buffer memory is strictly bounded by `$`O(C_{\text{max\_errors}}) = O(1)`$.
