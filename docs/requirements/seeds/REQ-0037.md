### REQ-0037: Classifier Definition & Feature Usage Metamodel Typing Relations
- **Normative Statement**: The core metamodel shall establish a strict formal duality between classifier definitions (`ClassifierDef`) representing reusable types and feature usages (`FeatureUsage`) representing contextual usages of classifiers. Every feature usage must bind to an authoritative classifier definition via a typed reference relation.
- **Formal Invariant**: Every structural element in the metamodel is strictly partitioned into definitions and usages:
$$
\text{Elements} = \text{ClassifierDefs} \cup \text{FeatureUsages}, \quad \text{ClassifierDefs} \cap \text{FeatureUsages} = \emptyset
$$
Typing Relation Invariant:
$$
\tau : \text{FeatureUsages} \longrightarrow \text{ClassifierDefs}
$$
Subtyping & Conformance Invariant:
$$
u \in \text{FeatureUsages} \wedge \tau(u) = C_1 \wedge C_1 \le C_2 \implies u \text{ conforms to } C_2
$$
Contextual Overriding Invariant: Feature usages may redefine default values, multiplicity bounds, or nested feature types of their classifier definition only in accordance with monotonic covariance rules.
- **Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds**: Type resolution executes in $O(1)$ amortized time via interned symbol handle index lookups.
- **Verification & Conformance Criteria**: Type checker validates 100% of feature usages against declared classifier definitions and reports un-typed usages as errors.
