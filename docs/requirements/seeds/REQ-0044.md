### REQ-0044: Lossless AST Serialization & Deserialization (JSON & CBOR Formats)
- **Normative Statement**: The AST engine shall provide high-performance, lossless serialization and deserialization of the compiled model AST to and from minified JSON and binary CBOR formats while preserving all node IDs, spans, attributes, constraints, and provenance metadata.
- **Formal Invariant**: For a compiled model AST $\mathcal{A}$, the serialization $\mathcal{S}$ and deserialization $\mathcal{D}$ round-trip satisfies strict structural isomorphism:
$$
\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{S}(\mathcal{A})) \cong \mathcal{A}
$$
Element-Wise Preservation Invariant:
$$
\forall n \in \text{Nodes}(\mathcal{A}), \quad \text{Deref}(\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{S}(n))) \equiv n
$$
Preserved properties include: element UUIDs, interned symbol handles, source byte spans $\langle \text{start}, \text{end} \rangle$, multiplicity intervals, and provenance anchors.
Binary Efficiency Invariant: The serialized CBOR binary representation satisfies:
$$
|\text{CBOR}(\mathcal{A})| \le 0.40 \cdot |\text{JSON}(\mathcal{A})|
$$
- **Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds**: Serialization and deserialization execute in $O(|\text{Nodes}(\mathcal{A})|)$ linear time, deserializing a 100,000-node model from CBOR in under 15 milliseconds.
- **Verification & Conformance Criteria**: Automated round-trip test asserts bit-for-bit identity across serialize-deserialize-serialize cycles.
