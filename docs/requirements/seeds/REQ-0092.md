### REQ-0092: Canonical Scalar Type Lowering & Precision Preservation
- **Normative Statement**: The lowering engine shall map primitive KerML `ScalarValues` types (`Boolean`, `Integer`, `Real`, `String`) into canonical target machine representations, preserving semantic value precision, exact integer boundaries, and floating-point IEEE-754 semantics.
- **Formal Invariant**: The primitive subtyping lattice enforces:
$$
\text{Positive} \le \text{Natural} \le \text{Integer} \le \text{Real}
$$
Target type mappings and value domains:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\llbracket \text{ScalarValues::Boolean} \rrbracket &\longleftrightarrow \mathbb{B} = \{ \text{false}, \text{true} \} \\
\llbracket \text{ScalarValues::Integer} \rrbracket &\longleftrightarrow \mathbb{Z}_{64} = [-2^{63}, 2^{63}-1] \\
\llbracket \text{ScalarValues::Real} \rrbracket &\longleftrightarrow \mathbb{R}_{64} \quad (\text{IEEE 754 binary64}) \\
\llbracket \text{ScalarValues::String} \rrbracket &\longleftrightarrow \text{SymbolId} \quad (\text{Interned UTF-8})
\end{aligned}
$$
Complexity bound: Scalar literals parse in $O(1)$ time with zero precision loss; scalar conversions obey monotonic subtyping projections.
