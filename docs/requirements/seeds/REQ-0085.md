### REQ-0085: SysML v2 RequirementDefinition (`requirement def`) & RequirementUsage Lowering
- **Normative Statement**: The requirements compiler shall formalize `requirement def` and `requirement` usages as structured contractual specification classifiers possessing deterministic alphanumeric identifiers, normative text contracts, subject elements, and formal assume-guarantee precondition/postcondition expressions.
- **Formal Invariant**: A requirement $R$ is formalized as a contractual tuple:
$$
R = \langle \text{id}: \text{SymbolId}, \text{text}: \text{String}, \text{subject}: \text{ClassifierNode}, \mathcal{A}_{\text{assume}}: \text{Expr} \to \mathbb{B}, \mathcal{G}_{\text{require}}: \text{Expr} \to \mathbb{B} \rangle
$$
Assume-Guarantee Contract Semantics: Under environment assumption $\mathcal{A}_{\text{assume}}$, the subject realization $S$ must guarantee condition $\mathcal{G}_{\text{require}}$:
$$
\mathcal{A}_{\text{assume}}(\sigma) \implies \mathcal{G}_{\text{require}}(\sigma)
$$
Requirement identifier uniqueness: Within a requirement model repository, all requirement IDs must be globally unique: $\forall R_1 \neq R_2, \text{id}(R_1) \neq \text{id}(R_2)$.
Complexity bound: Requirement contract parsing and ID deduplication execute in $O(1)$ amortized time per requirement entity.
