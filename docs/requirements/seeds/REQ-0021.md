### REQ-0021: Tabular Schema Ingestion for Structural Parts & Component Assemblies
- **Normative Statement**: The ingestion engine shall parse tabular schemas specifying structural parts and component assemblies into `IngestedPart` intermediate entities, extracting part names, types, multiplicity intervals `[min..max]`, structural containment hierarchies, and owned sub-components.
- **Formal Invariant**: Multiplicity intervals are parsed into bounded integer pairs:
$$
\text{ParseMultiplicity}(M) = \langle l, u \rangle \in \mathbb{N} \times (\mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}), \quad \text{where } 0 \le l \le u
$$
Default Multiplicity Invariant:
$$
M = \bot \implies \langle l, u \rangle = \langle 1, 1 \rangle
$$
Hierarchy containment forms an acyclic forest over part definitions:
$$
\text{ContainsPart}(P_{\text{parent}}, P_{\text{child}}) \implies P_{\text{child}} \notin \text{Ancestors}(P_{\text{parent}})
$$
Each `IngestedPart` entity records definition name, classifier type handle, multiplicity bounds $\langle l, u \rangle$, and owned property sets.
- **Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds**: Part table extraction executes in $O(R)$ time where $R$ is the number of table rows, maintaining constant-time insertion into the arena.
- **Verification & Conformance Criteria**: Verification suite confirms 100% of rows in component definition tables lower to valid `IngestedPart` AST nodes with validated multiplicity.
