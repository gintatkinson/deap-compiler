### REQ-0086: SysML v2 SatisfyRequirementUsage (`satisfy requirement`) Traceability
- **Normative Statement**: The traceability engine shall formalize `satisfy requirement` usages as directed realization dependencies linking structural components or behavioral actions to requirements, establishing verified bipartite satisfaction edges and compliance matrices.
- **Formal Invariant**: A satisfy relationship binds realization element $E$ to requirement $R$:
$$
E \xrightarrow{\text{satisfies}} R
$$
Traceability coverage: The compiler constructs the bipartite realization graph $G_{\text{trace}} = (V_E \cup V_R, E_{\text{sat}})$:
$$
\text{Coverage}(V_R) = \frac{|\{ r \in V_R \mid \exists e \in V_E, (e, r) \in E_{\text{sat}} \}|}{|V_R|}
$$
Orphan check: Every system requirement must link to at least one satisfying realization element ($\text{Coverage} = 1.0$), verified in deterministic $O(|V_R| + |E_{\text{sat}}|)$ time.
Complexity bound: Traceability matrix synthesis and orphan detection execute in deterministic linear time $O(|V_E| + |V_R| + |E_{\text{sat}}|)$.
