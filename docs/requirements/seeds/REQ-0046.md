### REQ-0046: Mutable AST Transformation & In-Place Rewrite Engine
- **Normative Statement**: The AST engine shall provide a mutable transformation pass engine for AST lowering, desugaring passes, optimization rewrites, and semantic enrichments while preserving topological integrity. Rewrites must update node attributes in-place within the arena and maintain generation counters to detect and invalidate stale external references.
- **Formal Invariant**: An AST rewrite pass is modeled as an arena state transformation:
$$
\text{RewritePass} : \mathcal{A}_t \longrightarrow \mathcal{A}_{t+1}
$$
where node slot indices remain invariant under in-place modification:
$$
\forall n \in \text{ModifiedNodes}, \quad \text{Index}(n, \mathcal{A}_{t+1}) = \text{Index}(n, \mathcal{A}_t)
$$
Generational Mutation Invariant: In-place mutation increments the slot generation counter to prevent ABA reference hazards:
$$
\text{Generation}(\text{Slot}_i, t+1) = \text{Generation}(\text{Slot}_i, t) + 1
$$
Topological Invariant Preservation: Rewrite passes must not introduce undefined references or dangling edges:
$$
\forall e = (u, v) \in \text{Edges}(\mathcal{A}_{t+1}), \quad u \in \text{Nodes}(\mathcal{A}_{t+1}) \wedge v \in \text{Nodes}(\mathcal{A}_{t+1})
$$
- **Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds**: In-place node mutation is $O(1)$ constant time; full rewrite passes scale in $O(|\text{Nodes}(\mathcal{A})|)$ linear time.
- **Verification & Conformance Criteria**: Verification suite verifies that desugaring passes transform syntax constructs in-place without triggering arena reallocations or leaking invalid handles.
