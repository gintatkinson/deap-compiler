### REQ-0039: Flow Payload Binding & Stream Rate Multiplicity Validation
- **Normative Statement**: The metamodel shall bind flow payloads to specific data types and validate stream transmission rate multiplicities, requiring item flows traversing connectors to enforce payload type compatibility and transmission rate capacity boundaries.
- **Formal Invariant**: Let $F$ be an item flow carrying payload type $\tau_F$ between ports $P_1$ and $P_2$. The flow typing rule requires covariance with port payload types:
$$
\tau_F :> P_1.\text{payload\_type} \quad \wedge \quad \tau_F :> P_2.\text{payload\_type}
$$
Channel Capacity Invariant: Flow transmission rate multiplicity $R_{\text{flow}}$ (occurrences per unit time) and payload size $\text{sizeof}(\tau_F)$ must not exceed designated channel capacity $C_{\text{channel}}$:
$$
R_{\text{flow}} \cdot \text{sizeof}(\tau_F) \le C_{\text{channel}}
$$
Type Incompatibility Error: Mismatched flow types without declared serialization adapters trigger compile-time diagnostic error $\text{DiagnosticCode::E0202}$.
- **Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds**: Type compatibility checking executes in $O(H)$ where $H$ is the depth of the subtyping hierarchy, bounded by $O(1)$ amortized lattice operations.
- **Verification & Conformance Criteria**: Type checker tests confirm that overflowing channel capacities or incompatible payload types fail closed with diagnostic reports.
