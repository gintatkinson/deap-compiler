### REQ-0096: SysML v2 Quantities Library: `ISQ` & `ISQBase` Dimension Systems
- **Normative Statement**: The metrology subsystem shall bootstrap the normative SysML v2 `ISQ` and `ISQBase` standard library packages, defining the International System of Quantities 7 base dimensions and derived quantity spaces over an integer dimensional lattice $\mathbb{Z}^7$.
- **Formal Invariant**: Every quantity dimension $D \in \text{ISQ}$ corresponds to a unique integer vector $\mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{Z}^7$:
$$
\mathbf{d} = (\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \epsilon, \zeta, \eta) \iff [M]^\alpha [L]^\beta [T]^\gamma [I]^\delta [\Theta]^\epsilon [N]^\zeta [J]^\eta
$$
where basis dimensions represent: $[M]$ Mass, $[L]$ Length, $[T]$ Time, $[I]$ Electric Current, $[\Theta]$ Thermodynamic Temperature, $[N]$ Amount of Substance, $[J]$ Luminous Intensity.
Derived ISQ dimensions:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Force} &= [M]^1 [L]^1 [T]^{-2} \\
\text{Energy} &= [M]^1 [L]^2 [T]^{-2} \\
\text{Power} &= [M]^1 [L]^2 [T]^{-3}
\end{aligned}
$$
Complexity bound: Dimensional vector additions and scalar multiplications validate in $O(1)$ integer vector arithmetic operations.
