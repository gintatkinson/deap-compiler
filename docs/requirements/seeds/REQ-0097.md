### REQ-0097: SysML v2 Quantities Library: `SI` Base & Derived Unit Definitions
- **Normative Statement**: The metrology subsystem shall bootstrap the normative SysML v2 `SI` and `SIPrefixes` standard library packages, defining the 7 coherent SI base units, derived units, metric decimal scale factors ($10^k$), and exact rational conversion constants.
- **Formal Invariant**: Every SI unit $U$ is defined relative to coherent base units $\mathcal{B} = \{ \text{kg}, \text{m}, \text{s}, \text{A}, \text{K}, \text{mol}, \text{cd} \}$:
$$
1 \text{ Unit} = c \cdot \text{kg}^\alpha \cdot \text{m}^\beta \cdot \text{s}^\gamma \cdot \text{A}^\delta \cdot \text{K}^\epsilon \cdot \text{mol}^\zeta \cdot \text{cd}^\eta
$$
where $c \in \mathbb{Q}^+$ is the exact rational scaling factor and $(\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta, \epsilon, \zeta, \eta) \in \mathbb{Z}^7$.
Metric prefixes apply decimal powers:
$$
\text{Prefix}(k) \implies c_{\text{prefixed}} = 10^k \cdot c, \quad k \in \{ -24, -21, \dots, 21, 24 \}
$$
Complexity bound: Conversion between coherent units executes in exact rational arithmetic without floating-point error.
