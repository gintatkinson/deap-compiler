### REQ-0047: Graph-Theoretic AST Representation & Directed Multigraph Metamodel
- **Normative Statement**: The compiler core shall build a bidirectional directed property multigraph representing structural containment, feature typings, port connections, generalizations, allocations, and dependency relations across AST nodes using an abstract adjacency-list multigraph structure. The implementation must remain technology-neutral and decoupled from concrete external graph libraries.
- **Formal Invariant**: The AST model graph is defined as a directed multigraph $G = (V, E)$:
$$
V = \text{Nodes}(\mathcal{A}), \quad E \subseteq V \times V \times \text{EdgeKind}
$$
where edge categories span all structural and semantic relations:
$$
\text{EdgeKind} \in \{ \text{Contains}, \text{Specializes}, \text{ConnectsTo}, \text{FlowsTo}, \text{AllocatesTo}, \text{Constrains} \}
$$
Bidirectional Adjacency Invariant: For every edge $e = (u, v, k) \in E$:
$$
e \in \text{OutgoingEdges}(u) \quad \wedge \quad e \in \text{IncomingEdges}(v)
$$
Adjacency queries for incoming and outgoing edges execute via direct index lookups without full-graph scanning:
$$
T_{\text{neighbors}}(v) \in O(\text{degree}(v))
$$
- **Computational Complexity & Algorithmic Bounds**: Edge insertion executes in $O(1)$ amortized time; total graph construction scales linearly $O(|V| + |E|)$.
- **Verification & Conformance Criteria**: Verification suite asserts that bidirectional edge navigation is consistent across 100% of graph edges.
